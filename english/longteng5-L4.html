課文速覽（三大重點）

🔬 什麼是培植肉

許多新創公司發展出特殊製程：取動物幹細胞，轉化為原始纖維，再組合成肌肉組織。這種「實驗室培植肉」是在培養皿裡、而非在農場養成，並獲得 Bill Gates 與農業巨頭 Cargill 的投資。

🌱 支持者觀點

支持者強調傳統肉品生產難以兼顧動物福祉；在實驗室培育肉能免除屠宰場與對動物的不人道對待與折磨。它更永續、對環境更友善，據估計溫室氣體排放可降低達 96%，還能額外添加維生素與礦物質。

⚖️ 爭議與省思

然而這項新興食物來源也有隱憂：維持細胞培養設施可能耗費大量能源、是否健康安全仍有疑慮、缺乏天然結締組織與脂肪恐影響口感風味。在邁向主流之前，須同時理解其利與弊。

🔬 一｜引言與製程：什麼是實驗室培植肉
▶Can you imagine taking a bite of a steak that was grown in a Petri dish instead of on a farm?
你能想像咬下一塊在培養皿裡、而非在農場養成的牛排嗎？

Petri dish（培養皿）；take a bite of = 咬一口；instead of = 而非；that 引導關係子句修飾 steak（被動 was grown）。
▶It may sound like something from a sci-fi movie, but these steaks are already a reality!

sound like = 聽起來像；sci-fi = 科幻的（science fiction）；but 連接對比子句。
▶Many start-ups have developed a special production process in which meat is created by taking animal stem cells, transforming them into primitive fibers, and combining these fibers to form muscle tissue.
許多新創公司已發展出一種特殊的生產流程：取用動物幹細胞，將它們轉化為原始纖維，再把這些纖維組合起來形成肌肉組織，藉此製造出肉。
🔍 句構分析 ①Many start-upsS have developedV a special production processO [in which meat is created by taking..., transforming..., and combining...]介系詞＋關代子句
in which = 介系詞＋關代（見句型一）；stem cell 幹細胞、primitive fiber 原始纖維、muscle tissue 肌肉組織；by + V-ing（taking／transforming／combining）三個動名詞並列表方法。
▶The final product, known as lab-grown meat, is a thought-provoking recent trend in food science backed by Bill Gates and agricultural giant Cargill.
這項最終產品，也就是所謂的實驗室培植肉，是食品科學界一股發人深省的近期趨勢，並獲得比爾·蓋茲與農業巨頭嘉吉的支持。

▶As this technology moves toward mainstream adoption, it is important to understand both the promises and the problems with this emerging food source.
隨著這項技術逐漸邁向主流採用，理解這種新興食物來源的種種好處與問題就顯得十分重要。

As = 隨著；it is important to V 虛主詞句型；both A and B（promises and problems）；emerging = 新興的。
🌱 二｜支持者觀點：免除屠宰、更為永續
▶Supporters of lab-grown meat highlight several substantial benefits that traditional meat production cannot offer.
實驗室培植肉的支持者強調數項傳統肉品生產所無法提供的重大好處。
🔍 句構分析 ①Supporters of lab-grown meatS highlightV several substantial benefits [that traditional meat production cannot offer]O（含關係子句）
substantial（重大的）為核心字；highlight = 強調；that 引導關係子句修飾 benefits。
▶First, one could argue that traditional meat production facilities, such as farms or factories, hardly ever have animals' best interests at heart.
首先，可以說傳統的肉品生產設施——例如農場或工廠——幾乎從未真正把動物的最佳利益放在心上。
🔍 句構分析 ①oneS could argueV [that traditional meat production facilities... hardly ever have animals' best interests at heart]O（名詞子句）
have...at heart = 把…放在心上（片語）；hardly ever = 幾乎從不；such as = 例如。
▶Growing meat in a laboratory instead eliminates the need for slaughterhouses as well as the unethical treatment of animals.
相反地，在實驗室裡培育肉品則消除了對屠宰場的需求，也免除了對動物不人道的對待。
🔍 句構分析 ①Growing meat in a laboratoryS（動名詞） eliminatesV the need for slaughterhouses as well as the unethical treatment of animalsO
unethical（不道德的）為核心字；eliminate = 消除；as well as = 以及；slaughterhouse = 屠宰場。
▶In other words, you could rest assured that the lab-grown beef patty on your plate didn't come from a cow that had to endure physical or mental torment.
換句話說，你大可放心：你盤中那塊實驗室培植的牛肉餅，並非來自一頭必須忍受身體或精神折磨的牛。
🔍 句構分析 ①youS could rest assuredV [that the... patty... didn't come from a cow (that had to endure...)]O（名詞子句，含關係子句）
torment（折磨）為核心字；rest assured (that) = 儘管放心（片語）；in other words = 換言之；endure = 忍受。
▶Better still, lab-grown meat could make meat production more sustainable.
更棒的是，實驗室培植肉還可能讓肉品生產變得更為永續。
🔍 句構分析 ①lab-grown meatS could makeV meat productionO more sustainableOC
better still = 更好的是（片語）；make + O + OC（使…更…）；sustainable = 永續的。
▶The raising of livestock needed to fulfill the current global demand for meat actually takes a heavy toll on the environment.
為了滿足當前全球對肉品的需求而進行的牲畜養殖，其實對環境造成了沉重的代價。
🔍 句構分析 ①The raising of livestock [needed to fulfill...]S（含分詞修飾） takesV a heavy toll on the environmentO
livestock（家畜）、toll（代價）為核心字；take a heavy toll on = 對…造成沉重代價（片語）；needed to... 過去分詞修飾 livestock。
▶According to FAO, it accounts for nearly fifteen percent of all greenhouse gas emissions worldwide.
根據聯合國糧農組織（FAO），它占了全球所有溫室氣體排放量的近百分之十五。
🔍 句構分析 ①itS accounts forV nearly fifteen percent of all greenhouse gas emissionsO
according to = 根據；account for = 占（比例）；greenhouse gas emissions = 溫室氣體排放。
▶Experts estimate that by switching to lab-grown meat, emissions could be lowered by up to ninety-six percent.
專家估計，藉由改用實驗室培植肉，排放量最多可降低達百分之九十六。
🔍 句構分析 ①ExpertsS estimateV [that... emissions could be lowered by up to 96%]O（名詞子句，含被動）
estimate（估計）、switch（轉換）為核心字；by switching to = 藉由改用（介系詞＋動名詞）；被動 could be lowered；up to = 高達。
▶In addition, many claim it won't just be the environment that benefits.
此外，許多人主張，受益的將不只是環境而已。
🔍 句構分析 ①manyS claimV [(that) it won't just be the environment that benefits]O（含分裂句）
in addition = 此外；It is/won't be ... that ... 為強調句（分裂句），強調 the environment。
▶Scientists can actually make lab-grown meat healthier and more nutritious by adding vitamins and minerals that are not always found in natural meat.
科學家其實可以藉由添加天然肉品中不一定含有的維生素與礦物質，讓實驗室培植肉更健康、更營養。
🔍 句構分析 ①ScientistsS can makeV lab-grown meatO healthier and more nutritiousOC by adding vitamins and minerals [that...]副詞（方法）
vitamin（維生素）、mineral（礦物質）為核心字；make + O + OC；by + V-ing 表方法；that 引導關係子句。
⚖️ 三｜疑慮與問題：明日之肉的隱憂
▶However, despite the evident advantages of this forward-thinking method of food production, there are also some concerns surrounding the meat of tomorrow.
然而，儘管這種前瞻性的食品生產方式具有明顯的優點，圍繞著這種「明日之肉」仍存在一些疑慮。
🔍 句構分析 ①despite the evident advantages...介系詞片語（讓步） there areV some concerns [surrounding the meat of tomorrow]S（含分詞修飾）

for a start = 首先；far-reaching = 深遠的；in the long term = 就長期而言。
▶While advocates for lab-grown meat say it can help fight climate change, some research suggests that maintaining the infrastructure to grow cell cultures requires significant quantities of energy and leads to substantial gas emissions.
雖然實驗室培植肉的擁護者說它有助於對抗氣候變遷，某些研究卻指出，維持培養細胞的基礎設施需要大量能源，並導致可觀的氣體排放。
🔍 句構分析 ①While advocates... say...讓步子句 some researchS suggestsV [that maintaining the infrastructure... requires... and leads to...]O（名詞子句）

▶Another issue to consider is whether this type of artificial meat is actually healthy and safe for humans to consume.
另一個值得考量的問題是：這種人造肉是否真的健康、能安全地供人食用。
🔍 句構分析 ①Another issue to considerS isV [whether this type of artificial meat is... healthy and safe...]C（名詞子句）
whether = 是否（引導名詞子句作補語）；to consider 不定詞修飾 issue；for humans to consume = 供人食用。
▶Although great in theory, lab-grown meat is unnatural, similar to genetically modified food.
儘管在理論上很棒，實驗室培植肉卻是不自然的，類似於基因改造食品。

▶Since the latter is sometimes said to induce allergic reactions or even cause more serious health problems like cancer, some people are warning against human-made meat for fear that it might have similar side effects.

latter（後者）、induce（引發）、allergic（過敏的）、reaction（反應）為核心字；Since = 由於；be said to V = 據說；for fear that = 唯恐。
▶One final area of concern is the actual taste of lab-grown meat.

concern（擔憂）為核心字；area of concern = 令人擔憂的面向；actual = 實際的。
▶Without the naturally occurring connective tissue and fat that are present in real meat, lab-grown meat may be somewhat lacking in taste and texture and thus less appealing to some consumers.

▶Therefore, more extensive research is needed to determine how the flavor and composition of this new meat might be altered to better suit consumer tastes.
因此，需要更廣泛的研究來確定，該如何調整這種新肉品的風味與成分，以更貼合消費者的口味。

核心句型文法解析
👆 點擊每個句型標題，展開「公式 + 例句 + 常見錯誤 + 學測重點」。
句型一｜介系詞＋關係代名詞（in which）▼
先行詞 + 介系詞 + which / whom ... （＝關代作介系詞受詞時，介系詞移到關代前）

關代作介系詞受詞時，正式用法可將介系詞移到 which／whom 之前。

a special production process in which meat is created...
一種製造肉品的特殊生產流程…（課文）
✗ in that（介系詞後不可接 that）；✗ in where。
還原：meat is created in the process → the process in which meat is created。學測高頻。
句型二｜虛主詞 it（it is + adj. + (for sb.) to V）▼
It is + 形容詞 + (for + 受詞) + to V ...（真主詞為不定詞）

不定詞當主詞時常後移，句首用虛主詞 it 代替。

It is important to understand both the promises and the problems.
理解好處與問題兩面是重要的。（課文）
It is essential for us to conduct more research.
我們進行更多研究是必要的。（課文）
✗ It is important understanding...（虛主詞後接不定詞，非動名詞）。
動作者用 for + 受格；如描述人的性質（kind、nice）則用 of + 受格。
句型三｜被動語態（含情態助動詞被動 could/might be p.p.）▼
S + be動詞 + p.p. (+ by ...) ／ S + 助動詞 + be + p.p.

主詞是動作的承受者時用被動；含助動詞時為「助動詞 + be + p.p.」。

Emissions could be lowered by up to 96%.
排放量可被降低達 96%。（課文）
The flavor might be altered to better suit consumer tastes.
風味可能被調整以更貼合口味。（課文）
✗ could lowered / could been lowered（助動詞後須用原形 be + p.p.）。
課文被動極多：is created、was grown、is needed、be judged。學測常考。
句型四｜分詞片語後置修飾（known as / backed by / surrounding）▼
名詞 + 現在分詞(V-ing) / 過去分詞(p.p.) 片語（＝省略關代＋be）

主動/進行用 V-ing，被動用 p.p.，皆可置於名詞後代替關係子句。

the final product, known as lab-grown meat, ... backed by Bill Gates
被稱為培植肉、由蓋茲支持的最終產品（課文，過去分詞＝被動）
some concerns surrounding the meat of tomorrow
圍繞明日之肉的一些疑慮（課文，現在分詞＝主動）
✗ 主動被動用錯：a product knowing as...（應為 known）。
還原：the product which is known as...（省略 which is）。學測閱讀常見。
句型五｜對等連接：both A and B／whether ... or not▼
both A and B（A、B 對等）；Whether + S + V or not（無論是否）

both A and B 連接對等成分；whether... or not 引導讓步子句表「無論是否」。

understand both the promises and the problems
同時理解好處與問題（課文，both A and B）
Whether we welcome it or not, lab-grown meat is innovative.
無論我們是否歡迎，培植肉都很創新。（課文）
✗ both A but B；both A and B 中 A、B 詞性結構須平行。
both A and B 作主詞時動詞用複數。學測常考對等與平行結構。
