🟦 Dialogue｜Ela 節食到不舒服（In the classroom）

Ela 覺得不舒服、肚子好餓。Cody 問她 Have you had breakfast yet? 她說吃過了，但只吃一根香蕉，因為她正在節食（be on a diet）。自從在 Line 群組看到大家的合照、覺得自己很胖後，她每餐吃很少，有時乾脆不吃正餐（skip meals），想要越瘦越好（as slim as possible）。Cody 說 Appearance is not everything，並給她訣竅（tips）：均衡飲食（a balanced diet）、養成運動習慣（build a habit of exercising）——他姊姊每週至少（at least）運動三次，已經瘦了五公斤。Ela：I’m all ears!… I need to take action now.

🟩 Reading｜A Letter from a Teenager（Ted 寫給 Debbie）

高中生 Ted 寫信向專欄作家 Debbie 求助（ask for help）。他擅長打鼓（good at playing the drums），音樂帶給他很多樂趣（joy），夢想當流行歌手（pop star），剛和朋友組了樂團（band）。最近（lately）花很多時間練團，考試考很差，爸媽很生氣（angry），上週為了他對音樂的興趣（interest）大吵一架（a serious fight），爸媽要他放棄（give up）樂團，還說他們的音樂是垃圾（garbage）。他問：How can I talk them into letting me follow my dream? 信末附上信封（envelope）與郵票（stamp）的格式。

🟧 Grammar｜現在完成式（經驗／持續／完成）＋時態判斷

have / has + p.p.（課本動詞三態表）；Have you ever…? / never（經驗）；How long…? for / since（持續）；already / yet（完成）；gone to vs been to（Taco & Tom）；Review 1：過去式 vs 現在完成式（20 校考）與附加問句。

💬 Dialogue（上）｜In the classroom
▶ElaI don’t feel well. I’m so hungry.
我覺得不舒服。我好餓。
🔍 句構分析 ①IS don’t feel連綴動詞否定 well形容詞（身體好的）
②IS ’mV so hungryC（主詞補語）
feel well 的 well 是形容詞「健康的」（✗ feel good 指心情）。I don’t feel well.＝我身體不舒服。
▶CodyAre you all right? Have you had breakfast yet?
你還好嗎？你吃過早餐了沒？
🔍 句構分析 ①AreV youS all rightC（主詞補語）
②Have助動詞 youS hadp.p. breakfastO yet疑問句句尾
本課課名！Have you + p.p. + yet? 問「已經…了嗎」。have → had → had（吃）。yet 放疑問句、否定句句尾（already／yet 9 校考）。
▶ElaYes, I have already had breakfast, but I only ate a banana. I’m on a diet.
有，我已經吃過早餐了，可是我只吃了一根香蕉。我在節食。
🔍 句構分析 ①Yes修飾語 IS have already hadalready 在 have 與 p.p. 之間 breakfastO
②but修飾語 IS only修飾語 ate過去式 a bananaO
③IS ’mV on a diet節食中
already 用在肯定句，放 have 和 p.p. 中間。前半句講「到現在已經吃過」→ 完成式；後半句講「吃了什麼」這件過去的事 → 過去式 ate。be on a diet 節食中（diet 14 校）。
▶CodyHere. You need to eat something to raise your blood sugar. How long have you been on a diet?
給你。你需要吃點東西來提高血糖。你節食多久了？
🔍 句構分析 ①Here給你（遞東西）
②YouS needV to eat somethingO to raise your blood sugar不定詞表目的
③How long多久 haveV youS beenp.p. on a dietC（主詞補語）
raise (v.) 提高；舉起（raise your hand）≠ rise（自己上升，不接受詞）。How long + have / has + S + p.p.? 問持續多久，回答用 for / since（22 校全考）。
▶ElaSince I last saw the photo of us in the Line group. I look fat in the photo.
從我上次在 Line 群組看到我們的合照之後。我在照片裡看起來好胖。
🔍 句構分析 ①Since I last saw the photo of ussince＋過去式子句 in the Line group修飾語
②IS look連綴動詞 fatC（主詞補語） in the photo修飾語
since + 過去式子句（since I last saw…）回答 How long。look 是連綴動詞 → 接形容詞 fat。photo (n.) 照片。
▶ElaNow I eat very little for each meal, and sometimes, I just skip meals.
現在我每一餐都吃很少，有時候乾脆不吃正餐。
🔍 句構分析 ①Now修飾語 IS eatV very little很少（量） for each meal修飾語
②and sometimes修飾語 IS just修飾語 skipV mealsO
very little＝很少（不可數的量）；skip meals 不吃正餐（skip → skipped 雙寫 p）。each + 單數名詞（each meal）。
▶CodyYou haven’t eaten enough food for over a week. That’s crazy!
你已經超過一個星期沒吃夠食物了。太瘋狂了！
🔍 句構分析 ①YouS haven’t eaten現在完成式否定 enough foodO for over a weekfor＋一段時間
②ThatS ’sV crazyC（主詞補語）
eat → ate → eaten。for + 一段時間（over a week＝超過一週）。enough 修飾名詞放前面（enough food），修飾形容詞放後面（old enough）。crazy 荒唐的、瘋狂的（9 校）。
▶ElaI know, but I really want to be as slim as possible.
我知道，可是我真的想要越瘦越好。
🔍 句構分析 ①IS knowV
②but修飾語 IS really修飾語 wantV to be as slim as possibleas＋原級＋as possible
as + 形容詞原級 + as possible 盡可能…（新泰考 as slim as possible）。slim 苗條的（11 校）。
▶CodyAppearance is not everything, and I think you look great.
外表不是一切，而且我覺得你看起來很好。
🔍 句構分析 ①AppearanceS is notV everythingC（主詞補語）
②and修飾語 IS thinkV
③youS look連綴動詞 greatC（主詞補語）
appearance (n.) 外表（appear 出現 → appearance）。A is not everything.＝A 不是一切。look + 形容詞 great。

▶CodyHave you ever tried exercising?

▶ElaNo, I never have. I don’t like to exercise.

▶CodyYou haven’t seen my sister for a long time, have you? She has lost five kilograms since she started exercising.
你很久沒見到我姊姊了，對吧？從她開始運動以來，她已經瘦了五公斤。

③SheS has lostV five kilogramsO since she started exercisingsince＋過去式子句

▶ElaFive kilos? How does she do it?

🔍 句構分析 ①Five kilos省略句
②How修飾語 doesV sheS doV itO
kilo＝kilogram 公斤。does 是助動詞，第二個 do 是本動詞（做）。
▶CodyShe exercises at least three times a week.

▶CodyYou got it.

You got it.＝你說對了、沒錯（口語）。

你節食多久了？——從我上次看到我們的合照之後。（課本句）
His grandpa died three years ago. ＝ His grandpa has been dead for three years.
他爺爺過世三年了。
✗ since six months（一段時間用 for）　✗ He has died for three years.（瞬間動詞不能接一段時間：die → be dead、buy → have、begin → be on、go on a diet → be on a diet）
22 校、62 題考這組。解題口訣：since 後面找「點」，for 後面找「段」；遇到瞬間動詞＋一段時間，改成「狀態」。
句型二｜ever / never / already / yet ＋ 附加問句▼
Have you ever + p.p.? → Yes, I have. / No, I haven’t. / No, never. S + have / has + already + p.p.（肯定句） S + haven’t / hasn’t + p.p. + yet.（否定）　Have you + p.p. + yet?（疑問） 附加問句：He has…, hasn’t he?　She has never…, has she?
Have you ever tried exercising? — No, I never have.
你試過運動嗎？——沒有，從來沒有。（課本句）
Have you had breakfast yet? — Yes, I have already had breakfast.
你吃過早餐了沒？——有，我已經吃過了。（課本句）
You haven’t seen my sister for a long time, have you?
你很久沒見到我姊姊了，對吧？（課本句；否定句 → 附加問句用肯定。She has never…, has she? 同理）
He’s eaten lunch, hasn’t he?
他吃過午餐了，不是嗎？（He’s＝He has，後面接 p.p.）
✗ I haven’t finished it already.（否定用 yet）　✗ Did you ever…? 在段考中多半要改 Have you ever…?　✗ She has never…, hasn’t she?
ever/never 14 校、already/yet 9 校、附加問句 10 校。看到 He’s / She’s 後面接 p.p.，一定是 has 的縮寫。
句型三｜have been to vs have gone to vs have been in▼
have / has been to + 地點　 → 去過（人已經回來） have / has gone to + 地點　 → 已經去了（人現在不在這裡） have / has been in + 地點 + for / since → 待在某地多久了
句子意思人在哪？
Cody has been to Japan twice.去過日本兩次回來了
Cody has gone to Japan.去日本了在日本或路上
Cody has been in Japan for a week.在日本待一週了在日本
Where’s Taco? — He has gone to a summer school for pets in Rock City.
Taco 呢？——牠去 Rock City 的寵物夏令學校了。（課本 Taco & Tom；人不在 → gone）
I have never been abroad.
我從沒出過國。（abroad 是副詞，不加 to）
✗ I have gone to Japan twice.（說話者人就在這裡，不可能「去了還沒回來」又兩次）　✗ have been to abroad
14 校考這組。看「次數」(twice, many times) 或 ever/never → been to；看「他不在、找不到人」→ gone to。
句型四｜時態判斷：過去式 vs 現在完成式（Review 1 頭號考點，20 校）▼
過去式：明確過去時間 → yesterday、last…、… ago、in 2020、When…?、已過世的人 現在完成式：過去延續到現在／經驗／已完成 → since、for the past…、so far、lately、ever、already、yet
過去式的線索現在完成式的線索
last month、two days agosince June、for the past two years
When did you…?How long have you…?
Grandpa worked…（已過世）so far、lately、already、yet
I went on a diet two months ago. ＝ I have been on a diet for two months.
我兩個月前開始節食。＝我已經節食兩個月了。
For the past twenty years, Ms. Lin has taught at this school.
過去二十年來，林老師一直在這所學校教書。
A: Have you got my postcard? B: Yes. I got it last Friday.
A：你收到我的明信片了嗎？B：收到了，上週五收到的。（問句完成式，答句有明確時間 → 過去式）
✗ I have seen him yesterday.（有明確過去時間不可用完成式）　✗ When have you bought it?（When 問句用過去式）　✗ My grandpa has worked here for 30 years.（若他已過世，要用 worked）
翰林 Review 1 的頭號考點，但 20 校、30 題。步驟：① 先圈時間詞 ② 有明確過去時間 → 過去式 ③ 有 since / for the past / so far / lately → 完成式。
句型五｜as + adj./adv. + as possible ＆ talk sb. into + V-ing▼
as + adj. / adv. + as possible ＝ as … as + S + can talk sb. into + V-ing（說服某人做）／talk sb. out of + V-ing（說服某人不做）
I really want to be as slim as possible.
我真的想要越瘦越好。（課本 Ela 的話）
Ted practices as hard as possible. ＝ Ted practices as hard as he can.
Ted 盡全力練習。
How can I talk them into letting me follow my dream?
我要怎麼說服他們讓我追夢？
✗ play as good as possible（修飾動詞用副詞 well）　✗ talk them into let me…（into 後接 V-ing）
talk sb. into 5 校考；和 Unit 2 的 trick sb. into + V-ing 同結構，常一起出。
