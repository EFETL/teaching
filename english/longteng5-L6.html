課文速覽（三大重點）

🎤 歷史時刻

1963/8/28，金恩博士在林肯紀念堂前對逾 25 萬人發表演說。他描繪一個種族歧視終結、黑人被平等對待的未來——這成為民權運動的決定性時刻。

✊ 百年仍不自由

林肯簽署《解放宣言》一百年後，黑人仍受種族隔離與歧視之苦、身陷貧困與流放。金恩以「One hundred years later…」層層排比控訴這可恥的處境，並宣告「絕不滿足」直到正義來臨。

🕊️ 我有一個夢

以「I have a dream…」的重複與意象，金恩描繪黑白孩子攜手、以品格而非膚色被評價的願景。其雄辯喚醒無數美國人的良知，至今仍激勵人心。

🎤 一｜導論：歷史性的演說
▶On August 28, 1963, Martin Luther King, Jr., marched to the Lincoln Memorial in Washington, DC, and delivered a historic speech before a crowd of over 250,000 people.
1963 年 8 月 28 日，小馬丁·路德·金恩遊行至華盛頓特區的林肯紀念堂，在超過 25 萬人的群眾面前發表了一場歷史性的演說。
🔍 句構分析 ①Martin Luther King, Jr.S marchedV to the Lincoln Memorial副詞 and deliveredV a historic speechO
march（遊行）、memorial（紀念堂）為重點字；deliver a speech = 發表演說；before = 在…面前。
▶In that speech, King outlined his hopes for the future—a time when racism would end and African Americans would be treated by whites as equals.
在那場演說中，金恩勾勒出他對未來的期盼——一個種族主義終結、非裔美國人被白人平等對待的時代。
🔍 句構分析 ①KingS outlinedV his hopes for the futureO —a time when racism would end...同位語（含關係副詞 when）
outline（概述）、racism（種族主義）為核心字；treat A as B = 把 A 當作 B；when 引導關係子句修飾 time。
▶That speech turned out to be a decisive moment in the civil rights movement.
那場演說最終成為民權運動中決定性的一刻。
🔍 句構分析 ①That speechS turned out to beV a decisive moment in the civil rights movementC
decisive（決定性的）、civil（公民的）為核心字；turn out to be = 結果證明是；civil rights movement = 民權運動。
▶Had Dr. King not made the speech, the United States might not have introduced a series of laws to grant new rights to African Americans within just a few years.
若金恩博士當年沒有發表這場演說，美國或許不會在短短幾年內就推出一系列法律，賦予非裔美國人新的權利。
🔍 句構分析 ①Had Dr. King not made the speech與過去相反的假設（倒裝） the United StatesS might not have introducedV（過去推測） a series of laws...O
grant（授予）為核心字；Had + S + p.p.＝If S had p.p. 的倒裝（見句型一）；主句 might not have + p.p.。
✊ 二｜演說：百年之後，仍不自由
▶I am happy to join with you today in what will go down in history as the greatest demonstration for freedom in the history of our nation.
今天，我很高興能與各位一同參與這場將名留青史、成為我國史上最偉大的爭自由遊行。
🔍 句構分析 ①IS am happyV to join with you不定詞 in what will go down in history...介系詞＋名詞子句
go down in history = 名留青史（片語）；demonstration = 示威遊行；what 引導名詞子句作 in 的受詞。
▶Five score years ago, a great American, in whose symbolic shadow we stand today, signed the Emancipation Proclamation.
一百年前，一位偉大的美國人——今日我們正站在他象徵性的身影之下——簽署了《解放宣言》。
🔍 句構分析 ①a great AmericanS , in whose symbolic shadow we stand today,介系詞＋關代（非限定） signedV the Emancipation ProclamationO
Five score = 一百（score＝20）；in whose shadow 介系詞＋關代（見句型二）；指林肯與《解放宣言》。
▶This momentous decree came as a great beacon light of hope to millions of Negro slaves who had been seared in the flames of withering injustice.
這項重大的法令，對於數百萬曾在凋敝不義的烈焰中飽受煎熬的黑奴而言，宛如一座偉大的希望燈塔。
🔍 句構分析 ①This momentous decreeS came asV a great beacon light of hopeC to millions of Negro slaves [who had been seared...]副詞（含關係子句）
flame（火焰）、wither（凋敝）為重點字；come as = 宛如；意象：燈塔＝希望；過去完成被動 had been seared。
▶It came as a joyous daybreak to end the long night of their captivity.
它宛如一個歡欣的破曉，結束了他們囚禁的漫漫長夜。
🔍 句構分析 ①ItS came asV a joyous daybreakC to end the long night of their captivity不定詞（目的）
captivity（囚禁）為補充字；意象對比：daybreak（破曉）↔ night（長夜）；捕捉「自由」的希望。
▶But one hundred years later, the Negro still is not free.
但一百年後，黑人仍然沒有獲得自由。
🔍 句構分析 ①one hundred years later時間 the NegroS still is notV freeC
此句起連用四次「One hundred years later…」句首重複（anaphora），層層堆疊控訴力量（見句型五）。
▶One hundred years later, the life of the Negro is still sadly crippled by the manacles of segregation and the chains of discrimination.
一百年後，黑人的生活仍悲哀地被種族隔離的枷鎖與歧視的鎖鏈所桎梏。
🔍 句構分析 ①the life of the NegroS is still crippled byV（被動） the manacles of segregation and the chains of discriminationby 受詞
cripple（桎梏、重創）、segregation（種族隔離）為核心字；意象 manacles/chains（枷鎖/鎖鏈）＝束縛。
▶One hundred years later, the Negro lives on a lonely island of poverty in the midst of a vast ocean of material prosperity.
一百年後，黑人依然生活在貧困的孤島上，四周卻是物質繁榮的汪洋大海。
🔍 句構分析 ①the NegroS livesV on a lonely island of poverty in the midst of a vast ocean of material prosperity副詞
midst（之中）為補充字；意象對比：island of poverty（貧困孤島）↔ ocean of prosperity（繁榮汪洋）。
▶One hundred years later, the Negro is still languishing in the corners of American society and finds himself an exile in his own land.
一百年後，黑人依然在美國社會的角落中飽受折磨，發現自己在故土上竟如同流亡者。
🔍 句構分析 ①the NegroS is languishingV and findsV himselfO an exile in his own landOC
exile（流亡者）為核心字；find + O + OC（發現…是…）；languish = 受煎熬。
▶And so we've come here today to dramatize a shameful condition.
因此，我們今天來到這裡，就是要凸顯這一可恥的處境。
🔍 句構分析 ①weS have comeV here today to dramatize a shameful condition副詞（不定詞表目的）
shameful（可恥的）為補充字；dramatize = 使戲劇化、凸顯。
🔥 三｜演說：我們絕不滿足（as long as）
▶...There are those who are asking..."When will you be satisfied?" We can never be satisfied as long as the Negro is the victim of the unspeakable horrors of police brutality.
有人問：「你們什麼時候才會滿足？」只要黑人仍是警察暴行那難以言喻恐怖下的受害者，我們就永遠不會滿足。
🔍 句構分析 ①WeS can never be satisfiedV as long as the Negro is the victim of...條件副詞子句
此段連用五次「We can never/cannot be satisfied as long as…」排比（見句型四、五）；as long as = 只要；brutality = 暴行。
▶We can never be satisfied as long as our bodies, heavy with the fatigue of travel, cannot gain lodging in the motels of the highways and the hotels of the cities.
只要我們拖著旅途的疲憊之軀，卻無法在公路旁的汽車旅館或城裡的旅館獲得住宿，我們就永遠不會滿足。
🔍 句構分析 ①our bodiesS , heavy with the fatigue of travel,形容詞片語（插入） cannot gainV lodgingO
fatigue（疲勞）為補充字；be heavy with = 充滿、負載著（片語）；lodging = 住宿。
▶We cannot be satisfied as long as the Negro's basic mobility is from a smaller ghetto to a larger one.
只要黑人的基本遷徙自由只是從較小的貧民窟搬到較大的貧民窟，我們就無法滿足。
🔍 句構分析 ①as long as連接詞 the Negro's basic mobilityS isV from a smaller ghetto to a larger oneC
mobility（流動/遷徙性）為補充字；one 代替 ghetto；ghetto = 貧民窟。
▶We can never be satisfied as long as our children are stripped of their self-hood and robbed of their dignity by a sign stating: "For Whites Only."
只要我們的孩子仍被一塊寫著「僅限白人」的標誌剝奪自我認同與尊嚴，我們就永遠不會滿足。
🔍 句構分析 ①our childrenS are stripped of ... and robbed ofV（被動並列） their self-hood ... their dignity受詞 by a sign stating...by 受詞
rob（剝奪）、dignity（尊嚴）為核心/補充字；strip/rob sb. of sth. = 剝奪某人某物；stating 現在分詞修飾 sign。
▶We cannot be satisfied as long as a Negro in Mississippi cannot vote and a Negro in New York believes he has nothing for which to vote.
只要密西西比的黑人不能投票、紐約的黑人覺得根本沒有值得他投票的對象，我們就無法滿足。
🔍 句構分析 ①as long as連接詞 a Negro in MississippiS cannot voteV ... nothing for which to vote介系詞＋關代＋不定詞
nothing for which to vote = 沒有可為之投票的對象（介系詞＋關代＋不定詞，見句型二）。
▶No, no, we are not satisfied, and we will not be satisfied until justice rolls down like waters, and righteousness like a mighty stream.
不，不，我們並不滿足；直到正義如流水般傾瀉、公理如奔騰江河般奔流，我們才會滿足。
🔍 句構分析 ①weS will not be satisfiedV until justice rolls down like waters...時間副詞子句
until = 直到；明喻 like waters / like a mighty stream；righteousness = 公義。
🕊️ 四｜演說：我有一個夢（I have a dream）
▶...Let us not wallow in the valley of despair, I say to you today, my friends. And so even though we face the difficulties of today and tomorrow, I still have a dream. It is a dream deeply rooted in the American dream.
朋友們，今天我要對你們說：我們不要沉淪在絕望的幽谷裡。因此，即使我們面對今日與明日的種種困難，我仍然懷抱著一個夢——一個深植於美國夢之中的夢。
🔍 句構分析 ①even though we face the difficulties...讓步子句 IS still haveV a dreamO
even though = 即使；意象 valley of despair（絕望幽谷）；rooted in = 深植於。
▶I have a dream that one day this nation will rise up and live out the true meaning of its creed: "We hold these truths to be self-evident, that all men are created equal."
我有一個夢：有一天，這個國家將奮起，真正實踐它信條的真義——「我們認為這些真理不言而喻：人人生而平等。」
🔍 句構分析 ①IS haveV a dream [that one day this nation will... live out...]O（同位語子句）
live out = 實踐（片語）；此段連用多次「I have a dream that…」句首重複（見句型五）；引美國《獨立宣言》。
▶I have a dream that one day on the red hills of Georgia, the sons of former slaves and the sons of former slave owners will be able to sit down together at the table of brotherhood.
我有一個夢：有一天，在喬治亞州的紅土丘陵上，昔日奴隸的子孫與昔日奴隸主的子孫，能夠一同坐在兄弟情誼的桌旁。
🔍 句構分析 ①the sons of former slaves and the sons of former slave ownersS（並列） will be able to sit downV together at the table of brotherhood副詞
be able to V = 能夠；意象 table of brotherhood（兄弟情誼之桌）＝和解共處。
▶I have a dream that one day even the state of Mississippi, a state sweltering with the heat of injustice and oppression, will be transformed into an oasis of freedom and justice.
我有一個夢：有一天，就連密西西比州——一個被不義與壓迫的熱浪蒸騰的州——都將蛻變為自由與正義的綠洲。
🔍 句構分析 ①even the state of MississippiS , a state sweltering with...,同位語（含分詞） will be transformedV（被動） into an oasis of freedom and justice副詞
oppression（壓迫）為核心字；transform A into B = 使 A 轉變為 B；意象對比 heat（熱浪）↔ oasis（綠洲）。
▶I have a dream that my four little children will one day live in a nation where they will not be judged by the color of their skin but by the content of their character.
我有一個夢：有一天，我的四個孩子將生活在一個國度裡，在那裡他們不會以膚色、而是以品格的內涵來被評斷。
🔍 句構分析 ①my four little childrenS will liveV in a nation [where they will not be judged by A but by B]關係副詞子句
not by A but by B = 不以 A 而以 B（見句型三）——本課最著名的金句；where 關係副詞修飾 nation。
▶I have a dream today! I have a dream that one day, down in Alabama, little black boys and black girls will be able to join hands with little white boys and white girls as sisters and brothers.
今天，我懷抱著一個夢！我有一個夢：有一天，在阿拉巴馬州，黑人的男孩女孩能與白人的男孩女孩像姊妹兄弟般手牽著手。
🔍 句構分析 ①little black boys and black girlsS will be able to join hands withV little white boys and white girlsO as sisters and brothers副詞
join hands (with) = 攜手合作（片語）；「I have a dream today!」為情感高潮的短句重複。
🌄 五｜演說：帶著信念，自由響起
▶I have a dream that one day every valley shall be exalted, every hill and mountain shall be made low, the rough places will be made plain, and the crooked places will be made straight; and the glory of the Lord shall be revealed and all flesh shall see it together.
我有一個夢：有一天，一切幽谷都將被高舉、一切丘陵高山都將被削平，崎嶇之地將化為坦途、彎曲之路將變得筆直；主的榮光將要顯現，凡有血肉之軀者都將一同看見。
🔍 句構分析 ①every valleyS shall be exaltedV（被動）；the rough placesS will be madeV plainC
多組被動並列排比（shall be exalted / made low / made plain / made straight）；引《聖經·以賽亞書》，莊嚴語氣。
▶This is our hope, and this is the faith that I go back to the South with. With this faith, we will be able to hew out of the mountain of despair a stone of hope.
這是我們的希望，這是我帶回南方的信念。懷著這份信念，我們將能從絕望之山中，鑿出一塊希望之石。
🔍 句構分析 ①thisS isV the faith [that I go back to the South with]C（關係子句，介系詞 with 在後）
此段連用三次「With this faith, we will be able to…」句首重複；意象 mountain of despair ↔ stone of hope。
▶With this faith, we will be able to transform the jangling discords of our nation into a beautiful symphony of brotherhood.
懷著這份信念，我們將能把我國刺耳的紛擾，轉化為一曲美麗的兄弟情誼交響樂。
🔍 句構分析 ①weS will be able to transformV the jangling discords of our nationO into a beautiful symphony of brotherhood副詞
symphony（交響樂）為補充字；transform A into B；音樂意象：discords（不和諧音）↔ symphony（交響樂）。
▶With this faith, we will be able to work together, to pray together, to struggle together, to stand up for freedom together, knowing that we will be free one day.
懷著這份信念，我們將能一同工作、一同祈禱、一同奮鬥、一同為自由挺身而出，深知有一天我們終將自由。
🔍 句構分析 ①weS will be able to work... pray... struggle... stand upV（不定詞並列） , knowing that we will be free one day分詞構句
to V 排比（work / pray / struggle / stand up）；stand up for = 挺身捍衛；knowing... 分詞構句表附帶。
▶And when we let freedom ring from every village and every city, we will be able to speed up that day when all of God's children will be able to join hands and sing: Free at last! Free at last! Thank God Almighty, we are free at last!
當我們讓自由從每個村莊、每座城市響起，我們將能加速那一天的到來——那時，上帝所有的子民都能攜手同聲高唱：終於自由了！終於自由了！感謝全能的上帝，我們終於自由了！
🔍 句構分析 ①when we let freedom ring...時間副詞子句 weS will be able to speed upV that day [when...]O（含關係副詞）
let + O + V原形（let freedom ring）；結尾以黑人靈歌「Free at last!」重複收束，情感達到頂點。
🌟 六｜賞析結語：不朽的雄辯
▶Although Dr. King's dream has yet to be fully realized, his eloquence appealed to human emotions, and his rhetorical skills awoke the consciences of many Americans who had ignored or condoned racial oppression.
儘管金恩博士的夢想尚未完全實現，他的雄辯打動了人心，他的修辭功力更喚醒了許多曾經漠視或縱容種族壓迫的美國人的良知。
🔍 句構分析 ①Although Dr. King's dream has yet to be fully realized讓步子句 his eloquenceS appealed toV human emotionsO
eloquence（雄辯）、rhetorical（修辭的）、conscience（良知）為核心字；have yet to V = 尚未…（片語）；appeal to = 打動、訴諸。
▶"I Have a Dream" was to civil rights supporters what a battle cry is to a warrior.
〈我有一個夢〉之於民權運動的支持者，正如戰吼之於一名戰士。
🔍 句構分析 ①"I Have a Dream"S wasV to A what B is to C類比句型
warrior（戰士）為補充字；A is to B what C is to D = A 之於 B 猶如 C 之於 D（重要類比句型）。
▶The speech continues to inspire people to this day and will likely do so as long as inequality exists.
這場演說至今仍持續激勵著人們，而且只要不平等依然存在，它很可能將持續發揮這樣的力量。
🔍 句構分析 ①The speechS continues to inspireV peopleO and will likely do so as long as inequality exists副詞（含條件子句）
as long as = 只要（見句型四）；do so = 代替前面的動作（inspire people）；to this day = 直到今日。

核心句型文法解析
👆 點擊每個句型標題，展開「公式 + 例句 + 常見錯誤 + 學測重點」。
句型一｜與過去事實相反的假設（倒裝 Had + S + p.p.）▼
If S had p.p., S would/might have p.p. → 省略 If 倒裝：Had + S + p.p., S would/might have p.p.

與過去事實相反的假設；省略 If 時，把 had 提到主詞前形成倒裝。

Had Dr. King not made the speech, the US might not have introduced those laws.
若金恩沒發表演說，美國或許不會推出那些法律。（課文）
✗ Had Dr. King didn't make...（倒裝後不再用 did）；否定用 Had + S + not + p.p.。
同類倒裝：Were I you...（與現在相反）；Should you need...（未來）。學測高頻。
句型二｜介系詞＋關係代名詞（含 whose / for which to V）▼
先行詞 + 介系詞 + which / whom / whose + N ...

關代作介系詞受詞時，介系詞可移到關代前（正式）。

a great American, in whose symbolic shadow we stand
一位偉大的美國人，我們正站在他象徵性的身影下（課文）
he has nothing for which to vote
他沒有可為之投票的對象（課文）
✗ in that / for that（介系詞後不可接 that）。
whose ＝ 所有格關代（接名詞）；學測常考關代格位。
句型三｜not A but B（不是 A 而是 B）▼
not + A but + B（A、B 對等；動詞與最近的主詞一致）

表「不是 A，而是 B」，A、B 詞性/結構須對等。

…judged not by the color of their skin but by the content of their character.
…不以膚色、而以品格內涵來評斷。（課文金句）
✗ not by A and by B；A、B 結構須平行（by + N / by + N）。
延伸 not only A but also B；學測常考平行與對等。
句型四｜as long as（只要，條件副詞子句）▼
S + V ..., as long as + S + V ...（只要…就…）

as long as 表「條件」＝只要；亦可表「長達」（時間）。

We can never be satisfied as long as injustice exists.
只要不義存在，我們就絕不滿足。（課文）
條件子句表未來時用現在式（exists，非 will exist）。
同義 so long as / provided that；學測常考條件連接詞。
句型五｜修辭：句首重複與排比（Anaphora & Parallelism）▼
重複相同句首 + 對等結構，堆疊情感與節奏

本篇最著名的修辭：以相同句首反覆開頭（anaphora），營造氣勢。

One hundred years later, ... / I have a dream that ... / With this faith, ...
「一百年後…」「我有一個夢…」「懷著這份信念…」層層排比。（課文）
寫作可善用排比與句首重複增強說服力，但需保持結構對等。
學測寫作評分重視「連貫與修辭」；適度排比能加分。
