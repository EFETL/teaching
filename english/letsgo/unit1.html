句型回答例句
some／any
一些／任何		有 → some

他／她會…嗎？		Yes, she does.
No, he doesn't.		Does Lina talk on the phone?

你會…嗎？		Yes, I do.

有一個…嗎？		Yes, there is.

Can ... swim?
…會…嗎？		Yes, they can.
No, they can't.		Can they swim?
No, they can't.
三個口訣：
  1. 問什麼，就用什麼回答：問 Is 答 is／isn't；問 Does 答 does／doesn't；問 Do 答 do／don't；問 Can 答 can／can't。
  2. he／she 回答時動作字要加 s：She talks on the phone. 但 doesn't、don't、can't 後面的動作字不加 s。
  3. 有 → some，沒有 → any。
🎨 顏色對照：橘色＝誰、綠色＝做什麼、紫色＝小幫手字（is／do／does／can／some／any／Yes／No）、藍色＝東西或地方

⚠️ 最容易錯的五點

  1. Does 後面動作字不加 s：Does Lina talks → Does Lina talk…?
  2. doesn't 後面動作字也不加 s：He doesn't likes pasta. → He doesn't like pasta.
  3. 沒有的句子要用 any：I don't have some candy. → I don't have any candy.
  4. Is 後面的動作字要加 -ing：Is John walk? → Is John walking?
  5. 問 Where 不能回答 Yes／No：Where is Ted? — Yes, he is. → He's in the library.
