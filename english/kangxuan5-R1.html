🟧 Review A＋B｜時態總複習、分詞形容詞

課本用「餅乾怪獸」複習 7 種時態：現在簡單式、現在進行式、過去簡單式、過去進行式、未來式（be going to / will）、現在完成式。再複習 L2 的 -ing / -ed 分詞形容詞（The story is interesting to me. / I am very interested in the story.）。段考最愛考：過去式 vs 現在完成式（康軒 25 校）。

📘 Review A｜複習時態（Read Aloud）
▶The monster eats a cookie every day.
（現在簡單式）怪獸每天吃一塊餅乾。
🔍 句構分析 ①The monsterS eatsV（現在簡單式・單數加 s） a cookieO every day頻率
現在簡單式：習慣、事實。線索 every day / usually / often。主詞第三人稱單數動詞加 s。
▶The monster is eating a cookie now.
（現在進行式）怪獸現在正在吃一塊餅乾。
🔍 句構分析 ①The monsterS is eatingV（be＋V-ing） a cookieO now修飾語
現在進行式：be + V-ing，線索 now / Look! / Listen!
▶The monster ate many cookies last week.
（過去簡單式）怪獸上週吃了很多餅乾。
🔍 句構分析 ①The monsterS ateV（過去式） many cookiesO last week明確過去時間
過去簡單式：明確的過去時間（last week / yesterday / … ago / in 2020）。eat-ate-eaten。
▶The monster was eating a cookie then.
（過去進行式）怪獸那時正在吃一塊餅乾。
🔍 句構分析 ①The monsterS was eatingV（was/were＋V-ing） a cookieO then那時
過去進行式：過去某時刻正在做（台北誠正：When I saw the boy, he was playing basketball.）。
▶The monster is going to eat a cookie tomorrow. The monster will eat seven cookies next week.
（未來式）怪獸明天要吃一塊餅乾。怪獸下週會吃七塊餅乾。
🔍 句構分析 ①The monsterS is going to eatV（be going to＋原形） a cookieO tomorrow修飾語
②The monsterS will eatV（will＋原形） seven cookiesO next week修飾語
未來式：be going to + 原形 / will + 原形；線索 tomorrow / next … / soon。
▶The monster has eaten a cookie.
（現在完成式）怪獸已經吃了一塊餅乾。
🔍 句構分析 ①The monsterS has eatenV（have/has＋p.p.） a cookieO
現在完成式：have/has + p.p.，表經驗、持續、已完成（L1 Grammar A/B/C）。不能和明確過去時間連用（✗ has eaten a cookie yesterday）。
▶Q: Have you ever read an English novel? A: Yes, I have.
問：你讀過英文小說嗎？答：有，我讀過。
🔍 句構分析 ①Have助動詞 youS ever修飾語 readp.p.（讀音 [rɛd]） an English novelO
②Yes修飾語 IS have簡答
Review 1 Read and Fill In 第 1 段的答案。novel（小說）是 R1 單字。read-read-read 三態同形，但過去式、p.p. 唸 [rɛd]。
▶Q: What did you read? A: My Sister’s Keeper. I read it last summer. Q: Did you like it? A: Yes, I did.
問：你讀了什麼？答：《姊姊的守護者》。我去年夏天讀的。問：你喜歡嗎？答：喜歡。
🔍 句構分析 ①WhatO did ... readV（過去式問句） youS
②IS readV（過去式） itO last summer修飾語
③Did ... likeV youS itO
時態判斷經典：先用完成式問經驗（Have you ever…?），一旦追問細節、出現 last summer → 改用過去式（What did you read? / I read it last summer.）。
▶Q: How long have you been interested in singing? A: Since I was a junior high school student.
問：你對唱歌有興趣多久了？答：從我是國中生開始。
🔍 句構分析 ①How long修飾語 have ... beenV（現在完成式） youS interested in singingC（主詞補語）
②Sincesince＋過去式子句 IS wasV a junior high school studentC（主詞補語）
How long + 現在完成式；回答 since + 過去式子句（since I was…）。Read and Fill In 第 2 段答案：have / been / was。
▶Q: Do you want to be a singer in the future? A: Yes, I do. I am going to start my singing classes next month.
問：你將來想當歌手嗎？答：想。我下個月要開始上歌唱課。
🔍 句構分析 ①Do ... wantV（現在式問句） youS to be a singerO in the future修飾語
②IS am going to startV（未來式） my singing classesO next month修飾語
用 Do 問 → Yes, I do；next month → 未來式 am going to start（或 will start）。
📘 Review B｜分詞形容詞（Read Aloud）＋ Activity
▶The story is interesting to me. The news was surprising to everyone.
（現在分詞）這個故事讓我覺得有趣。這消息讓每個人都很驚訝。
🔍 句構分析 ①The storyS isV interestingV-ing（令人…） to me修飾語
②The newsS wasV surprisingC（主詞補語） to everyone修飾語
事物 + be + V-ing (+ to 人)：事物「令人」……。news 是不可數名詞 → was。
▶I am very interested in the story. Everyone was surprised at the news.
（過去分詞）我對這個故事很感興趣。每個人都對這消息感到驚訝。
🔍 句構分析 ①IS amV very interestedV-ed（感到…） in the story修飾語
②EveryoneS wasV surprisedC（主詞補語） at the news修飾語
人 + be + V-ed + 介系詞：interested in、surprised at。Everyone 視為單數 → was。
▶MollyMath is the most interesting subject to me.
數學對我來說是最有趣的科目。
🔍 句構分析 ①MathS isV the most interesting subject最高級 to me修飾語
Activity 範例。interesting 的最高級 the most interesting；subject 是 L1 單字。
▶JackMolly said she was interested in math.
Molly 說她對數學有興趣。
🔍 句構分析 ①MollyS saidV she was interested in mathO（省略 that；時態一致 is→was）
轉述句（L2 Grammar 2B）：said → 子句 is 改 was、I 改 she。
🍔 Review C｜Useful Language — A Special Order（點餐用語）
▶ClerkWelcome to Yummy Burgers. How may I help you?
歡迎光臨 Yummy Burgers。有什麼可以為您服務的嗎？
🔍 句構分析 ①Welcome toV（祈使／招呼） Yummy BurgersO
②How修飾語 may ... helpV IS youO
店員招呼語：How may / can I help you?
▶CustomerI need a hamburger with extra cheese.
我要一個加起司的漢堡。
🔍 句構分析 ①IS needV a hamburgerO with extra cheesewith＋N（附加）
extra（額外的）是 R1 單字；with extra + 食材＝多加……。
▶CustomerI’d like to order a large French fries without salt.
我想點一份不加鹽的大薯。
🔍 句構分析 ①IS ’d like to orderV（would like to V） a large French friesO without saltwithout＋N（不要）
without + N＝不要……。a large (order of) French fries＝大薯。
▶CustomerCan I have a cup of tea with no ice?
我可以要一杯去冰的茶嗎？
🔍 句構分析 ①Can助動詞 IS haveV a cup of teaO with no ice去冰
with no ice＝去冰；a cup of + 不可數名詞。
▶ClerkSure. Anything else?
好的。還需要其他東西嗎？
🔍 句構分析 ①Sure修飾語
②Anything else省略句（＝Do you want anything else?）
Anything else?＝還要什麼嗎？Example：I love popcorn. I always eat a lot when I watch a movie.

▶Jenny, over here.
Jenny，這裡！
🔍 句構分析 ①Jenny稱呼
②over here在這裡
over here＝（招手）在這邊。
▶JennyHi, Mike.
嗨，Mike。
🔍 句構分析 ①Hi修飾語 Mike稱呼
Jenny 抵達機場，用親臉頰的方式和 Mike 打招呼。
▶Wow! I can’t believe you’re finally here.
哇！真不敢相信你終於到了。
🔍 句構分析 ①Wow修飾語
②IS can’t believeV you’re finally hereO（省略 that 的名詞子句）
can’t believe (that) + 子句（L2 Grammar 2 也有：I can’t believe you didn’t do that.）。finally＝終於。
▶Are you hungry? Do you want to get something to eat?
你餓了嗎？想吃點東西嗎？
🔍 句構分析 ①AreV youS hungryC（主詞補語）
②Do ... want to getV youS something to eat不定詞修飾 something
something to eat＝吃的東西（不定詞放在 something 後面修飾）。
▶That would be great.
那太好了。
🔍 句構分析 ①ThatS would beV greatC（主詞補語）
接受邀請的禮貌說法：That would be great.
▶Wait here while I get us a taxi.
我去幫我們叫計程車時，你們在這裡等。
🔍 句構分析 ①Wait祈使句 here修飾語
②while 子句 IS getV usO a taxiO
while（當……時）是 R1 單字；get + 人 + 物＝幫某人弄到……。
▶GirlWhy did Jenny kiss you on the cheeks?
Jenny 為什麼親你的臉頰？
🔍 句構分析 ①Why修飾語 did ... kissV（過去式問句） JennyS youO on the cheeks修飾語
kiss + 人 + on the cheek(s)＝親某人的臉頰；kiss（親吻）、cheek（臉頰）是 R1 單字。
▶MikeIt’s just the way French people greet each other, dear.
親愛的，那只是法國人彼此打招呼的方式。
🔍 句構分析 ①ItS ’sV just the wayC French people greet each other關係子句（省略 that/in which） dear稱呼
the way + 子句＝……的方式。greet（打招呼）、each other（彼此）。法國的貼臉頰禮叫 la bise。
🎃 Reading Challenge 1（搭配 L1・品德教育）｜A NEW COLOR
▶ChloeNelson, have you seen this article about Halloween? It’s very surprising.
Nelson，你看過這篇關於萬聖節的文章嗎？非常令人驚訝。
🔍 句構分析 ①Nelson稱呼 have ... seenV（現在完成式・經驗） youS this article about HalloweenO
②ItS ’sV very surprising事物令人驚訝
Have you seen…? 問經驗（L1）；文章「令人」驚訝 → surprising（L2）。article（文章）是 R1 單字。高雄七賢段考把 article 改成 reading 出克漏字。
▶NelsonNo, but I’m interested in finding out more.
沒有，但我有興趣多了解一些。
🔍 句構分析 ①No修飾語 but修飾語 IS ’m interested inbe＋V-ed＋介系詞 finding out moreO（動名詞）
be interested in + V-ing；find out＝查明、了解。高雄七賢克漏字第 16 題就考這格（am interested in）。
▶ChloeWell, it seems that since 2018, blue has become a new color for Halloween buckets.
嗯，似乎從 2018 年起，藍色就成了萬聖節糖果桶的新顏色。
🔍 句構分析 ①Well修飾語 it虛主詞 seemsV that since 2018, blue has become a new color for Halloween buckets真主詞（that 子句）
It seems that + 子句＝似乎……（seem 是 R1 單字）。since 2018 + has become（現在完成式）。bucket（桶子）。七賢第 17 題考 has become。
▶NelsonBlue? Why have people started using blue Halloween buckets?
藍色？為什麼大家開始用藍色的萬聖節桶子？
🔍 句構分析 ①Why修飾語 have ... startedV（現在完成式） peopleS using blue Halloween bucketsO（動名詞）
start + V-ing / to V 都可以。
▶ChloeIt all began with Alicia Plumer and her 21-year-old son.
這一切都從 Alicia Plumer 和她 21 歲的兒子開始。
🔍 句構分析 ①It allS began withV（begin with 從……開始） Alicia Plumer and her 21-year-old sonO
21-year-old 複合形容詞：year 不加 s、中間有連字號（比較 He is 21 years old.）。
▶ChloeHe loves Halloween a lot, but he is autistic and has problems getting along with people.
他非常喜歡萬聖節，但他有自閉症，和人相處有困難。
🔍 句構分析 ①HeS lovesV HalloweenO a lot修飾語
②but修飾語 heS is ... and hasV autisticC（主詞補語） problemsO getting along with peoplehave problems＋V-ing
have problems (in) + V-ing＝做……有困難；get along with＝和……相處。autistic＝自閉症的（＊註解字）。
▶ChloeAlicia knew that some people would have problems with that, so she came up with the idea of a blue Halloween bucket.
Alicia 知道有些人會對此有困擾，所以她想出了藍色萬聖節桶子的點子。
🔍 句構分析 ①AliciaS knewV that some people would have problems with thatO（that 子句；時態一致 would）
②so修飾語 sheS came up withV（想出） the idea of a blue Halloween bucketO
knew that … would：主要子句過去式 → 子句用 would（L2 時態一致）。come up with＝想出（康軒 3 校考過）。
▶NelsonDo you mean that she gave him a blue Halloween bucket instead of the more common orange one?
你是說她給他一個藍色萬聖節桶子，而不是比較常見的橘色桶子？
🔍 句構分析 ①Do ... meanV youS that she gave him a blue Halloween bucket instead of the more common orange oneO（that 子句）
instead of + N＝而不是……；one 代替前面的 bucket。common（L1 高頻字）的比較級 more common。
▶ChloeYes. She thought that when people saw it, they would know that the person had special needs.
對。她認為大家看到它時，就會知道那個人有特殊需求。
🔍 句構分析 ①Yes修飾語 SheS thoughtV that ... they would know that the person had special needsO（that 子句內又有 that 子句） when people saw it時間子句
thought that … would know that … had：一路時態一致。special needs＝特殊需求。Activity 判斷：這句是 opinion（她「認為」）。
▶NelsonThat’s very smart of her. How did she let people know about her son and his blue bucket?
她真聰明。她怎麼讓大家知道她兒子和他的藍色桶子的？
🔍 句構分析 ①ThatS ’sV very smartC（主詞補語） of herof＋人（稱讚人的特質）
②How修飾語 did ... let使役動詞 sheS peopleO know about her son and his blue bucket原形動詞補語
It’s / That’s + 人格形容詞 + of + 人（smart / kind / nice of her）；比較 It’s hard for me to…（L1）。let + 人 + 原形。
▶ChloeShe posted it on Facebook, and people were deeply touched and shared it over 28,000 times.
她把它貼到 Facebook 上，大家深受感動，分享了超過 28,000 次。
🔍 句構分析 ①SheS postedV itO on Facebook修飾語
②and修飾語 peopleS were ... touched and sharedV deeply修飾語 itO over 28,000 times修飾語
touched（受到感動的）是 R1 單字，人「被感動」用 -ed（比較 touching 令人感動的）。七賢第 19 題考 deeply touched。
▶NelsonThat’s great. I never get tired of hearing stories about being nice and kind. Thanks for sharing.
太好了。我從來不會厭倦聽關於善良體貼的故事。謝謝你的分享。
🔍 句構分析 ①ThatS ’sV greatC（主詞補語）
②IS never修飾語 get tired of厭倦 hearing stories about being nice and kindO（動名詞）
③Thanks for感謝 sharingO
get tired of + V-ing（L2 be tired of）；Thanks for + V-ing。about being…：介系詞後接 V-ing。
🎤 Reading Challenge 2（搭配 L2・生涯規劃教育）｜The Difficulty of Becoming a K-Pop Star
▶Many teenagers in South Korea dream of being a K-pop star.
許多南韓青少年夢想成為 K-pop 明星。
🔍 句構分析 ①Many teenagers in South KoreaS dream ofV＋介系詞 being a K-pop starO（動名詞）
dream of + V-ing＝夢想……。teenager（青少年）。
▶However, before they can have a chance of becoming successful, they must become trainees first.
然而，在他們有機會成功之前，必須先成為練習生。
🔍 句構分析 ①However轉折 before 子句 theyS can haveV a chance of becoming successfulO
②theyS must becomeV traineesC（主詞補語） first修飾語
a chance of + V-ing；successful（L1 段考高頻字）。trainee＝練習生（＊註解字）。However 是 L1 的對比轉折語。
▶That, in fact, is not as easy as it may sound.
事實上，那並不像聽起來那麼容易。
🔍 句構分析 ①ThatS in fact事實上 is notV as easynot as…as as it may sound比較對象
not as + 原級 + as＝不像……那麼……。in fact＝事實上。
▶First, these teenagers have to spend years training hard.
第一，這些青少年必須花好幾年努力訓練。
🔍 句構分析 ①First列點 these teenagersS have to spendV yearsO training hardspend 時間＋V-ing
spend + 時間 + V-ing（L2 也有：spend time going out）。First / Second / Third / Finally＝L2 Reading Skills 列點轉折語。
▶They must learn to sing, dance, and act, and still find time to do their schoolwork.
他們必須學唱歌、跳舞和演戲，還要找時間做功課。
🔍 句構分析 ①TheyS must learn ... and still findV to sing, dance, and actO timeO to do their schoolwork不定詞（目的）
learn to V；find time to V＝找時間做……。
▶Second, they’re away from their families, so they feel lonely from time to time.
第二，他們離開家人，所以有時會感到寂寞。
🔍 句構分析 ①Second修飾語 theyS ’re away from遠離 their familiesO
②so修飾語 theyS feelV lonelyC（主詞補語） from time to time有時
be away from＝離開；from time to time＝有時候。lonely 是形容詞（-ly 結尾但不是副詞）。

核心句型文法解析
👆 點擊每個句型標題，展開「公式 + 例句 + 常見錯誤 + 會考／段考重點」。
句型一｜Review A：七種時態總整理（餅乾怪獸）▼
現在簡單式 S + V(s) The monster eats a cookie every day. 現在進行式 S + am/is/are + V-ing The monster is eating a cookie now. 過去簡單式 S + V-ed / 不規則過去式 The monster ate many cookies last week. 過去進行式 S + was/were + V-ing The monster was eating a cookie then. 未來式 S + be going to / will + V … a cookie tomorrow / seven cookies next week. 現在完成式 S + have/has + p.p. The monster has eaten a cookie.

先找時間線索再決定時態：every day（習慣）、now（正在）、last week（過去）、then（過去那時正在）、tomorrow / next week（未來）、already / since / for / ever（完成）。

Have you ever read an English novel? — Yes, I have.
你讀過英文小說嗎？——有。（課本 Read and Fill In）
What did you read? — I read it last summer.
你讀了什麼？——我去年夏天讀的。（追問細節改過去式）
I am going to start my singing classes next month.
我下個月要開始上歌唱課。（課本）
✗ The monster is eat a cookie now.（→ is eating）✗ He has eaten it last week.（→ ate）✗ I will to start…（will + 原形）
康軒 25 校、34 題考時態判斷；江翠、新北中正把這類題目標為【R1】。
句型二｜段考重點：過去式 vs 現在完成式▼
過去式：明確過去時間 yesterday / last … / … ago / in 2020 / just now / When …? 現在完成式：與現在有關 since / for / ever / never / already / yet / so far / How long Have you ever been to …? — Yes, I went there last year.（追問細節改過去式） It has been + 一段時間 + since + S + 過去式

問自己：「事情發生的時間點有沒有被說出來？」有 → 過去式；沒說時間、強調「到現在」的經驗或結果 → 現在完成式。When 問的是時間點，不能配完成式。

Blue has become a new color for Halloween buckets since 2018.
從 2018 年起藍色成了萬聖節桶子的新顏色。（RC1）
I have already had lunch. — Oh? When did you eat?
我已經吃過午餐了。——喔？你什麼時候吃的？（台中向上原型）
Andy lived in Kaohsiung three years ago, but now he has moved to Taipei.
Andy 三年前住高雄，但現在已經搬到台北了。（台北誠正原型）
✗ When have you eaten lunch?（When → 過去式）✗ I have read it last week.（last week → read）✗ The dog has died for two years.（die 是瞬間動詞 → has been dead）
口訣：When、ago、last、yesterday、just now → 過去式；since、for、ever、yet、so far → 完成式。瞬間動詞換狀態：die→be dead、buy→have、begin→be on、leave→be away、come→be here。
句型三｜Review B：分詞形容詞 -ing / -ed（L2 複習）▼
事物 + be + V-ing (+ to 人) The story is interesting to me. 人 + be + V-ed + 介系詞 + 事物 I am very interested in the story. Everyone was surprised at the news. Activity：interested / interesting excited / exciting bored / boring surprised / surprising tired / tiring

Activity 題：What do you think is the most interesting subject?（事物）／When was the last time you felt really bored?（人的感受）／Are you often tired when you get up?

It’s very surprising. / People were deeply touched.
非常令人驚訝。／大家深受感動。（RC1）
I’m interested in finding out more.
我有興趣多了解一些。（RC1，介系詞後接 V-ing）
Talk about the most exciting experience in your life.
談談你人生中最刺激的經驗。（課本 Activity）
✗ I felt really boring.（人的感受 → bored）✗ the most excited experience（經驗是事物 → exciting）
情緒形容詞康軒 30 校全考；R1 的 touched / touching 也是同一組概念。
句型四｜It’s + adj. + of 人 vs for 人＋It seems that…（Reading Challenge 1）▼
It’s / That’s + 人格形容詞(smart/kind/nice) + of + 人 (+ to V) That’s very smart of her. It’s + 事情形容詞(hard/easy/common) + for + 人 + to V It was common for people to fry fish.（L1） It seems that + 子句 ＝ 似乎…… It seems that blue has become a new color.

形容詞描述「人的特質」用 of；描述「事情的難易、普遍」用 for。It seems that 的 it 是虛主詞，真主詞是 that 子句。

That’s very smart of her.
她真聰明。（RC1）
It seems that since 2018, blue has become a new color for Halloween buckets.
似乎從 2018 年起，藍色成了萬聖節桶子的新顏色。（RC1）
It is kind of you to help me. / It is hard for me to get up early.
你幫我真好心。／早起對我來說很難。
✗ It’s very smart for her.（smart 是人的特質 → of）✗ It’s hard of me to get up early.（→ for）
康軒 7 校考虛主詞 It is + adj + for sb + to V（台中五權、屏東中正、新北中正…）。
句型五｜Reading Skills：事實 vs 意見、文章結構（Reading Challenge）▼
Fact（事實）：可以查證的事 … he is autistic and has problems getting along with people. Opinion（意見）：想法、推測、感受 She thought that when people saw it, they would know… 文章結構：主題 → 列點細節（First / Second / Third / Finally）→ 結論建議

RC1 Activity 要把句子分成 fact / opinion；看到 think / believe / seem / should / smart 等表達想法、推測的字，多半是 opinion。RC2 的主題判斷與文章結構題：南韓青少年想當明星 → 練習生的問題 → 確定你的夢想（①③②）。

… she came up with the idea of a blue Halloween bucket.
她想出了藍色桶子的點子。（fact）
She thought that when people saw it, they would know that the person had special needs.
她認為大家看到就會知道。（opinion）
K-pop trainees do not have an easy life.
K-pop 練習生的生活並不輕鬆。（RC2 主題句）
✗ 把 It seems that…（推測語氣）一律當成 fact：課本把它放在待判斷的句子中，要看內容是否可查證。
高雄七賢段考把 RC1 改成克漏字（am interested in / has become / touched…），會考閱讀也常考主旨、事實意見判斷。
