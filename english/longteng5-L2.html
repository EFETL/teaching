課文速覽（三大重點）

📰 什麼是假新聞

假新聞＝看似真實、卻含錯誤或誤導訊息的新聞。問題與新聞業一樣古老，但網路與社群讓製造、散播更快更廣。人們常不查證來源就信以為真。

🔍 兩種來源＋真實案例

兩大類型：(1) 草率新聞（未查證就發）；(2) 蓄意造假（為政治/社會目的）。案例：1998 年 MMR 疫苗假研究重創接種率；2018 年台灣衛生紙之亂引發搶購。

🧠 如何辨識與自保

看報導有無記者署名、是否引用多個一手來源、發表平台是否可信（BBC/CNN）、用語是否客觀。以批判思考＋查證，才能不落入假新聞陷阱。

📰 一｜什麼是假新聞（定義與現況）
▶Fake news can be defined as news containing false or misleading information that appears truthful.
假新聞可以定義為：含有錯誤或誤導性資訊、卻看起來真實的新聞。

define A as B（被動 A be defined as B）；containing... 現在分詞修飾 news；misleading（誤導的）、truthful（真實的）為重點字。
▶Although fake news has long been in existence and this issue is as old as the news industry itself, the Internet and social media have made creating and sharing fake news easier and faster than ever.
儘管假新聞早已存在、這個問題和新聞業本身一樣古老，網路與社群媒體卻讓製造和散播假新聞比以往更容易、更快速。

make + O + OC（比較級）；as old as = 和…一樣古老；than ever = 比以往。
▶People may be deceived by fake news, believing the content to be genuine without questioning the sources.
人們可能被假新聞欺騙，相信內容是真的，卻不去質疑消息來源。

deceive（欺騙）為核心字；believing... 現在分詞構句（見句型五）；without + V-ing = 沒有…。
▶With so much fake news being spread every day, it is now more important than ever to understand where such news comes from and to question the news stories we read.
由於每天都有這麼多假新聞被散播，如今比以往更重要的是：了解這類新聞從何而來，並質疑我們所讀的新聞報導。

🔍 二｜假新聞的兩種類型
▶Fake news comes in many forms, but two types are the most common.
假新聞有很多形式，但有兩種最為常見。
🔍 句構分析 ①Fake newsS comes inV many formsO but連接詞 two typesS areV the most commonC
come in many forms = 有多種形式；the most common（最高級）。
▶First, fake news can come from sloppy journalism, which occurs when journalists write stories based on unreliable information or without fact-checking their sources prior to publication.
第一，假新聞可能來自草率的新聞作業——也就是記者根據不可靠的資訊、或在發表前未查證來源就寫報導。
🔍 句構分析 ①fake newsS can come fromV sloppy journalismO , which occurs when...非限定關係子句
journalism（新聞業）、journalist（記者）為核心字；prior to = 在…之前（片語）；based on = 根據。
▶Second, some news contains deliberately false information to achieve social or political goals; examples include attempts to influence people to vote for a certain candidate or support a particular cause.
第二，有些新聞為了達成社會或政治目的而蓄意包含不實資訊；例如試圖影響人們投票給某位候選人，或支持某項理念。
🔍 句構分析 ①some newsS containsV deliberately false informationO to achieve social or political goals不定詞（目的）
②examplesS includeV attempts to influence...O
attempt（企圖）、candidate（候選人）、political 為重點字；deliberately = 蓄意地；influence sb. to V = 影響某人做…。
💉 三｜案例一：MMR 疫苗假研究
▶The following example illustrates the potential long-lasting consequences of spreading false information.
以下的例子說明了散播不實資訊可能造成的長遠後果。

illustrate（說明、例證）為核心字；consequences of + V-ing = …的後果；long-lasting = 長久的。
▶In 1998, a British researcher published a small-scale study in a respected medical journal.
1998 年，一位英國研究者在一份受敬重的醫學期刊上發表了一篇小規模研究。

▶The study claimed a link between the MMR vaccine and autism.

▶The media provided extensive coverage of this flawed study, leading to the widespread belief that the vaccine could cause autism.
媒體對這篇有瑕疵的研究進行大量報導，導致大眾普遍相信疫苗會造成自閉症。

▶As a result, public confidence in the vaccine was shattered, and vaccination rates declined.
結果，大眾對疫苗的信心崩潰，接種率也隨之下降。
🔍 句構分析 ①public confidence in the vaccineS was shatteredV（被動）
②vaccination ratesS declinedV
shatter（使破滅、粉碎）為核心字，此處被動 was shattered；as a result = 結果；decline = 下降。
▶However, subsequent studies with larger samples revealed different findings, and the original study was eventually proved inaccurate.
然而，後續以更大樣本進行的研究得出不同結果，原本那篇研究最終被證明並不正確。
🔍 句構分析 ①subsequent studies with larger samplesS revealedV different findingsO
②the original studyS was provedV（被動） inaccurateC
subsequent（隨後的）為核心字；reveal = 揭露；prove + adj. = 證明是…。
▶Despite this, misleading details from the initial study have continued to spread for years.
儘管如此，來自最初那篇研究的誤導性細節仍持續流傳了多年。

despite + N = 儘管（後接名詞，勿接子句）；misleading（誤導的）為重點字。
▶Some parents thus have refused to vaccinate their children for fear that it might do more harm than good.
因此有些父母拒絕為孩子接種，唯恐接種弊大於利。
🔍 句構分析 ①Some parentsS have refusedV to vaccinate their childrenO（不定詞） for fear that it might do more harm than good目的副詞（唯恐）
for fear that + S + might V = 唯恐、以免（見句型三）；do more harm than good = 弊大於利。
▶More than two decades have now passed since the original study was published, but its effects can still be observed worldwide.
自那篇研究發表至今已超過二十年，但它的影響在世界各地仍可見到。

🧻 四｜案例二：台灣衛生紙之亂
▶Another example shows how companies manipulate the news for their own advantage.
另一個例子顯示企業如何為了自身利益操弄新聞。

▶On February 23, 2018, a major hypermarket in Taiwan sent an email to several media outlets, exaggerating an upcoming increase in the price of toilet paper.

▶It was later found that the retailer's true intention in doing so was merely to advertise its own toilet paper promotion, but by noon that day, the damage had already been done.
後來人們發現，該零售商這麼做的真正意圖，只是為了宣傳自家的衛生紙促銷；但到了那天中午，傷害已然造成。

▶Two mainstream media outlets reported on the imminent price hike without fact-checking, and others soon followed suit.

🧠 五｜如何辨識可信的報導
▶These events remind us that we must remain alert to fake news.
這些事件提醒我們，必須對假新聞保持警覺。
🔍 句構分析 ①These eventsS remindV usO that we must remain alert to fake news受詞子句
remind sb. that... = 提醒某人…；remain + adj. = 保持…；alert to = 對…警覺。
▶Whenever we come across new information, it is essential to evaluate its validity so that we do not mistake fake news for the truth.
每當我們接觸到新資訊時，評估它的可信度是很重要的，以免把假新聞當成真相。
🔍 句構分析 ①Whenever we come across new information時間副詞子句 it虛主詞 isV essentialC to evaluate its validity真主詞（不定詞） so that we do not mistake fake news for the truth目的子句
Whenever = 每當（見句型四）；evaluate（評估）、validity（可信度）為核心字；mistake A for B = 把 A 誤認為 B；so that = 以便。
▶One notable feature of reliable reporting is the inclusion of the writer's name, which allows readers to view that writer's biography and verify his or her credibility.
可靠報導的一個顯著特徵，是會標明作者姓名，讓讀者能查看該作者的簡介、驗證其可信度。
🔍 句構分析 ①One notable feature of reliable reportingS isV the inclusion of the writer's nameC , which allows readers to view... and verify...非限定關係子句
notable（顯著的）、biography（傳記）、verify（查證）、credibility（可信度）皆為重點字；allow sb. to V = 讓某人…。
▶Another important sign is whether the article cites multiple primary sources, a practice that supports its trustworthiness.
另一個重要指標是文章是否引用多個一手來源——這種做法能支持其可信度。
🔍 句構分析 ①Another important signS isV whether the article cites multiple primary sourcesC（名詞子句） , a practice that...同位語
cite（引用）為補充字；whether... 名詞子句作補語；primary source = 一手來源；a practice that... 同位語＋關係子句。
▶It is also worth considering where the article was published, since respected media platforms such as the BBC or CNN tend to provide dependable coverage.
文章發表在哪裡也值得考量，因為像 BBC 或 CNN 這類受敬重的媒體平台，往往能提供可靠的報導。
🔍 句構分析 ①It虛主詞 isV also worth considering where the article was publishedC since... dependable coverage原因子句
be worth + V-ing = 值得…；such as = 例如；tend to V = 傾向於；dependable = 可靠的。
▶Last but not least, factual reports are usually written in an objective manner that avoids emotional manipulation and provocative language.
最後但同樣重要的是，事實性的報導通常以客觀的方式書寫，避免情緒操弄與煽動性的語言。
🔍 句構分析 ①Last but not least轉承語 factual reportsS are usually writtenV（被動） in an objective manner [that avoids...]副詞（含關係子句）

▶By paying attention to these features, we can reduce the risk of falling for fake news.
只要留意這些特徵，我們就能降低上假新聞當的風險。
🔍 句構分析 ①By paying attention to these features介系詞片語（方法） weS can reduceV the risk of falling for fake newsO

✅ 六｜結語：對策與行動
▶Useful strategies include thinking critically about what we are reading and conducting further research on news stories to check their content before accepting them as truth.
有用的策略包括：對所讀內容進行批判思考，並在把新聞當成真相之前，進一步查證其內容。
🔍 句構分析 ①Useful strategiesS includeV thinking critically... and conducting further research...O（動名詞並列）
include + V-ing；think critically = 批判思考；accept A as B = 把 A 當成 B。
▶In the current age of news overload, we must all watch out for fake news lest we fall victim to it.
在這個資訊爆炸的時代，我們每個人都必須提防假新聞，以免成為它的受害者。
🔍 句構分析 ①In the current age of news overload副詞 weS must all watch out forV fake newsO lest we fall victim to it目的副詞（以免）
lest + S + (should) V = 以免（見句型三）；watch out for = 提防；fall victim to = 成為…的受害者。
▶With due diligence, we can win the war against fake news and help improve society.
只要盡到應有的謹慎查證，我們就能打贏對抗假新聞這場仗，並協助改善社會。
🔍 句構分析 ①With due diligence介系詞片語 weS can winV the war against fake newsO and help improveV societyO

核心句型文法解析
👆 點擊每個句型標題，展開「公式 + 例句 + 常見錯誤 + 學測重點」。
句型一｜被動語態 be + 過去分詞▼
S + be + p.p. (+ by 行為者)（各時態隨 be 動詞變化）

主詞是「動作承受者」時用被動；本課大量出現。

Fake news can be defined as misleading news.
假新聞可被定義為誤導性新聞。（課文）
Public confidence was shattered. / Its effects can be observed.
大眾信心崩潰。／其影響可被觀察到。（課文）
✗ 及物動詞漏用 be：Public confidence shattered by...（缺 was）。
完成被動：the damage had been done；學測常考時態＋被動組合。
句型二｜虛主詞 it（it is + adj. + to V / that…）▼
It is + adj. + to V ...（真主詞為不定詞） It is worth + V-ing ...

主詞太長時用 it 當虛主詞，真主詞（不定詞/子句）移到後面。

It is essential to evaluate the validity of information.
評估資訊的可信度是很重要的。（課文）
It is also worth considering where the article was published.
文章發表在哪裡也值得考量。（課文）
✗ To evaluate information is essential 雖對，但長主詞英文偏好虛主詞 it。
be worth 後接 V-ing（非 to V）；學測常考。
句型三｜lest / for fear that（以免、唯恐）▼
S + V ..., lest + S + (should) + V原形 ... S + V ..., for fear that + S + might/should + V ...

表「以免、唯恐」的目的（否定意味）；lest 後接原形（should 可省）。

We must watch out for fake news lest we (should) fall victim to it.
我們必須提防假新聞，以免成為受害者。（課文）
Parents refused to vaccinate for fear that it might do harm.
父母拒絕接種，唯恐有害。（課文）
✗ lest 後再加 not（本身已含否定）：lest we do not fall（錯）。
同義：so that... not / in case；學測綜合測驗曾考 lest。
句型四｜Whenever（複合關係副詞，引導副詞子句）▼
Whenever + S + V ..., S + V ...（＝every time / no matter when）

Whenever = 每當、無論何時，引導時間/讓步副詞子句。

Whenever we come across new information, we should evaluate it.
每當接觸到新資訊，我們就該加以評估。（課文）
✗ 與名詞子句的 whatever/whoever 混用位置；Whenever 引導的是副詞子句。
whenever / wherever / however 皆為複合關係副詞；學測常考語意判斷。
句型五｜現在分詞片語作修飾／分詞構句▼
名詞 + V-ing ...（主動修飾名詞） 主句, V-ing ...（分詞構句，表附帶/結果）

V-ing 可後位修飾名詞，或接於主句後表附帶狀況、結果。

news containing false information（修飾 news）
含有不實資訊的新聞（課文）
The media gave it coverage, leading to a false belief.
媒體加以報導，導致錯誤的認知。（課文，表結果）
✗ 懸垂分詞：分詞邏輯主詞須與主句主詞一致。
主動 V-ing、被動 p.p.（information based on... ＝被動）；學測高頻。
