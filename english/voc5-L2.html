本課主題速覽

🟦 規定 vs. 溫情

「我」在麥當勞扮演 Ronald McDonald 到醫院陪伴孩子。公司有明確規定：不能單獨行動、絕不能碰觸孩子，以免傳染細菌。違反規定可能被解雇。

🟩 Billy 的擁抱

癌症病童 Billy 一再要求一個擁抱。「我」在「保住工作」與「不留遺憾」之間掙扎，最後決定打破規定，把 Billy 抱在懷裡，聽他說出心底的擔憂。

🟧 一個改變的差別

兩天後 Billy 過世，媽媽卻來電道謝：Billy 說今年不在乎見不見得到聖誕老人，因為他被 Ronald 抱過了。一個擁抱，made a difference。附對話：Eric 分享暑假當志工。

課文逐句精講（Reading + 翻譯）
👆 點擊任何一句英文，即可展開「中文翻譯 + 句構分析（主詞／動詞／受詞／補語）+ 重點解析」。點 🔊 只朗讀、不展開解析。
🔍 句構顏色對照： 主詞 S　動詞 V　受詞 O　補語 C　修飾語

本課為第一人稱敘事：一位曾在麥當勞扮演 Ronald McDonald 的員工，回憶自己為了病童 Billy 而「打破規定」的那一天。文後附一段對話〈Good Deeds during Summer Vacation〉。

📖 Reading｜The Day I Broke the Rules（我打破規定的那一天）
▶A few years ago, I was employed by McDonald's.
幾年前，我受雇於麥當勞。
🔍 句構分析 ①A few years ago時間 IS was employedV（被動） by McDonald'sby + 動作者
be employed by = 受雇於（被動語態，employ 為核心字）。主動：McDonald's employed me.
▶On "Ronald Day," I would dress up as Ronald McDonald and visit children in community hospitals.
在「Ronald 日」那天，我會打扮成 Ronald McDonald，到社區醫院探望孩子們。
🔍 句構分析 ①On "Ronald Day"時間 IS would dress upV（過去習慣） as Ronald McDonald副詞 and visitV childrenO
would + V = 過去經常（習慣性）；dress up as = 打扮成（某角色）；community（社區）為核心字。
▶I really enjoyed the opportunity to make these children's day a little more joyful.
我真的很享受這個能讓這些孩子的一天更加快樂一點的機會。
🔍 句構分析 ①IS enjoyedV the opportunity to make these children's day more joyfulO（＋不定詞修飾）
the opportunity to V = 做……的機會；make + 受詞 + 形容詞（使役）= 使……變得……；joyful（快樂的）為核心字。
▶However, although I loved my job, it could be emotionally draining occasionally.
然而，雖然我熱愛我的工作，它偶爾也會令人身心俱疲。
🔍 句構分析 ①although I loved my job讓步子句
②itS could beV emotionally drainingC occasionally副詞
although（雖然）引導讓步子句，不與 but 連用；draining = 令人耗盡精力的；occasionally（偶爾）為核心字。
▶There were clear rules that I was required to follow.
有一些我必須遵守的明確規定。
🔍 句構分析 ①There wereV clear rulesS（真主詞） that I was required to follow關係子句
be required to V = 被要求做……（require 為核心字）；that 為關係代名詞，引導修飾 rules 的形容詞子句。
▶First, it was important that I never go anywhere alone while on duty because my costume might surprise or scare little kids.
首先，我值勤時絕不能獨自到任何地方，這一點很重要，因為我的服裝可能會嚇到小孩。
🔍 句構分析 ①it虛主詞 wasV importantC that I never go anywhere alone...真主詞（名詞子句）
important 等「重要／必要」形容詞後的 that 子句用原形動詞（should 省略）：that I never go；while (I was) on duty = 值勤時（on duty 為片語）。
▶Also, it was essential that I never touch the kids since I might give them my germs.
此外，我絕不能碰觸孩子，這也很必要，因為我可能會把細菌傳給他們。
🔍 句構分析 ①it虛主詞 wasV essentialC that I never touch the kids真主詞 since I might give them my germs原因子句
essential（必要的）為核心字，後接 that 子句同樣用原形 touch；since = 因為；germ（細菌）為核心字。
▶I didn't like this regulation because I often wanted to hold their hands or hug them, but I could be fired for violating the rule.
我不喜歡這條規定，因為我常想牽他們的手或抱抱他們，但違反規定可能會讓我被解雇。
🔍 句構分析 ①IS didn't likeV this regulationO
②but IS could be firedV（被動） for violating the rule原因
regulation（規定）、violate（違反）皆為核心字；be fired = 被解雇；for + V-ing = 因為做……。
▶One day, when I had finished my job and was about to leave, I heard a little boy calling, "Ronald, Ronald!"
有一天，當我做完工作正要離開時，我聽到一個小男孩在叫：「Ronald！Ronald！」
🔍 句構分析 ①when I had finished my job and was about to leave時間子句
②IS heardV a little boyO callingOC（現在分詞）
be about to V = 正要、即將（片語）；感官動詞 hear + O + V-ing = 聽到某人正在……；had finished 為過去完成式（比 heard 更早）。
▶He looked only about six, but he was hooked up to more electronic equipment than I had ever seen.
他看起來只有大約六歲，但他身上接的電子儀器比我見過的都還要多。
🔍 句構分析 ①HeS lookedV only about sixC
②heS was hooked up toV（被動） more electronic equipmentO than I had ever seen比較子句
be hooked up to sth. = 連接到（儀器）；electronic（電子的）、equipment（設備，不可數）為核心字。
▶His parents told me that his name was Billy and that he was a cancer victim.
他的父母告訴我，他叫 Billy，是一位癌症患者。
🔍 句構分析 ①His parentsS toldV meO（人） that... and that...O（兩個名詞子句）
tell sb. that + 子句；兩個受詞子句並列時第二個 that 不可省略；victim（受害者、患者）為核心字。
▶I wanted to cheer him up, so I sat down to do some magic tricks for him.
我想讓他開心一點，所以坐下來為他變了一些魔術。
🔍 句構分析 ①IS wantedV to cheer him upO
②so IS sat downV to do some magic tricks不定詞（目的）
cheer sb. up = 使某人振作、開心；trick（把戲、魔術）為核心字；do magic tricks = 變魔術。
▶When I got up to say goodbye, he asked, "Ronald, would you hug me?"
當我起身道別時，他問：「Ronald，你可以抱抱我嗎？」
🔍 句構分析 ①When I got up to say goodbye時間子句 heS askedV "Ronald, would you hug me?"O（引述）
get up to V = 起身去做……；would you...? 為客氣的請求。
▶I really wanted to hug him, but a voice in my head reminded me that I couldn't—I'd lose my job.
我真的很想抱他，但腦中有個聲音提醒我不行——我會丟掉工作。
🔍 句構分析 ①a voice in my headS remindedV meO that I couldn'tO（子句）
remind sb. that + 子句 = 提醒某人……（remind 為核心字）；破折號後補充說明結果；I'd = I would。
▶I told Billy that I wasn't allowed to and suggested that we color a picture instead.
我告訴 Billy 我不被允許這麼做，並建議我們改成一起著色一張畫。
🔍 句構分析 ①IS toldV Billy that I wasn't allowed toO and suggestedV that we color a pictureO
be allowed to V = 被允許（後省略 hug him）；suggest that + S + (should) 原形 V：suggest 後的 that 子句用原形 color；instead = 作為替代。
▶Upon completing our picture, he again asked for a hug.
一畫完我們的圖，他又再次要求一個擁抱。
🔍 句構分析 ①Upon completing our picture介系詞片語（一……就） heS asked forV a hugO
Upon / On + V-ing = 一……就……（= As soon as）；ask for = 要求、請求。
▶Looking into his hopeful eyes, I didn't know what I could say.
望著他充滿期待的眼睛，我不知道該說什麼。
🔍 句構分析 ①Looking into his hopeful eyes分詞構句 IS didn't knowV what I could sayO（名詞子句）
句首 V-ing 分詞構句（= While I was looking...）修飾主詞 I；hopeful（充滿希望的）為核心字。
▶I didn't want to lose my job, but deep down, I knew that I would regret it if I turned down his request.
我不想丟掉工作，但在內心深處，我知道如果我拒絕他的請求，我一定會後悔。
🔍 句構分析 ①IS knewV that I would regret itO if I turned down his request條件子句
deep down = 內心深處；regret（後悔）為核心字；turn down = 拒絕（片語，= reject）。
▶After a hard struggle, I decided to ignore the rule. I picked Billy up and held him in my arms.
經過一番天人交戰後，我決定無視這條規定。我把 Billy 抱起來，緊緊摟在懷裡。
🔍 句構分析 ①After a hard struggle時間 IS decidedV to ignore the ruleO
②IS picked Billy upV（片語動詞） and held him
struggle = 掙扎；decide to V = 決定；pick up = 抱起；代名詞受詞 Billy/him 夾在 pick 與 up 之間。
▶For the next hour, I listened to him talking about all his concerns.
接下來的一個小時，我聽著他訴說所有的擔憂。
🔍 句構分析 ①For the next hour時間 IS listened toV himO talking about all his concernsOC（現在分詞）
listen to sb. V-ing = 聽某人正在……；concern（擔憂）為核心字，此處為名詞。
▶He was afraid that if he died, his little brother might get lost while walking home from school alone.
他害怕如果自己死了，弟弟獨自走路放學回家時可能會迷路。
🔍 句構分析 ①HeS was afraidV that... his little brother might get lostO（子句）
be afraid that + 子句 = 害怕……；get lost = 迷路；while walking = while (he was) walking（副詞子句省略主詞與 be）。
▶Also, he was scared that his dog might go hungry because he had hidden all its bones.
此外，他也擔心他的狗會餓肚子，因為他把所有的骨頭都藏起來了。
🔍 句構分析 ①heS was scaredV that his dog might go hungryO because he had hidden all its bones原因子句
go + 形容詞（go hungry 餓肚子）表「變成某狀態」；had hidden 為過去完成式（藏在「擔心」之前）。
▶Before leaving the hospital that day, I gave Billy's parents my phone number and told them to call me if there was anything I could do.
那天離開醫院前，我把電話號碼給了 Billy 的父母，並告訴他們若有任何我能幫忙的事就打給我。
🔍 句構分析 ①Before leaving the hospital介片 IS gaveV Billy's parents my phone numberO+O and toldV them to call meO+OC
give sb. sth.（雙受詞）；tell sb. to V = 叫某人做……；if there was anything (that) I could do。
▶Two days later, I received a call from Billy's mom, but it was bad news—Billy had passed away.
兩天後，我接到 Billy 媽媽的電話，但那是壞消息——Billy 已經過世了。
🔍 句構分析 ①IS receivedV a call from Billy's momO
②but it was bad newsC
receive（收到）為核心字；pass away = 過世（die 的委婉說法）；had passed away 為過去完成式。
▶She wanted to thank me for making a difference in his life because Billy had said to her, "Mommy, I don't care anymore about seeing Santa this year because I was held by Ronald McDonald!"
她想謝謝我為他的生命帶來改變，因為 Billy 曾對她說：「媽咪，我今年不再在意能不能見到聖誕老人了，因為我被 Ronald McDonald 抱過了！」
🔍 句構分析 ①SheS wantedV to thank me for making a differenceO because Billy had said...原因子句
thank sb. for + V-ing = 為……感謝某人；make a difference = 帶來改變、有影響（片語）；I was held = 被動（我被抱）。
💬 Dialogue｜Good Deeds during Summer Vacation（暑假的善行）
▶Eric and Albert are talking about their summer plans. Eric shares a meaningful way to pass the time.
Eric 和 Albert 正在討論暑假計畫。Eric 分享了一種有意義的消磨時間方式。
🔍 句構分析 ①Eric and AlbertS are talkingV about their summer plans副詞
a meaningful way to V = 做……的有意義方式；pass the time = 消磨時間。
▶A: Summer is right around the corner. What are you going to do this vacation? B: I have no idea. And you? What are your plans?
A：夏天快到了。這個假期你打算做什麼？ B：我毫無頭緒。你呢？你有什麼計畫？
🔍 句構分析 ①SummerS isV right around the cornerC
right around the corner = 近在眼前、即將到來；have no idea = 毫無頭緒。
▶A: I'm going to be a volunteer at Ronald McDonald House Charities. I did it last summer too! B: You mean Ronald the clown from McDonald's?
A：我要去麥當勞叔叔之家慈善基金會當志工。去年暑假我也做過！ B：你是說麥當勞那個小丑 Ronald 嗎？
🔍 句構分析 ①IS am going to beV（未來） a volunteerC
be going to V = 打算（計畫中的未來）；volunteer = 志工；You mean...? = 你是說……？
▶A: Yes! It's actually a non-profit organization that helps needy children. B: Sounds interesting! So, what do you do as a volunteer?
A：對！它其實是個幫助貧困兒童的非營利組織。 B：聽起來很有趣！那你當志工都做些什麼？
🔍 句構分析 ①ItS isV a non-profit organizationC that helps needy children關係子句
non-profit = 非營利的；needy = 貧困的；Sounds interesting（主詞 It 省略，口語）。
▶A: Usually I dress up in a clown suit and entertain kids by telling jokes or reading stories. B: Cool! Can you put on the costume right now? I want to see!
A：通常我會穿上小丑裝，用講笑話或說故事來娛樂孩子。 B：酷！你現在可以穿上那套服裝嗎？我想看！
🔍 句構分析 ①IS dress upV and entertainV kidsO by telling jokes...方法
by + V-ing = 藉由做……；put on = 穿上（動作）；costume = 服裝。
▶A: Here it is! What do you think? B: That's hilarious! I'm sure the kids love it!
A：在這裡！你覺得如何？ B：太好笑了！我相信孩子們一定很愛！
🔍 句構分析 ①ThatS isV hilariousC
Here it is = 在這裡（介紹物品時用）；hilarious = 非常好笑的；I'm sure (that)... = 我確定……。
▶A: You know—and I'm not joking—you would make a great clown, too. B: I think you're right. Maybe I should also sign up to be a volunteer at McDonald's!
A：你知道嗎——我不是在開玩笑——你也會是個很棒的小丑。 B：我想你說得對。也許我也該報名去麥當勞當志工！
🔍 句構分析 ①youS would makeV a great clownO
make a great clown = 會成為很棒的小丑（make = 成為）；sign up = 報名。

核心句型文法解析
👆 點擊每個句型標題，展開「公式 + 例句 + 常見錯誤 + 統測重點」。
句型一｜It is + 形容詞 + that + S + (should) 原形 V▼
It is essential / important / necessary + that + S + (should) 原形V

表「必要、重要、建議」的形容詞後，that 子句用原形動詞（should 可省略）。

It was essential that I never touch the kids.
我絕不能碰觸孩子，這很必要。（原形 touch）
It is important that everyone be on time.
每個人準時很重要。（be 為原形）
✗ It is essential that he touches...（不可用 touches）；主詞第三人稱也用原形。
同類形容詞：important, necessary, vital。同理 suggest / insist / demand + that + 原形。
句型二｜used to vs. would（過去習慣）▼
would + V原形（過去經常做的動作） used to + V原形（過去的習慣或狀態，現已不再）

課文 "I would dress up as Ronald" 用 would 表過去反覆的動作。

On "Ronald Day," I would dress up and visit children.
在「Ronald 日」我總會打扮並探望孩子。
I used to be shy, but now I love the stage.
我以前很害羞，但現在我愛上舞台。
would 只能表「動作」；表過去「狀態」（如 used to be tall）只能用 used to，不可用 would。
注意區分 be used to + V-ing（習慣於）與 used to + 原形（過去習慣）。
句型三｜Upon / On + V-ing（一……就……）▼
Upon / On + V-ing, S + V ...（＝ As soon as S + V）
Upon completing our picture, he again asked for a hug.
一畫完圖，他又要求一個擁抱。（課文）
= As soon as he completed the picture, he asked for a hug.
同義改寫。
Upon 後接動名詞 V-ing，不接原形；主詞須與主句相同。
統測綜合測驗常考 Upon / On + V-ing 與 As soon as 的互換。
句型四｜感官／使役動詞 + O + V-ing / 原形▼
hear / see / listen to + O + V-ing（正在進行） hear / see + O + 原形V（全程、完整動作）
I heard a little boy calling, "Ronald!"
我聽到一個小男孩正在叫。（強調進行）
I listened to him talking about his concerns.
我聽他訴說擔憂。（課文）
✗ I heard him to call.（感官動詞後不加 to）；用 V-ing 或原形，不用不定詞。
被動時要還原 to：He was heard to call...（主動無 to，被動加 to）。
句型五｜分詞構句（V-ing 置句首修飾主詞）▼
V-ing ..., S + V ...（＝ When/Because/While + S + V）
Looking into his hopeful eyes, I didn't know what to say.
望著他期待的眼神，我不知道該說什麼。（課文）
= While I was looking into his eyes, I didn't know what to say.
還原成副詞子句。
分詞的邏輯主詞須與主句主詞相同；主動用 V-ing、被動用 p.p.（過去分詞）。
統測常考分詞構句與 Felt/Feeling 的辨析（主動用 V-ing）。
