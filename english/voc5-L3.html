本課主題速覽

🟦 Nike｜勝利女神

創辦人 Phil Knight 以希臘神話中「長翅膀的勝利女神 Nike」命名。她跑得飛快、能帶領他人於戰役中獲勝，藉此讓產品對運動員更具吸引力，聯想到運動的卓越。

🟩 Starbucks｜海妖 Siren

商標來自希臘神話的雙尾海妖 siren。星巴克創立於西雅圖大海港，設計師翻書時偶然發現海妖形象。海妖以歌聲吸引水手，正如咖啡香吸引顧客。

🟧 品牌與神話

兩家企業都巧妙地把神話元素轉化成品牌訊息，邀請消費者把產品與神話人物連結，彷彿產品也擁有這些傳奇人物的魔力。附對話：Kimberly 分享入選校隊的好消息。

本課說明兩大品牌 Nike 與 Starbucks 如何借用希臘神話元素設計名稱與商標。文後附一段對話〈Good News at the Park〉。

📖 Reading｜Mythology in Famous Brands（名牌中的神話）
▶Did you know that the brand of shoe you wear and the coffee you drink could be related to characters in Greek mythology?
你知道你穿的鞋子品牌和喝的咖啡，可能都與希臘神話中的人物有關嗎？
🔍 句構分析 ①Did youS knowV that the brand... could be related to charactersO（名詞子句）
brand（品牌）為核心字；be related to = 與……有關；the shoe (that) you wear 為省略關係代名詞的子句。
▶Although those ancient stories may seem very distant from our modern society, the famous companies Nike and Starbucks use elements of mythology in their name and logo, respectively.
雖然那些古老的故事看似離我們的現代社會很遙遠，但知名企業 Nike 與 Starbucks 分別在其名稱和商標中運用了神話元素。
🔍 句構分析 ①Although those ancient stories may seem very distant...讓步子句
②the famous companies Nike and StarbucksS useV elements of mythologyO respectively副詞
ancient（古老的）、distant（遙遠的）、element（元素）、respectively（分別地）皆為核心字；seem + adj. = 看似。
▶Most people worldwide have heard of Nike. However, few know that the company's name can be traced back to Greek mythology.
全世界大多數人都聽過 Nike。然而，很少人知道這家公司的名字可以追溯到希臘神話。
🔍 句構分析 ①Most people worldwideS have heard ofV NikeO
②fewS（很少人） knowV that... can be traced back to...O
hear of = 聽說過；few = 很少（人，表否定）；be traced back to = 追溯到（片語）。
▶Phil Knight, one of the founders, chose the name because Nike was the winged goddess of victory.
創辦人之一 Phil Knight 選了這個名字，因為 Nike 是長著翅膀的勝利女神。
🔍 句構分析 ①Phil KnightS , one of the founders,同位語 choseV the nameO because...原因子句
one of the founders 為同位語補充說明主詞；victory（勝利）為核心字；winged = 有翅膀的；goddess = 女神。
▶She had a reputation as a very fast runner. Also, she had the power to lead others to victory during battle.
她以跑得非常快而聞名。此外，她還擁有在戰役中帶領他人獲勝的力量。
🔍 句構分析 ①SheS hadV a reputation as a very fast runnerO
②sheS hadV the power to lead others to victoryO（＋不定詞）
reputation（名聲）為核心字；have a reputation as = 以……聞名；lead sb. to victory = 帶領某人獲勝。
▶Knight's intention was to make the company's products more appealing to athletes.
Knight 的用意是讓公司的產品對運動員更具吸引力。
🔍 句構分析 ①Knight's intentionS wasV to make the company's products more appealingC（不定詞）
intention（意圖，動詞 intend）為核心字；make + O + adj.（使役）；appealing to = 對……有吸引力；athlete（運動員）為核心字。
▶He hoped that people would associate the company's name with athletic excellence.
他希望人們會把公司的名字與運動的卓越聯想在一起。
🔍 句構分析 ①HeS hopedV that people would associate the name with athletic excellenceO
associate A with B = 把 A 與 B 聯想（associate 為核心字）；excellence（卓越）為核心字，形容詞 excellent；athletic = 運動的。
▶In other words, when customers wear Nike shoes, they will believe that they can conquer competitors as if they were the goddess Nike herself.
換句話說，當顧客穿上 Nike 鞋時，他們會相信自己能征服對手，彷彿自己就是女神 Nike 本人。
🔍 句構分析 ①when customers wear Nike shoes時間子句 theyS will believeV that they can conquer competitorsO as if they were the goddessas if 子句
conquer（征服）、competitor（競爭者）為核心字；as if + 過去式（were） = 彷彿（與現在事實相反，假設語氣）。
▶In regard to Starbucks, it is the company's logo that comes from Greek mythology.
至於 Starbucks，來自希臘神話的是這家公司的商標。
🔍 句構分析 ①In regard to Starbucks副詞 it isthe company's logo強調部分 that comes from Greek mythologythat 子句
in regard to = 關於、至於（片語）；It is X that... 為強調句（分裂句），強調 the company's logo。
▶Most of us have seen it before, but perhaps none of us have ever stopped to wonder why the company chose the twin-tailed creature known as a siren as its logo.
我們大多數人以前都看過它，但或許沒有人曾停下來想過，為什麼這家公司選了那個被稱為海妖的雙尾生物當商標。
🔍 句構分析 ①none of usS have stoppedV to wonder why...不定詞（目的）
stop to V = 停下來去做（stop V-ing 則是停止做）；known as = 被稱為（分詞修飾 creature）；twin-tailed = 雙尾的。
▶Starbucks was founded in Seattle, which is one of the United States' largest seaports. Coffee beans from all over the world arrive at this port.
星巴克創立於西雅圖，那是美國最大的海港之一。來自世界各地的咖啡豆都會運抵這個港口。
🔍 句構分析 ①StarbucksS was foundedV（被動） in Seattle, which is one of the largest seaports關係子句（補述）
be founded = 被創立；, which 為關係代名詞的補述用法（非限定，補充說明 Seattle）；seaport = 海港。
▶While looking through some books about the sea, the designer of the Starbucks logo came across an image of a siren.
在翻閱一些關於大海的書時，星巴克商標的設計師偶然看到一張海妖的圖像。
🔍 句構分析 ①While looking through some books分詞構句（時間） the designerS came acrossV an image of a sirenO
While looking = While (he was) looking（省略主詞與 be）；look through = 翻閱；come across = 偶然發現（皆為片語）。
▶In Greek mythology, these creatures not only were beautiful in appearance but also had heavenly singing voices.
在希臘神話中，這些生物不僅外表美麗，還擁有天籟般的歌聲。
🔍 句構分析 ①these creaturesS not only wereV beautifulC but also hadV heavenly singing voicesO
not only A but also B = 不僅 A 而且 B（對等連接，見句型一）；appearance = 外表；heavenly = 天籟般的。
▶Sirens would sit on the shore singing, and sailors in passing ships would be drawn to their voices.
海妖會坐在岸邊唱歌，而經過船隻上的水手都會被她們的歌聲吸引。
🔍 句構分析 ①SirensS would sitV on the shore singing分詞（伴隨）
②sailorsS would be drawn toV（被動） their voicesO
would 表過去經常；sit ... singing 現在分詞表伴隨動作；be drawn to = 被吸引到（draw = 吸引）。
▶Starbucks hopes that the fragrance of its coffee will attract customers to its cafés in the same way.
星巴克希望咖啡的香氣能以同樣的方式吸引顧客走進它的咖啡館。
🔍 句構分析 ①StarbucksS hopesV that the fragrance will attract customersO（名詞子句）
fragrance（香氣）為核心字；attract A to B = 把 A 吸引到 B；in the same way = 以同樣的方式。
▶As you can see, Nike and Starbucks are two examples of businesses which were inspired by mythology when creating their brands.
如你所見，Nike 和 Starbucks 就是兩個在打造品牌時受到神話啟發的企業例子。
🔍 句構分析 ①As you can see副詞子句 Nike and StarbucksS areV two examples of businesses which were inspired...C（＋關係子句）
be inspired by = 受……啟發；when creating = when (they were) creating（分詞省略）；which 引導修飾 businesses 的子句。
▶They cleverly transformed elements of Greek mythology into messages relevant to their brands.
他們巧妙地把希臘神話的元素轉化成與品牌相關的訊息。
🔍 句構分析 ①TheyS transformedV elements of Greek mythologyO into messages relevant to their brands副詞
transform A into B = 把 A 轉化為 B（transform 為核心字）；relevant to = 與……相關（relevant 為核心字）。
▶By doing so, they invite consumers to make connections between their products and mythological beings. They want consumers to look at their products as if they shared the magical qualities of these famous figures.
藉此，他們邀請消費者把產品與神話人物連結起來。他們希望消費者看待這些產品時，彷彿產品也擁有這些著名人物的魔力。
🔍 句構分析 ①By doing so方法 theyS inviteV consumers to make connectionsO+OC
②They want consumers to look at their products as if they shared magical qualitiesas if 子句
invite / want sb. to V（受詞補語用不定詞）；make connections between A and B；as if + 過去式（shared） = 彷彿；magical（有魔力的）為核心字。
💬 Dialogue｜Good News at the Park（公園裡的好消息）
▶Kimberly is practicing soccer in the park. She runs into her friend Kristin and shares some good news with her.
Kimberly 正在公園練足球。她巧遇朋友 Kristin，並和她分享一些好消息。
🔍 句構分析 ①SheS runs intoV her friend KristinO
run into sb. = 巧遇某人（= come across）；share sth. with sb. = 與某人分享。
▶A: Hey, Kimberly! What are you up to? B: I'm practicing my soccer skills. I have to improve as quickly as possible!
A：嘿，Kimberly！你在忙什麼？ B：我在練足球技巧。我得盡快進步！
🔍 句構分析 ①IS have to improveV as quickly as possible副詞
What are you up to? = 你在忙什麼？as + 副詞 + as possible = 盡可能地……。
▶A: Why is that? B: Well, I just found out that I made it onto our school soccer team.
A：為什麼呢？ B：嗯，我剛得知我入選了我們學校的足球隊。
🔍 句構分析 ①IS found outV that I made it onto our teamO
find out = 查明、得知；make it onto the team = 成功入選隊伍。
▶A: Congratulations on achieving your goal! Can I join you for a while? I can help you practice. B: Sure! Actually, could you do me a favor first?
A：恭喜你達成目標！我可以加入你一會兒嗎？我可以幫你練習。 B：當然！其實，你可以先幫我一個忙嗎？
🔍 句構分析 ①Congratulations on achieving your goal介片
congratulate sb. on sth. / Congratulations on + V-ing = 恭喜；do sb. a favor = 幫某人一個忙。
▶A: Of course. What is it? B: I would like you to take a few pictures of me kicking the ball. I want to create a social media post to share the good news with my friends.
A：當然。是什麼忙？ B：我想請你幫我拍幾張踢球的照片。我想發一則社群媒體貼文，跟朋友分享這個好消息。
🔍 句構分析 ①IS would likeV you to take a few picturesO+OC
would like sb. to V = 想請某人做……；pictures of me kicking = 我踢球的照片（動名詞 me kicking）。
▶A: OK. I've got my camera app on. Now pose! B: (After a while...) Take a look and see what you think.
A：好。我已經開好相機 App 了。擺姿勢吧！ B：（過了一會兒……）看一下，說說你覺得如何。
🔍 句構分析 ①TakeV（祈使） a lookO and see what you think
pose = 擺姿勢；take a look = 看一下；see what you think = 看看你覺得怎樣。
▶A: Hmm...My eyes are closed in that one. Could you take another? B: Of course. Ready...set...smile!
A：嗯……那張我眼睛閉起來了。可以再拍一張嗎？ B：當然。預備……準備……笑一個！
🔍 句構分析 ①My eyesS are closedV（被動狀態） in that one副詞
take another (picture) = 再拍一張；Ready, set, smile! 為拍照口令（原為賽跑 Ready, set, go!）。

核心句型文法解析
👆 點擊每個句型標題，展開「公式 + 例句 + 常見錯誤 + 統測重點」。
句型一｜not only A but also B（不僅…而且…）▼
S + not only + V/adj. ... but also + V/adj. ... （A、B 詞性與結構須對等）
These creatures not only were beautiful but also had heavenly voices.
這些生物不僅美麗，還有天籟般的歌聲。（課文）
✗ not only...but also 連接的兩部分結構不對等；also 不可省略時勿改成 and。
句首倒裝：Not only were they beautiful, but they also had lovely voices.（Not only 置句首要倒裝）
句型二｜as if / as though + 過去式（假設語氣）▼
S + V(現在) + as if + S + 過去式 / were（與現在事實相反）
They believe they can conquer competitors as if they were the goddess.
他們相信能征服對手，彷彿自己就是女神。（課文）
They look at products as if they shared magical qualities.
他們看待產品，彷彿產品擁有魔力。（課文）
as if 表「與事實相反」時用過去式；be 動詞一律用 were（✗ was）。
as if + 過去完成式（had + p.p.）則表「與過去事實相反」。
句型三｜強調句（分裂句）It is ... that ...▼
It is / was + 被強調部分 + that (who) + 其餘句子
It is the company's logo that comes from Greek mythology.
來自希臘神話的正是這家公司的商標。（課文）
原句：The company's logo comes from Greek mythology.
強調主詞 the logo。
強調人可用 who；被強調部分若為時間/地方，仍用 that（勿用 when/where）。
與虛主詞 It is + adj. + that 不同：強調句拿掉 It is...that 仍是完整句子。
句型四｜關係代名詞補述用法（, which / , who）▼
先行詞 + , which / who + 補充說明（非限定）
Starbucks was founded in Seattle, which is one of the largest seaports.
星巴克創立於西雅圖，那是最大的海港之一。（課文）
補述用法前面要加逗號，且不可用 that（✗ Seattle, that is...）。
, which 可代替前面「整個句子」：She passed, which surprised everyone.
句型五｜分詞構句（時間副詞子句省略）While + V-ing▼
While / When + V-ing ..., S + V ...（＝ While S + was V-ing）
While looking through some books, the designer came across a siren.
翻書時，設計師偶然看到海妖。（課文）
= While he was looking through some books, the designer...
還原副詞子句。
省略時主詞須與主句相同；主動用 V-ing、被動用 being + p.p. 或直接 p.p.。
此結構常保留 While / When / After 使語意更清楚，統測綜測常考。
