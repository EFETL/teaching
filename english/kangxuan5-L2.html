🟩 Reading｜Food Delivery Apps Make Life Easier

在美國，每五個年輕人就有三個（three out of every five）用外送 App；在台灣，各年齡（age）層的使用者也越來越多。從圖表（chart）看出五大理由（reasons）：First of all 省時間 → Second 方便又有用（helpful），生病或疲累時很有幫助 → Third 常有折扣（discounts） → Fourth 更多選擇（choices） → What’s more 天氣不好時不必出門。In a word，外送 App 不僅更受歡迎，也讓生活更輕鬆。

🟦 Dialogue｜Nick 和 Bella 的下雨天：Yummy Sloth 外送 App

下雨天 Nick 好無聊（bored），Bella 厭倦了（tired of）坐在沙發（couch）上無所事事，冰箱又沒吃的。Nick 想試朋友推薦的新外送 App Yummy Sloth，Bella 說：「我不介意給它一次機會（I don’t mind giving it a chance）。」新用戶打五折（50% discount）！Bella 點了雞肉堡，App 顯示約 30 分鐘。結果 30 分鐘後 Nick 查看位置（location）才發現——他根本沒按（tap）「Place Order」按鈕（button），只好再等 30 分鐘！

🟧 Grammar｜情緒形容詞＋that 子句＋轉述句

課本 Grammar 1：-ing（事物令人…）vs -ed（人感到…），be bored with / excited about / interested in / surprised at。Grammar 2：think / believe / know + (that) 子句；He said (that)… / Tom told us (that)… 轉述時態往前推（is → was、will → would）。段考另加 mind + V-ing、not only…but also、動名詞主詞配單數動詞。

📗 Reading｜Food Delivery Apps Make Life Easier
▶With so many food delivery apps out there, who wants to cook every day?
外面有這麼多外送 App，誰還想每天煮飯呢？
🔍 句構分析 ①With so many food delivery apps out there附帶狀況 whoS wants to cookV（want to V） every day修飾語
delivery（運送；外送）是本課段考最高頻字（24 校）。who wants to…? 是修辭問句，意思是「沒有人想」。
▶In the USA, three out of every five young people use food delivery apps.
在美國，每五個年輕人中就有三個使用外送 App。
🔍 句構分析 ①In the USA修飾語 three out of every five young people比例主詞 useV（複數） food delivery appsO
three out of every five＝每五個中有三個（屏東中正、鳳甲考過）；主詞是 people（複數）→ use。
▶In Taiwan, that number is growing among people of all ages.
在台灣，這個數字在各年齡層的人之中都在增加。
🔍 句構分析 ①In Taiwan修飾語 that numberS is growingV（現在進行式） among people of all ages在……之中
among（在……之中，三者以上）vs between（兩者之間）。age（年齡）：people of all ages＝各年齡層的人。
▶From the chart on the next page, we can see that people are interested in these apps for several reasons.
從下一頁的圖表，我們可以看出人們對這些 App 感興趣有幾個原因。
🔍 句構分析 ①From the chart on the next page修飾語 weS can seeV that people are interested in these apps for several reasonsO（that 名詞子句）
see that + 子句（Grammar 2 的 that 子句）。be interested in（13 校）。chart（圖表）、reason（原因，20 校）、several（幾個的，12 校）。
▶First of all, using food delivery apps saves time because people do not have to spend time going out to restaurants.
首先，使用外送 App 能節省時間，因為人們不必花時間出門去餐廳。
🔍 句構分析 ①First of all列點轉折語 using food delivery apps動名詞主詞（單數） savesV timeO
②because 子句 peopleS do not have to spendV timeO going out to restaurantsspend 時間＋V-ing
動名詞當主詞 → 單數動詞 saves（新北中正、江翠考）。spend + 時間 + V-ing＝花時間做……。First of all＝首先（Reading Skills 列點轉折語）。
▶They can just sit comfortably at their homes or offices and wait for their delicious meals to arrive.
他們只要舒服地坐在家裡或辦公室，等美味的餐點送到就好。
🔍 句構分析 ①TheyS can just sit ... and wait forV（兩個原形動詞） comfortably舒適地 at their homes or offices修飾語 their delicious mealsO to arrive不定詞補語
wait for + 人／物 + to V＝等……來做……。comfortably 是 comfortable 的副詞。
▶Second, many people think that food delivery apps are convenient and helpful.
第二，很多人認為外送 App 既方便又有用。
🔍 句構分析 ①Second列點轉折語 many peopleS thinkV that food delivery apps are convenient and helpfulO（that 名詞子句）
think that + 子句（課本 Grammar 2 A）。helpful（有用的，11 校）、convenient（方便的）。
▶With them, people do not need to cook. That can really help when people are sick or tired.
有了它們，人們就不用煮飯。當人們生病或疲累時，這真的很有幫助。
🔍 句構分析 ①With them有了它們 peopleS do not need to cookV
②ThatS can really helpV
③when 子句 peopleS areV sick or tiredC（主詞補語）
tired（疲勞的）是本課單字；比較 tiring（令人疲倦的）。need to V＝需要做。
▶Third, delivery apps usually offer discounts. They can help people save money.
第三，外送 App 通常會提供折扣，能幫人們省錢。
🔍 句構分析 ①Third修飾語 delivery appsS usually修飾語 offerV discountsO
②TheyS can helpV peopleO save moneyhelp＋人＋原形V
offer（提供）、discount（折扣，19 校）。help + 人 + (to) V。
▶Fourth, food delivery apps offer users more choices.
第四，外送 App 提供使用者更多選擇。
🔍 句構分析 ①Fourth修飾語 food delivery appsS offerV users間接受詞 more choices直接受詞
offer + 人 + 物 雙受詞。choice（選擇，名詞，20 校）← choose（動詞），易混 chance。
▶They let people choose their favorite types of food and restaurants.
它們讓人們選擇自己最喜歡的食物種類和餐廳。
🔍 句構分析 ①TheyS let使役動詞 peopleO choose their favorite types of food and restaurants原形動詞補語
let + 人 + 原形 V（使役動詞，江翠、桃園大有考 have/let/make + 原形）。
▶What’s more, some users use food delivery apps when the weather is bad.
此外，有些使用者在天氣不好時使用外送 App。
🔍 句構分析 ①What’s more此外 some usersS useV food delivery appsO
②when 子句 the weatherS isV badC（主詞補語）
What’s more＝此外、而且，用來補充下一點。
▶With them, people can enjoy their favorite food without going out.
有了它們，人們不用出門就能享用最愛的食物。
🔍 句構分析 ①With them修飾語 peopleS can enjoyV their favorite foodO without going out介系詞＋V-ing
without + V-ing：介系詞後面接動名詞。
▶In a word, food delivery apps have not only become more popular but also made our lives easier.
總之，外送 App 不僅變得更受歡迎，也讓我們的生活更輕鬆。
🔍 句構分析 ①In a word總之 food delivery appsS have not only become ... but also madeV（現在完成式，對稱 p.p.） more popularC（主詞補語） our livesO easier受詞補語
not only A but also B 前後對稱：have become … (have) made（宜蘭中華、屏東中正、新北中正、永和都考這句）。make + 受詞 + 形容詞。In a word＝總之。
💬 Dialogue｜Nick 與 Bella（at home on a rainy day）
▶NickI don’t like rainy days. I’m bored.
我不喜歡下雨天。我好無聊。
🔍 句構分析 ①IS don’t likeV rainy daysO
②IS ’mV bored人感到無聊
人「感到」無聊 → bored（✗ I’m boring＝我是個無趣的人）。bored 康軒 15 校考過。
▶BellaYeah, I’m tired of sitting here on the couch and doing nothing.
對啊，我厭倦了坐在沙發上什麼事都不做。
🔍 句構分析 ①Yeah修飾語 IS ’m tired ofbe＋adj.＋介系詞 sitting here on the couch and doing nothingO（兩個動名詞對等）

▶BellaAlso, I’m hungry, but I don’t think there’s any food in the fridge.
而且我餓了，但我想冰箱裡沒有任何食物。
🔍 句構分析 ①Also修飾語 IS ’mV hungryC（主詞補語）
②but修飾語 IS don’t thinkV there’s any food in the fridgeO（省略 that 的名詞子句）
I don’t think (that) …：英文習慣把否定放在 think 上（＝我想冰箱沒有吃的）。any 用於否定句。
▶NickWait. Yesterday, a friend told me about a new food delivery app, Yummy Sloth. Do you want to try it?
等等。昨天有個朋友跟我說了一個新的外送 App，叫「Yummy Sloth」。你想試試看嗎？
🔍 句構分析 ①Wait祈使句
②Yesterday修飾語 a friendS toldV meO about a new food delivery app, Yummy Sloth同位語
③Do ... want to tryV youS itO
tell + 人 + about + 事。Yummy Sloth（好吃樹懶）＝App 名稱，和 a new food delivery app 是同位語。
▶BellaSure, I’ve always had an interest in new things. I don’t mind giving it a chance.
當然，我一直對新事物很有興趣。我不介意給它一次機會。
🔍 句構分析 ①Sure修飾語 IS ’ve always hadV（現在完成式・持續） an interest in new thingsO
②IS don’t mindV giving it a chanceO（動名詞）
have an interest in＝對……有興趣（interest 當名詞）。mind + V-ing（12 校考；台中向上考原句 I don’t mind giving it a chance）。chance（機會，13 校）。
▶Nick(Nick is downloading the app onto his smartphone.) Guess what? New users get a 50% discount!
（Nick 正在把 App 下載到智慧型手機上。）你猜怎樣？新用戶可以打五折！
🔍 句構分析 ①NickS is downloadingV（現在進行式） the appO onto his smartphone修飾語
②Guess祈使句 whatO
③New usersS getV a 50% discountO
download（下載，18 校）。Guess what?＝你猜怎麼著（要宣布消息）。a 50% discount＝打五折（a 20% discount＝打八折）。
▶BellaWhat a surprise! What are we waiting for?
真是驚喜！我們還在等什麼？
🔍 句構分析 ①What a surprise感嘆句（What＋a＋名詞）
②WhatO are ... waiting forV（現在進行式） weS
What + a/an + (adj.) + 名詞! 感嘆句；surprise 在這裡是名詞「驚喜」。What are we waiting for?＝快點吧！
▶NickHey, you’re going to love this. Your favorite fast food restaurant is on the list.
嘿，你一定會喜歡這個。你最愛的速食店在清單上。
🔍 句構分析 ①youS ’re going to loveV（未來式） thisO
②Your favorite fast food restaurantS isV on the list地方
list（清單）是本課單字；on the list＝在清單上。
▶BellaExcellent! I’d like a chicken burger.
太棒了！我要一個雞肉漢堡。
🔍 句構分析 ①Excellent感嘆
②IS ’d (would) likeV a chicken burgerO
點餐用語 I’d like + 名詞 / to V。
▶NickGot it.
知道了。
🔍 句構分析 ①Got it省略句（＝I got it）
Got it.＝我知道了／收到。
▶BellaHow long will it take for our meals to get here?
我們的餐點要多久才會送到這裡？
🔍 句構分析 ①How long多久 will ... takeV it虛主詞 for our meals修飾語 to get here真主詞
How long will it take (for + 物) + to V?＝要花多久才……？（統計：How long will it take for our meal to get here?）
▶NickThe app shows it’ll take about 30 minutes.
App 顯示大約要 30 分鐘。
🔍 句構分析 ①The appS showsV it’ll take about 30 minutesO（省略 that 的名詞子句）
show (that) + 子句。it’ll＝it will。
▶BellaThat’s fine. At least we don’t have to go out in the rain.
沒關係。至少我們不必冒雨出門。
🔍 句構分析 ①ThatS ’sV fineC（主詞補語）
②At least至少 weS don’t have to go outV in the rain修飾語

▶NickI agree.
我同意。
🔍 句構分析 ①IS agreeV
表示同意：I agree. / I couldn’t agree more.（課本 Oral Practice）
▶Bella(30 minutes later) Where are our meals? I’m as hungry as a horse, and if I don’t eat soon, I’ll be very angry.

核心句型文法解析
👆 點擊每個句型標題，展開「公式 + 例句 + 常見錯誤 + 會考／段考重點」。
句型一｜課本 Grammar 1：情緒形容詞 -ing（令人…的）vs -ed（感到…的）▼
事物 + be + V-ing (+ to 人) The idea is boring / exciting / interesting / surprising (to me). 人 + be + V-ed + 介系詞 + 事物 I am bored with / excited about / interested in / surprised at the idea. 其他：tiring / tired（of）　scary / scared（of）（-y 結尾！）

判斷關鍵：主詞是「感受者（人）」→ -ed；主詞是「引起感受的事物」→ -ing。人也可以用 -ing，表示「這個人讓別人覺得…」（He is boring.＝他很無趣）。

The joke is interesting to Ben. / Ben is interested in the joke.
這笑話讓 Ben 覺得有趣。／Ben 對這笑話感興趣。（課本 Example）
It’s a boring day. I am bored. / Climbing all these stairs is tiring. I am tired.
真是無聊的一天，我好無聊。／爬樓梯好累人，我好累。（課本 Word Bank）
I don’t like rainy days. I’m bored. / I’m tired of sitting here on the couch.
我不喜歡下雨天，我好無聊。／我厭倦坐在沙發上。（課文）
✗ I’m boring.（想說「我覺得無聊」→ I’m bored）✗ The movie is excited.（→ exciting）✗ scaring（「嚇人的」是 scary）✗ be interested on（→ in）
康軒 30 校、101 題考情緒形容詞，和現在完成式並列第一名考點。課本漫畫〈龜兔賽跑〉：The race was exciting to the hare. / The tortoise was surprised at the hare’s words.
句型二｜情緒形容詞 + 介系詞 / to V / that 子句▼
be surprised at + N be surprised to + V be surprised that + 子句 be tired of + N/V-ing be bored with + N/V-ing be interested in + N/V-ing be excited about + N be scared of + N be worried that + 子句 It is + surprising/exciting… + that + 子句

情緒形容詞後面接「原因」有三種方式：介系詞＋名詞/動名詞、to＋原形、that＋完整句子。It is + adj. + that… 的 It 是虛主詞，代替後面的 that 子句。

What a surprise! / It is surprising that the woman tipped me.
真是驚喜！／那位女士給我小費真令人驚訝。（課文／課本 Word Bank）
I was surprised to find that she was my old neighbor.
我很驚訝地發現她是我以前的鄰居。（新北中正原型）
The tortoise was surprised at the hare’s words.
烏龜對兔子的話感到驚訝。（課本 Read and Write）
✗ I’m surprised the news.（→ surprised at the news）✗ be interested on（→ in）✗ be tired of to sit（of 後面接 V-ing）
康軒 15 校考 be + adj. + that。江翠：I am surprised to know from the newspaper that …（when / how / it’s 為誘答）。
句型三｜課本 Grammar 2A：think / believe / know + (that) 子句▼
S + think / believe / know / hope + (that) + S + V … （that 引導名詞子句當受詞，that 可省略） I don’t think (that) … ＝ 我想……不……（否定放在 think） That + 子句 + 單數動詞 …（that 子句當主詞時 that 不能省略）

that 子句是一個「完整句子」當動詞的受詞。英文說「我想……沒有」時，習慣否定 think：I don’t think there’s any food.

Many people think that food delivery apps are convenient and helpful.
很多人認為外送 App 方便又有用。（課文）
I don’t think there’s any food in the fridge.
我想冰箱裡沒有任何食物。（課文）
We think (that) the car race is exciting. / I know Kevin likes the trip to the USA.
我們認為賽車很刺激。／我知道 Kevin 喜歡美國之旅。（課本 Say and Write）
✗ I think what the app is useful.（完整句子用 that）✗ I think that there isn’t any food.（較自然：I don’t think there’s any food）
康軒 22 校考動詞 + that 子句。課本漫畫：Well, I think that you’re lying.（lie 說謊）
句型四｜課本 Grammar 2B：轉述句 He said (that)… / Tom told us (that)…（時態往前推）▼
He said, "The game is exciting." → He said (that) the game was exciting. Tom told us, "I want to go home." → Tom told us (that) he wanted to go home. Tom told us, "I will see you later." → Tom told us (that) he would see us later. 主要子句過去式 → 子句 is→was、want→wanted、will→would、can→could；人稱也要換

轉述別人說的話時，說話的動作已經過去（said / told），所以子句時態「往過去推一格」，代名詞也要跟著換（I → he、you → us）。say 後面不直接接人；tell + 人 + (that)…。

Marie said (that) her English was good.
Marie 說她的英文很好。（課本 Example）
Mike said (that) he planned to take a trip to the USA.
Mike 說他計畫去美國旅行。（課本 Say and Write）
Jane told me (that) she could go to the movies with me.
Jane 告訴我她可以跟我去看電影。（課本 Say and Write）
✗ Lily said she will go to a movie with me.（said → would）✗ He said me that…（→ He told me that… / He said to me that…）

句型五｜mind + V-ing、動名詞主詞、not only…but also（段考補充）▼
S + don’t / doesn’t mind + V-ing I don’t mind giving it a chance. Would you mind + V-ing? → Not at all.（不介意＝答應） V-ing … + 單數動詞 Using food delivery apps saves time. S + have not only + p.p.1 + but also + p.p.2（前後對稱）

課文裡三個段考愛考的句子：mind giving（mind 後只接 V-ing）、using … saves（動名詞主詞→單數）、have not only become … but also made（對稱）。

I don’t mind giving it a chance.
我不介意給它一次機會。（課文）
First of all, using food delivery apps saves time.
首先，使用外送 App 能節省時間。（課文）
Food delivery apps have not only become more popular but also made our lives easier.
外送 App 不僅更受歡迎，也讓生活更輕鬆。（課文）
✗ Would you mind to close the door?（→ closing）✗ Would you mind…? — Yes, sure.（語意變「介意」）✗ have not only become popular but also changes…（→ changed）
