主題速覽（三幕）

🌊 第一幕｜洪水下的失學危機

孟加拉位於南亞，每年季風（monsoon）帶來豪雨，約三分之一國土淹水。道路被淹、上千所學校一停課就是好幾個月，許多孩子因此失去受教機會。

🚤 第二幕｜船上學校的設計

建築師 Mohammed Rezwan 於 2002 年創辦船上學校，以傳統木船 noka 為設計基礎；船頂有防風雨屋頂與拱形金屬樑，靠太陽能板供電，教室裡有黑板與連網電腦。每艘船一次接送約 30 名學生，上課 3 小時後再開往下一個村子。

🌱 第三幕｜帶來的改變

學生學習生物多樣性、水污染等環境議題，放學領取太陽能燈夜讀；晚上船校在帆布上播放教育影片，並教大人營養衛生、人權、抗洪作物與理財，提高生產力與收入。柬埔寨、奈及利亞、菲律賓等易淹水國家也仿效此模式。

本課介紹孟加拉的「船上學校」如何讓孩子在洪水季節照常上學。以下摘要以教師自行改寫的英文呈現（非課文原句），依「洪水下的失學危機／船上學校的設計／帶來的改變」三個重點分段。

🌊 第一幕｜洪水下的失學危機（Floods and Lost Schooling）
▶Every monsoon season, heavy rain floods roughly a third of Bangladesh, a low-lying nation in South Asia.
每到季風季節，豪雨就會淹沒孟加拉約三分之一的土地；孟加拉是南亞一個地勢低窪的國家。
🔍 句構分析 ①Every monsoon season時間副詞 heavy rainS floodsV roughly a third of BangladeshO a low-lying nation in South Asia同位語
flood 在此作及物動詞「淹沒」（核心字）；a third = one third（三分之一，分子用基數、分母用序數）；逗號後的名詞片語為同位語，補充說明 Bangladesh。
▶When roads disappear under water, thousands of schools have no choice but to close for months.
當道路消失在水面下，上千所學校別無選擇，只能停課好幾個月。
🔍 句構分析 ①When roads disappear under water時間副詞子句
②thousands of schoolsS haveV no choice but to closeO（固定句型） for months副詞
have no choice but to V = 不得不…；thousands of + 複數名詞 = 數以千計的；for months 與課文片語 for months at a time（一連好幾個月）相呼應。
▶Consequently, the floods cause countless children to lose precious months of learning.
結果，洪水使無數孩子失去寶貴的好幾個月學習時光。
🔍 句構分析 ①Consequently轉折副詞 the floodsS causeV countless childrenO to lose precious months of learningC（不定詞作受詞補語）
cause + O + to V = 導致…去做（見句型一）；consequently ≈ as a result（因此）；countless = 無數的。
▶Believing that learning should not stop when the water rises, architect Mohammed Rezwan came up with a promising plan: schools that float.
建築師 Mohammed Rezwan 深信學習不該因水位上漲而中斷，於是想出一個很有前景的計畫：會漂浮的學校。
🔍 句構分析 ①Believing that learning should not stop when the water rises分詞構句（主動，表原因）
②architect Mohammed RezwanS came up withV（片語） a promising planO : schools that float同位語（冒號補充說明）
come up with = 想出（點子、辦法）；promising（有前景的）為核心字；Believing... 分詞構句的意義主詞與主句主詞 Rezwan 相同。
▶Since he started the program in 2002, his boats have allowed young pupils to keep studying all year round, rain or shine.
自從他在 2002 年展開這項計畫，他的船就讓年幼的學生不論晴雨都能全年持續學習。
🔍 句構分析 ①Since he started the program in 2002時間副詞子句
②his boatsS have allowedV（現在完成式） young pupilsO to keep studyingC（不定詞） all year round, rain or shine副詞
since + 過去時間，主要子句用現在完成式；allow + O + to V 與 cause 同句型；all year round = 一整年；rain or shine = 無論晴雨。
🚤 第二幕｜船上學校的設計（Designing a Floating Classroom）
▶Rezwan based his design on the noka, a traditional wooden boat that local villagers know well.
Rezwan 以 noka 為設計基礎，那是當地村民十分熟悉的傳統木船。
🔍 句構分析 ①RezwanS basedV his designO on the noka副詞 a traditional wooden boat [that local villagers know well]同位語（含限定關係子句）
base A on B = 以 B 為 A 的基礎；改被動即 A is based on B（見句型三）；that 為受格關代，可省略。
▶Above the classroom sits a weatherproof roof whose arched metal frame can stand up to fierce storms.
教室上方是一座防風雨的屋頂，它的拱形金屬骨架禁得起猛烈的暴風雨。
🔍 句構分析 ①Above the classroom地方副詞（倒裝） sitsV a weatherproof roof [whose arched metal frame can stand up to fierce storms]S（含所有格關係子句）
地方副詞置首倒裝：Above... + V + S（見句型四）；arched（拱形的）為核心字；weatherproof = 防風雨的；stand up to = 經得起、承受得住。
▶Without such a sturdy roof, the pounding rain would soon wreck the floating classroom.
要是沒有這麼堅固的屋頂，猛烈的雨水很快就會毀了這間水上教室。
🔍 句構分析 ①Without such a sturdy roof假設條件（= If it were not for...）
②the pounding rainS would soon wreckV（假設語氣） the floating classroomO
Without + N, S + would + 原形V：與現在事實相反的假設（見句型五、六）；sturdy = 堅固的；wreck = 毀壞。
▶Thanks to rooftop solar panels, the boat makes its own electricity, enough to run a laptop that is connected to the Internet.
多虧了屋頂上的太陽能板，這艘船能自己發電，足以讓一台連上網路的筆電運作。
🔍 句構分析 ①Thanks to rooftop solar panels原因副詞片語
②the boatS makesV its own electricityO enough to run a laptop [that is connected to the Internet]形容詞片語（補充說明）
thanks to + N = 多虧…；solar（太陽的）、panel（板）、install（安裝）為核心字；enough to V = 足以…。
▶Six mornings a week, each boat collects up to thirty pupils from muddy riverbanks, holds a three-hour session with them, and then moves on to serve another village.
每週六個早上，每艘船從泥濘的河岸接走最多三十名學生，和他們上三小時的課，接著再開往下一個村莊服務。
🔍 句構分析 ①Six mornings a week時間副詞 each boatS collectsV1 up to thirty pupilsO from muddy riverbanks副詞
②holdsV2 a three-hour sessionO with them副詞
③and then moves onV3 to serve another village不定詞（表目的）
muddy（泥濘的）、session（一段上課時間）為核心字；a three-hour session：「數字-名詞」複合形容詞，名詞用單數；三個動詞共用主詞 each boat（對等連接）。
🌱 第三幕｜帶來的改變（Learning Beyond the Classroom）
▶Beyond reading and math, pupils also explore green issues such as protecting wildlife and keeping rivers clean.
除了閱讀和數學，學生也探索環保議題，例如保護野生動物和維持河川乾淨。
🔍 句構分析 ①Beyond reading and math介系詞片語（除了…） pupilsS also副詞 exploreV green issues [such as protecting wildlife and keeping rivers clean]O（含舉例）
beyond ≈ besides ≈ aside from（除了…之外還有）；such as 後接名詞或動名詞舉例；keep + O + adj. = 使…保持某狀態；相關補充字 biodiversity、pollution。
▶After class, many students carry home cheap solar lanterns, so their studying no longer has to end at sunset.
放學後，許多學生帶著便宜的太陽能燈回家，因此他們的讀書時間不必再隨著日落而結束。
🔍 句構分析 ①After class時間副詞 many studentsS carry homeV cheap solar lanternsO
②so連接詞（表結果） their studyingS（動名詞） no longer has to endV at sunset副詞
no longer = 不再；so 連接結果子句；solar lantern = 太陽能提燈；home 在此為副詞，前面不加 to。
▶The schools serve adults too, offering evening films shown on large sails and practical lessons on health, nutrition, and human rights.
這些學校也服務成人，提供在大帆布上放映的晚間影片，以及健康、營養與人權等實用課程。
🔍 句構分析 ①The schoolsS serveV adultsO too副詞
②offering evening films [shown on large sails] and practical lessons on health, nutrition, and human rights分詞構句（主動，附帶說明）
practical（實用的）、nutrition（營養）、project（投影）為核心字；offering... = and they offer...；船校的受益者不只學童，連大人也包括在內，可用 not only A but also B 表達（見句型七）。
▶Farmers learn to grow flood-resistant crops and handle money wisely, which raises their productivity and gives them sufficient income all year.
農民學會種植抗洪作物並聰明理財，這提高了他們的生產力，也讓他們一整年都有足夠的收入。
🔍 句構分析 ①FarmersS learnV to grow flood-resistant crops and handle money wiselyO（不定詞）
②whichS（非限定關代，指前面整件事） raisesV their productivityO and givesV them間接受詞 sufficient income直接受詞 all year副詞
resistant（抵抗的）、productivity（生產力）、sufficient（足夠的）為核心字；flood-resistant = 耐洪水的；, which 可代替前面整個子句。
▶Today, the model has spread to other flood-prone nations, such as Cambodia, Nigeria, and the Philippines, proving that one bold idea can truly make waves.
如今，這個模式已推廣到其他容易淹水的國家，例如柬埔寨、奈及利亞與菲律賓，證明一個大膽的點子真的能掀起波瀾。
🔍 句構分析 ①Today時間副詞 the modelS has spreadV（現在完成式） to other flood-prone nations, such as Cambodia, Nigeria, and the Philippines副詞（含舉例）
②proving that one bold idea can truly make waves分詞構句（表結果，含 that 名詞子句）
make waves = 引起轟動、掀起波瀾（呼應課名的雙關）；flood-prone ≈ at risk of flooding（易淹水的）；藉此跨越 geographical barriers（地理障礙）。

Turning boats into classrooms is a great example of thinking outside the box.把船變成教室，是跳脫框架思考的絕佳例子。
in the same boat
同病相憐；處境相同
▶
大家面臨同樣的困難或處境。
Don't panic about the hard quiz; we're all in the same boat.別為那份難的小考慌張，大家處境都一樣。
keep one's head above water
勉強撐過去（尤指經濟上）
▶
在困境中勉強維持、不被壓垮，常指收支勉強打平。
Extra income from new crops helps poor families keep their heads above water.新作物帶來的額外收入，幫助貧困家庭勉強撐下去。
weather the storm
度過難關
▶
安然熬過一段艱難的時期。
With help from their neighbors, the villagers weathered the storm.在鄰居的幫助下，村民們度過了難關。
learn the ropes
摸清門道；學會基本訣竅
▶
源自航海（水手要先學會操作纜繩），指熟悉新工作或環境的基本做法。
New students usually learn the ropes within their first week.新生通常在第一週內就能摸清門道。
when it rains, it pours
禍不單行
▶
壞事一來就接二連三地發生。
First my bike broke, then I missed the bus — when it rains, it pours!先是腳踏車壞了，接著又錯過公車——真是禍不單行！

核心句型文法解析
👆 點擊每個句型標題，展開「公式 + 例句 + 常見錯誤 + 學測重點」。
句型一｜cause + O + to V（導致…去做）▼
S + cause + O + to V（同類：allow / enable / force / encourage + O + to V）

cause 後先接受詞，再接不定詞作「受詞補語」，表示「使某人／某物去…」。

The heavy rain caused the bus to arrive an hour late.
豪雨使公車晚了一小時才到站。
A broken bridge can cause students to miss weeks of school.
一座斷橋可能讓學生缺課好幾週。
✗ The storm caused the roads close / closing.（cause + O 之後須接 to V）
make / let / have 為使役動詞，接原形 V；cause / get / allow 接 to V。學測綜合測驗常比較。
句型二｜provide A with B ／ provide B for A（提供）▼
S + provide + 人 + with + 物 S + provide + 物 + for / to + 人

provide 要分清「人」與「物」的位置：人在前用 with，物在前用 for / to。

The library provides students with free Wi-Fi.
圖書館提供學生免費無線網路。
The charity provides warm meals for homeless people.
那個慈善團體為無家者提供熱騰騰的餐點。
✗ The library provides free Wi-Fi with students.（物在前時應用 for / to）
同句型：supply A with B、equip A with B、present A with B。學測句子合併常考。
句型三｜base A on B ／ A be based on B（以…為基礎）▼
S + base + A + on + B（主動） A + be based on + B（被動，最常見）

表示「以 B 為 A 的根據或基礎」；描述作品、設計的來源時多用被動。

This film is based on a famous novel.
這部電影改編自一部知名小說。
Our teacher bases her grades on both tests and homework.
老師依據考試和作業來打成績。
✗ This film is basing on a novel.（電影是「被」改編，應為 is based on）
be based in + 地點 = 總部設在…，例：The company is based in Tainan.
句型四｜地方副詞置首的倒裝（On top of / Above... + V + S）▼
地方副詞(片語) + be / 不及物 V（sit, stand, lie, hang）+ 主詞 S

為了強調位置，把地方副詞片語放句首，接著是動詞，最後才是主詞；動詞須與後面的「真正主詞」一致。

On top of the hill stands an old lighthouse.
山丘頂上矗立著一座老燈塔。
Beside the classroom door hang two colorful posters.
教室門邊掛著兩張彩色海報。
✗ Beside the door hangs two posters.（主詞 two posters 為複數，應用 hang）
主詞若為代名詞則不倒裝：On top of the hill it stands. 學測綜合測驗常考主詞動詞一致。
句型五｜與現在事實相反的假設：If S were..., S would...▼
If + S + were / 過去式V ..., S + would / could / might + 原形V

描述與「現在事實」相反或不太可能的情況；if 子句的 be 動詞不論人稱一律用 were。

If I were a bird, I would fly over the flooded fields.
如果我是一隻鳥，我就會飛越被淹沒的田野。
If our town had a boat school, no child would miss class in the rainy season.
如果我們鎮上有船上學校，雨季時就不會有孩子缺課。
✗ If I was a bird, I will fly...（正式文法 be 動詞用 were；主要子句須用 would + 原形）
可省略 if 改成倒裝：Were I a bird, I would... 學測常考改寫。
句型六｜Without + N = If it were not for + N（要不是…）▼
Without / But for + N, S + would + 原形V = If it were not for + N, S + would + 原形V

用介系詞片語代替 if 子句，表達與現在事實相反的假設；主要子句仍用 would / could + 原形。

Without electricity, we could not charge our phones.
要是沒有電，我們就無法替手機充電。
If it were not for her help, I would feel lost in this new city.
要不是有她的幫忙，我在這個新城市會感到茫然。
✗ If it is not for her help, I would feel lost.（與現在事實相反須用 were）
倒裝：Were it not for + N。與過去事實相反：If it had not been for + N, S would have p.p.
句型七｜not only A but also B（不但…而且…）▼
not only A but (also) B（A、B 詞性須對等） Not only + 助V + S + V ..., but S also V ...（置首須倒裝）

連接兩個對等的成分；Not only 放句首時，第一個子句要用疑問句語序（倒裝）。

The festival attracts not only local people but also foreign visitors.
這個節慶不只吸引當地人，也吸引外國遊客。
Not only did the storm close the roads, but it also cut off the power.
暴風雨不但封閉了道路，還切斷了電力。
✗ Not only the storm closed the roads, but...（置首須倒裝：Not only did the storm close...）
作主詞時動詞與 B 一致：Not only the students but also the teacher is excited.
句型八｜so that S can V ／ so as to V（為了；以便）▼
S + V ..., so that + S + can / may / will + V = S + V ..., so as to + 原形V（主詞須相同）

兩者都表「目的」：so that 後接完整子句，so as to 後接原形動詞。

She saves part of her pay every month so that she can buy a laptop.
她每月存下部分薪水，好買一台筆電。
We left home early so as to catch the first boat.
我們提早出門，以便趕上第一班船。
✗ so as to catching / so that to catch（so as to + 原形；so that + 子句）
否定：so as not to V / in order not to V。學測句子合併常考。
