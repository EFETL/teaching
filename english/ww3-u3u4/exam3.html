二、是非題 True or False（Q11–18）

先讀短文，再判斷句子對不對。對的寫 T，錯的寫 F。

Read 1：Sam finds a book at school. It's not his book. Sam says, "Let's take it to the Lost and Found." His teacher says, "Nice! You are honest."
11.(    ) The book is Sam's.
答：F
12.(    ) Sam takes the book home.
答：F
13.(    ) Sam's teacher says, "Nice!"
答：T
14.(    ) Sam is honest.
答：T
Read 2：Look at Ben's go bag. Water is in the bag. A flashlight is in the bag, too. A radio is not in the bag.
15.(    ) The go bag is Ben's.
答：T
16.(    ) A flashlight is in the go bag.
答：T
17.(    ) A radio is in the go bag.
答：F
18.(    ) Water is not in the go bag.
答：F
