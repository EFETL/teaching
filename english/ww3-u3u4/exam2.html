二、是非題 True or False（Q11–18）

先讀短文，再判斷句子對不對。對的寫 T，錯的寫 F。

Read 1：Mia finds a blue pen. It's not her pen. Her friend Ben says, "It's my pen!" Mia gives the pen to Ben. "Thank you!" says Ben. (give 給)
11.(    ) The pen is Mia's.
答：F
12.(    ) Ben's pen is blue.
答：T
13.(    ) Ben says, "Thank you."
答：T
14.(    ) Mia takes the pen to the Lost and Found.
答：F
Read 2：In Class 3A, the earthquake drill starts. Jello says, "Get under the desk!" The students go under their desks. Boka's schoolbag is on the chair.
15.(    ) The students go under their desks.
答：T
16.(    ) Jello says, "Go to the window!"
答：F
17.(    ) Boka's schoolbag is under the chair.
答：F
18.(    ) It's an earthquake drill.
答：T
