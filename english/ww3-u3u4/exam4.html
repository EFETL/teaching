二、是非題 True or False（Q11–18）

先讀短文，再判斷句子對不對。對的寫 T，錯的寫 F。

Read 1：Kim has a cute duck. The duck sings a tune on a cube. The duck is for the duke. (tune 曲調)
11.(    ) Kim has a duck.
答：T
12.(    ) The duck sings on a cube.
答：T
13.(    ) The duck is for Kim.
答：F
14.(    ) The duck is not cute.
答：F
Read 2：Look at Boka's classroom. A box is on the desk. A cat is in the box. A schoolbag is under the desk.
15.(    ) The box is on the desk.
答：T
16.(    ) The cat is under the desk.
答：F
17.(    ) The schoolbag is under the desk.
答：T
18.(    ) The schoolbag is in the box.
答：F
