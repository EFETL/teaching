🟦 導覽開場｜Rosa 的自由女神導覽

導遊 Rosa 在二十分鐘船程中介紹自由女神像：1886 年法國致贈美國、慶祝美國脫離英國獨立；總高 305 呎、雕像本身 151 呎，約 15 層樓高。

🟩 象徵意義｜為何是綠色？

表面是銅製，接觸空氣氧化由紅棕轉綠。左手平板刻著「July 4, 1776」，右手火炬象徵啟迪世界、指引移民；皇冠七道尖芒代表陽光，破鏈象徵掙脫壓迫的自由。

🟧 訂票對話｜Meg & Andrew 找便宜行程

兩人上網找划算的自由女神行程。「Lady Liberty Tour」含來回船票、導覽、午餐，卻貴得嚇人（cost an arm and a leg）；便宜的船不停島上。於是決定從別處省錢（cut costs）來湊預算。

課文逐句精講（Reading + 翻譯）
👆 點擊任何一句英文，即可展開「中文翻譯 + 句構分析（主詞／動詞／受詞／補語）+ 重點解析」。
🔍 句構顏色對照： 主詞 S　動詞 V　受詞 O　補語 C　修飾語

本課分兩部分：前半是導遊 Rosa 在前往自由女神像的船程中所做的導覽獨白，介紹雕像的由來與各部位象徵；後半是 Meg 與 Andrew 上網訂購自由女神導覽行程的對話。

🟦 Rosa｜Statue of Liberty Tour（導覽 Part 1：由來與外觀）
▶Welcome aboard, everyone! I'm Rosa, your guide for today.
歡迎大家登船！我是 Rosa，今天的導遊。
🔍 句構分析 ①WelcomeV（祈使句） aboard副詞（在船／車上）
②IS amV Rosa, your guide for todayC（主詞補語＋同位語）
aboard（副詞）= 在船／飛機／車上；All aboard! 為登船（車）的招呼語，呼應課名。
▶During our twenty-minute ride to the Statue of Liberty, I am going to tell you what this magnificent monument is all about.
在這趟前往自由女神像、約二十分鐘的船程中，我會告訴各位這座宏偉紀念碑的一切。
🔍 句構分析 ①During our twenty-minute ride to the Statue of Liberty時間副詞 IS am going to tellV youO（間受） what this magnificent monument is all aboutO（直受，名詞子句）
magnificent（宏偉的）、monument（紀念碑）為核心字；what... is all about = ……是怎麼一回事。
▶In 1886, America was given the Statue of Liberty by France to celebrate its independence from the United Kingdom.
1886 年，法國把自由女神像贈予美國，以慶祝美國脫離英國獨立。
🔍 句構分析 ①In 1886時間 AmericaS was givenV（被動） the Statue of LibertyO by France介副（動作者） to celebrate its independence...不定詞（表目的）
被動語態：give 為授與動詞，人當主詞被動＝ was given +物（見句型一）；independence（獨立，n.）為核心字。
▶This gift represented the intimate ties between France and the United States.
這份禮物象徵了法國與美國之間親密的關係。
🔍 句構分析 ①This giftS representedV the intimate ties between France and the United StatesO
intimate（親密的）為核心字；ties 此處作「關係、聯繫」解（常用複數）；between A and B = 在 A 與 B 之間。
▶Its total height is 305 feet, and the statue itself stands 151 feet tall, which is about the height of 15 floors. As you get closer, you will see how big the statue is.
它的總高度是 305 呎，雕像本身則高 151 呎，大約是 15 層樓的高度。當你越靠近，就會看見這座雕像有多大。
🔍 句構分析 ①the statue itselfS（加反身代名詞強調） standsV 151 feet tallC , which is about the height of 15 floors非限定關代（補述前句）
②As you get closer副詞子句（隨著） youS will seeV how big the statue isO（名詞子句）
stand + 高度 + tall = 有……高；as 此處 = 隨著／當……時；名詞子句 how big the statue is 用直述語序。
▶When it was first constructed, it was reddish-brown. But as you can see, the statue is green now. This is because its surface is made of copper, and it turns green with exposure to air.
它剛建成時是紅棕色的。但如你所見，雕像現在是綠色的。這是因為它的表面是銅製的，接觸空氣後就會變綠。
🔍 句構分析 ①When it was first constructed時間副詞子句（被動） itS wasV reddish-brownC
②its surfaceS is made ofV（被動片語） copper材料 turnsV（連綴） greenC
construct（建造，v.）→ construction（n.）為核心字；be made of = 由……製成（看得出原料，見片語表）；turn + 顏色 = 變成……色；exposure（暴露、接觸，n.）為核心字。
🟦 Rosa｜Statue of Liberty Tour（導覽 Part 2：各部位象徵）
▶Okay, we're getting quite close now. If you glance to your left, you should have a very clear view of the whole statue.
好，我們現在相當接近了。如果你往左瞥一眼，應該能清楚看見整座雕像。
🔍 句構分析 ①If you glance to your left條件副詞子句 youS should haveV a very clear view of the whole statueO
glance（瞥一眼，v./n.）為核心字，常搭 glance at / to；have a clear view of = 清楚看見……。
▶If you take note of her left hand, you'll see that Lady Liberty holds a giant tablet with the date "July 4, 1776" on it. It was on this day that the United States became independent from England.
如果你注意看她的左手，會看到自由女神拿著一塊巨大的平板，上面刻著「1776 年 7 月 4 日」。就是在這一天，美國脫離英國獨立。
🔍 句構分析 ①If you take note of her left hand條件子句 youS 'll seeV that Lady Liberty holds a giant tablet...O（名詞子句）
②It was on this day強調句 that the United StatesS becameV independent from EnglandC
take note of = 注意、留意（見片語表）；It was ... that ... 為強調句型（此處強調時間）；independent（獨立的，adj.）為核心字。
▶In her right hand is a giant torch. This torch symbolizes the idea that Lady Liberty can enlighten the world and guide immigrants to a new land.
她的右手拿著一支巨大的火炬。這火炬象徵自由女神能啟迪世界、指引移民前往新天地。
🔍 句構分析 ①In her right hand地方副詞（置句首） isV a giant torchS（倒裝，真主詞）— 地方副詞置句首的倒裝
②This torchS symbolizesV the idea that...O（後接同位語子句）
倒裝：地方副詞片語置句首，主詞與動詞倒裝（見句型三）；symbolize（象徵）、enlighten（啟迪）、immigrant（移民，n.）皆為核心字。
▶Apart from the tablet and the torch, other portions of the statue that serve as symbols include Lady Liberty's crown and the broken chains.
除了平板與火炬之外，雕像其他作為象徵的部位還包括自由女神的皇冠與斷裂的鎖鏈。
🔍 句構分析 ①Apart from the tablet and the torch介副（除了） other portions of the statueS that serve as symbols關係子句修飾 S includeV Lady Liberty's crown and the broken chainsO
apart from = 除了……之外（見片語表）；portion（部分，n.）為核心字；serve as = 充當、作為；主詞是複數 portions，故動詞用 include。
▶Can you see how many spikes there are on her crown? That's right; from the top of the crown rise seven spikes, which represent the rays of the sun. You may also have figured out that the broken chains symbolize freedom from oppression.
你能看出她的皇冠上有幾道尖芒嗎？沒錯，皇冠頂端伸出七道尖芒，代表太陽的光芒。你或許也已看出，斷裂的鎖鏈象徵掙脫壓迫的自由。
🔍 句構分析 ①from the top of the crown地方副詞（置句首） riseV seven spikesS（倒裝） , which represent the rays of the sun非限定關代
②YouS may have figured outV that the broken chains symbolize freedom...O（名詞子句）
figure out = 想通、看出（見片語表）；freedom（自由，n.）為核心字；from... rise seven spikes 又是地方副詞倒裝。
▶The sculptors who made this statue were in fact inspired by Libertas, the Roman goddess of freedom, and that is why Lady Liberty is dressed in a classical Roman robe.
打造這座雕像的雕刻家其實是受到羅馬自由女神 Libertas 的啟發，這就是為什麼自由女神身著古典羅馬長袍。
🔍 句構分析 ①The sculptorsS who made this statue關代子句（who＝人） were inspired byV（被動） Libertas...動作者
②thatS isV why Lady Liberty is dressed in a classical Roman robeC（名詞子句）
關係代名詞 who 引導子句修飾人（見句型二）；be inspired by = 受……啟發；classical（古典的）為核心字；be dressed in = 穿著。
▶Well, here we are. Please watch your step when you get off! Oh, one piece of advice: besides admiring the statue from the outside, I would also recommend that you take the stairs right up to the crown. If you climb up there, you can enjoy the glorious view of Lower Manhattan!
好，我們到了。下船時請小心腳步！喔，給你們一個建議：除了從外面欣賞雕像，我也建議各位走樓梯直達皇冠。爬上去的話，就能飽覽下曼哈頓的壯麗景色！
🔍 句構分析 ①Please watchV（祈使） your stepO when you get off時間子句
②IS would recommendV that you (should) take the stairs...O（建議子句，接原形）
watch your step = 小心腳步（見片語表）；advice（建議，n. 不可數）／advise（v.）；recommend that S (should) + 原形（見句型五）；admire（欣賞）、glorious（壯麗的）為核心字。
🟩 Meg & Andrew｜Booking a Statue of Liberty Tour（訂購自由女神導覽行程）
▶Meg and Andrew are trying to find a cheap Statue of Liberty tour online.
Meg 與 Andrew 正試著在網路上找便宜的自由女神像行程。
🔍 句構分析 ①Meg and AndrewS are tryingV（現在進行） to find a cheap Statue of Liberty tourO（不定詞） online副詞
try to V = 試圖做……；online 此處作副詞「在線上」。
▶Meg: I think I found a really good tour for our Statue of Liberty visit. Come check it out! It's this one right here, called "Lady Liberty Tour."
Meg：我想我找到一個很棒的自由女神參觀行程了。過來看看！就是這裡這個，叫做「Lady Liberty Tour」。
🔍 句構分析 ①IS foundV a really good tourO
②Come checkV（祈使，come + 原形） itO out介副
check it out = 看看、瞧瞧；called... 為過去分詞片語（＝ which is called）修飾 one。
▶Andrew: How exciting! Let me take a look. Let me see.... Return boat trip, guided tour, lunch included... seems perfect. How much does it cost?
Andrew：好興奮啊！讓我看看。我看看……來回船票、專人導覽、含午餐……看起來很完美。要多少錢？
🔍 句構分析 ①LetV（使役） meO take a lookOC（原形）
②How much疑問詞 does itS costV
How exciting! = 感嘆句（How + adj.）；let sb. + 原形；guided tour = 導覽行程；included 為過去分詞（被包含）。
▶Meg: Take a look at the price per person at the bottom.
Meg：看一下最下面的每人價格。
🔍 句構分析 ①TakeV（祈使） a lookO at the price per person at the bottom副詞
take a look at = 看一看；per person = 每人；at the bottom = 在底部。
▶Andrew: Wow. That costs an arm and a leg! Can you find anything cheaper?
Andrew：哇，那貴得嚇死人！你能找到更便宜的嗎？
🔍 句構分析 ①ThatS costsV an arm and a legO（俚語）
②Can youS findV anything cheaperO
cost an arm and a leg = 花一大筆錢、貴得嚇人（俚語，見俚語補充）；形容詞 cheaper 置於 anything 之後（不定代名詞後位修飾）。
▶Meg: The cheaper boat tours don't stop at Liberty Island. We would only get to see the statue from the boat.
Meg：比較便宜的船班不會停靠自由島。我們就只能從船上看雕像而已。
🔍 句構分析 ①The cheaper boat toursS don't stopV at Liberty Island地方副詞
②WeS would only getV to see the statue from the boatO（不定詞）
get to V = 得以（有機會）做……；stop at = 停靠於。
▶Andrew: But I really wanted to climb up inside Lady Liberty!
Andrew：可是我真的很想爬進自由女神像裡面耶！
🔍 句構分析 ①IS really wantedV to climb up inside Lady LibertyO（不定詞）
climb up = 往上爬；want to V 用過去式 wanted 表較委婉的語氣。
▶Meg: Me, too. Hey! I have an idea. How about trying to cut some other costs on our trip? Then we can fit this tour in our budget.
Meg：我也是。嘿！我有個主意。不如試著在旅程其他方面省一些錢？這樣就能把這個行程塞進我們的預算了。
🔍 句構分析 ①How about提議句型 trying to cut some other costs on our tripO（動名詞）
②weS can fitV this tourO in our budget副詞
How about + V-ing? = ……如何？（提議）；cut costs = 削減開支；fit... in one's budget = 符合預算。
▶Andrew: But how could we cut other costs?
Andrew：可是我們要怎麼省下其他開支呢？
🔍 句構分析 ①how疑問副詞 could weS cutV other costsO
could 此處表示委婉、假設的可能性。
▶Meg: For example, we could stay in a hostel instead of a hotel, or buy groceries instead of eating out in restaurants.
Meg：舉例來說，我們可以住青年旅館而不是飯店，或是買食材自己煮，而不是上餐廳外食。
🔍 句構分析 ①weS could stayV in a hostel instead of a hotel副詞 or buy groceries instead of eating out並列
instead of + N/V-ing = 而不是……；hostel（青年旅館）≠ hotel；eat out = 外食。

核心句型文法解析
👆 點擊每個句型標題，展開「公式 + 例句 + 常見錯誤 + 統測重點」。
句型一｜被動語態（含授與動詞被動）▼
S + be + 過去分詞 (p.p.) + (by 動作者) 授與動詞：人 + be + p.p. + 物（America was given the statue）

主詞是「動作的承受者」時用被動：be 動詞＋過去分詞。give 為授與動詞，人當主詞時後面仍保留「物」。

In 1886, America was given the Statue of Liberty by France.
1886 年，法國把自由女神像贈予美國。（課文）
When it was first constructed, the statue was reddish-brown.
雕像剛建成時是紅棕色的。（課文）
✗ America was given by France the statue（物與 by 片語順序錯）；✓ was given the statue by France。
統測常考主動↔被動轉換，以及 be made of / be dressed in 等被動片語。
句型二｜關係代名詞 who / which / that▼
先行詞（人）+ who + V ... 先行詞（物）+ which + V ...（非限定用逗號 , which）

關係代名詞引導形容詞子句修飾前面的名詞：人用 who，物用 which，兩者皆可用 that（非限定子句不可用 that）。

The sculptors who made this statue were inspired by Libertas.
打造這座雕像的雕刻家受到 Libertas 啟發。（課文）
Seven spikes rise from the crown, which represent the rays of the sun.
皇冠伸出七道尖芒，代表太陽的光芒。（課文，非限定）
✗ The sculptors which made...（人不可用 which）；✗ ..., that represent...（逗號後非限定不可用 that）。
統測常考 who／which／whose 辨別，以及關代可否省略。
句型三｜地方副詞置句首的倒裝▼
地方副詞片語 + 動詞 + 主詞（S 與 V 倒裝）

當表「地點」的副詞片語移到句首、且主詞為名詞時，主詞與動詞要倒裝（動詞在前）。動詞須與「真主詞」一致。

In her right hand is a giant torch.
她的右手拿著一支巨大的火炬。（課文，真主詞 torch 單數→ is）
From the top of the crown rise seven spikes.
皇冠頂端伸出七道尖芒。（課文，真主詞 spikes 複數→ rise）
✗ In her right hand a giant torch is（未倒裝）；動詞要與真主詞（torch/spikes）一致，不是與地方副詞。
若主詞是「代名詞」則不倒裝：Here it comes.（✗ Here comes it.）
句型四｜連綴動詞 + 形容詞補語▼
S + 連綴動詞 + adj.（主詞補語） 常見：be, become, turn, get, look, seem, feel, stay

連綴動詞後接「形容詞」說明主詞狀態，不接副詞。turn/become/get + 形容詞表「變成……」。

連綴動詞例
turn + 顏色It turns green. 它變綠。
become + adj.became independent 變得獨立
look / seem + adj.seems perfect 看起來完美
The copper turns green with exposure to air.
銅接觸空氣後變綠。（課文）
✗ It turns greenly（連綴動詞後不接副詞）；✗ seems perfectly（應為 seems perfect）。
句型五｜建議／要求：recommend / advise (that) S (should) + 原形▼
recommend / advise / suggest / insist (that) + S + (should) + 原形 V 提議：How about + V-ing? / What about + V-ing?

表「建議、要求」的動詞後接的 that 子句用「(should) + 原形動詞」，should 常省略。提議做某事用 How about + V-ing。

I would recommend that you take the stairs up to the crown.
我建議各位走樓梯上皇冠。（課文，take 用原形）
How about trying to cut some other costs?
不如試著省下其他開支？（課文，How about + V-ing）
✗ recommend that you takes（子句動詞不加 s，用原形）；✗ How about to try（How about 後接 V-ing）。
統測常考 suggest/advise/insist + (should) 原形，以及 advise sb. to V 的差異。
