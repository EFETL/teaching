🌏 兩個 Formosa

「Formosa」是台灣的非官方名稱；但少有人知，在阿根廷巴拉圭河畔還有另一個 Formosa。本課帶你比較這兩個同名之地（namesakes）的異同。

⛵ 命名由來

兩地都是 16 世紀歐洲探險家無意間抵達並命名的：葡萄牙水手稱台灣為「Ilha Formosa」（美麗之島），西班牙人也為南美這片土地取名 Formosa。他們因該地絕佳的戰略位置與經濟優勢而定居下來。

📍 地理巧合

台灣位於北緯 23°、阿根廷 Formosa 位於南緯 23°，離赤道等距，恰好位於地球兩端（antipodal）。兩地雖同名、有諸多相似，地貌與經濟卻仍相隔天壤（worlds apart）。

🌏 一｜兩個 Formosa：不只台灣
▶"Formosa" is commonly known as an unofficial name for Taiwan.
「Formosa（福爾摩沙）」廣為人知，是台灣的一個非官方名稱。
🔍 句構分析 ①"Formosa"S is commonly known asV（被動） an unofficial name for TaiwanC
be known as = 被稱為（見句型三）；unofficial（非官方的）＝ un-＋official。
▶However, what many people don't know is that there is another Formosa located on the banks of the Paraguay River in Argentina.
然而，許多人不知道的是，在阿根廷巴拉圭河畔還有另一個 Formosa。
🔍 句構分析 ①what many people don't knowS（名詞子句） isV that there is another Formosa...C（名詞子句）
what 引導名詞子句作主詞；located on = 位於（過去分詞修飾 Formosa）；on the banks of = 在…河畔。
▶To explore this connection, we can take a closer look at these two namesakes to see how they are similar and how they differ.
為了探究這層關聯，我們可以更仔細地看看這兩個同名之地，看看它們有何相似、又有何不同。
🔍 句構分析 ①To explore this connection不定詞（目的） weS can takeV a closer lookO
take a (closer) look at = 看一看（片語）；namesake = 同名之人／物；differ（不同，vi.）。
▶Interestingly, it appears that both Formosas were unintentionally reached by European explorers in the sixteenth century.
有趣的是，這兩個 Formosa 似乎都是 16 世紀時被歐洲探險家無意間抵達的。
🔍 句構分析 ①it虛主詞 appearsV that both Formosas were unintentionally reached...真主詞（名詞子句）
it appears that = 似乎…；were reached（被動語態，見句型一）；unintentionally = 無意間地。
⛵ 二｜命名由來：探險家與美麗之島
▶It is believed that a crew of Portuguese sailors came across Taiwan and named it "Ilha Formosa," which means "beautiful island."
據信，一群葡萄牙水手偶然發現了台灣，並將它命名為「Ilha Formosa」，意思是「美麗之島」。
🔍 句構分析 ①It虛主詞 is believedV（被動） that a crew of Portuguese sailors...真主詞子句
It is believed that = 據信…；crew（全體船員）、sailor（水手）；come across = 偶然發現；which 引導非限定關係子句。
▶Similarly, Formosa was the European name given to an area of land in South America by Spanish explorers around the same time.
同樣地，Formosa 也是西班牙探險家在大約同一時期為南美洲一塊土地所取的歐洲名稱。
🔍 句構分析 ①FormosaS wasV the European name [given to... by Spanish explorers]C（過去分詞片語修飾）
given to A by B = 由 B 給 A 的（過去分詞片語，見句型一）；similarly = 同樣地。
▶These explorers decided to settle there mainly because the area possessed an exceptional strategic location and economic advantages.
這些探險家決定在那裡定居下來，主要是因為該地擁有絕佳的戰略位置與經濟優勢。
🔍 句構分析 ①These explorersS decided to settleV there mainly because the area possessed...原因副詞子句
settle（定居）、possess（擁有）、exceptional（絕佳的）、strategic（戰略的）為核心字。
▶Apart from their shared name and historical similarities, there is an interesting geographical fact that connects these two locations.
除了共有的名字與歷史上的相似之處，還有一個有趣的地理事實把這兩地連結在一起。
🔍 句構分析 ①Apart from their shared name...介系詞片語 there isV an interesting geographical fact [that connects...]主詞（關係子句，見句型二）
apart from = 除了…之外；that connects... 關係子句修飾 fact（見句型二）。
📍 三｜地理巧合：地球兩端的對點
▶Taiwan is located at 23° N and Argentina's Formosa at 23° S, which means both areas are roughly the same distance from the equator.
台灣位於北緯 23 度，阿根廷的 Formosa 位於南緯 23 度，這意味著兩地離赤道的距離大致相同。
🔍 句構分析 ①TaiwanS is locatedV at 23° N副詞 , which means...非限定關係子句
be located at = 位於；which 指前面整句（非限定關係子句）；equator = 赤道。
▶What's more, each of them sits on the exact opposite side of the world from the other.
更甚者，兩地各自正好位於地球上與對方相對的另一端。
🔍 句構分析 ①each of themS sitsV on the exact opposite side of the world from the other副詞
what's more = 此外、更甚者；opposite（相對的）；each...from the other = 彼此相對。
▶If someone in northern Taiwan dug a hole straight down into the Earth, penetrated its core, and then continued digging to the surface on the other side, he or she would end up in Formosa, Argentina!
假如台灣北部有人筆直向下挖洞、穿過地心，再繼續往另一側的地表挖出去，他（她）最後會抵達阿根廷的 Formosa！
🔍 句構分析 ①If someone... dug a hole...與現在相反的假設（if 子句） he or sheS would end upV in Formosa, Argentina副詞
與現在事實相反假設：If S + 過去式, S would + 原形；core（地心）、end up = 最後落得／抵達。
▶Since most of the globe is covered in ocean, it is remarkable to find that the two Formosas are exactly antipodal!
由於地球大部分被海洋覆蓋，能發現這兩個 Formosa 恰好位於地球的對點，實在非常難得！
🔍 句構分析 ①Since most of the globe is covered in ocean原因子句 it虛主詞 isV remarkableC to find that...真主詞
since = 由於（表原因）；be covered in = 被…覆蓋；antipodal = 位於地球對點的。
🏔️ 四｜相隔天壤：迥異的地貌
▶Despite these things that they have in common, the two Formosas are, in many ways, still worlds apart.
儘管有這些共通之處，這兩個 Formosa 在許多方面仍相隔天壤。
🔍 句構分析 ①Despite these things [that they have in common]介系詞片語（含關係子句） the two FormosasS areV worlds apartC
despite = 儘管（後接名詞）；have...in common = 有共通點；be worlds apart = 相隔天壤（片語）。
▶Their natural landscapes, for a start, differ considerably.
首先，兩地的自然景觀就有相當大的差異。
🔍 句構分析 ①Their natural landscapesS , for a start,插入語 differV considerably副詞
differ（不同，vi.）、considerably（相當地）為核心字；for a start = 首先。
▶Two thirds of Taiwan's 36,000 square kilometers are covered with densely forested majestic mountains, some of which soar thousands of meters above sea level.
台灣 3 萬 6 千平方公里中，有三分之二被林木茂密、雄偉壯麗的山脈覆蓋，其中有些高聳於海平面數千公尺之上。
🔍 句構分析 ①Two thirds of Taiwan's 36,000 square kilometersS are covered withV（被動） majestic mountains [some of which soar...]by 受詞（含關係子句）
densely（密集地）、majestic（雄偉的）、soar（高聳／翱翔）為核心字；some of which＝介系詞＋關代（見句型二）。
▶Furthermore, the island's mountains, valleys, rivers, and reefs are home to hundreds of unique species found only in Taiwan.
此外，這座島嶼的山脈、山谷、河流與礁岩，孕育了數百種台灣獨有的物種。
🔍 句構分析 ①the island's mountains, valleys, rivers, and reefsS areV home to hundreds of unique species [found only in Taiwan]C（含過去分詞片語）
valley（山谷）為核心字；be home to = 是…的家園；found only in（過去分詞修飾 species）。
▶By contrast, most of Argentina's Formosa, which is two times the size of Taiwan, is fairly flat and made up of trees, grassland, or marshland.
相較之下，面積是台灣兩倍大的阿根廷 Formosa，大部分地勢相當平坦，由樹林、草原或沼澤地所組成。
🔍 句構分析 ①most of Argentina's FormosaS , which is two times the size of...,非限定關係子句 isV fairly flat and made up of...C
by contrast = 相較之下（見句型四）；make up of = 由…組成；倍數 two times the size of。
▶Its eastern region receives more rain than its other parts and also has more forests and streams.
它的東部地區比其他區域降雨更多，也擁有較多的森林與溪流。
🔍 句構分析 ①Its eastern regionS receivesV more rainO than its other parts比較
stream（溪流）為核心字；more rain than = 比…更多的雨（比較級）。
💰 五｜相隔天壤：對比的經濟
▶There is, in addition, a stark contrast between their economies.
此外，兩地的經濟之間也存在著鮮明的對比。

今日的台灣以其活絡的經濟與先進的資訊科技產業而聞名於世。

▶Moreover, the Asian island is famous for its natural beauty and friendly people and thus attracts visitors from far and wide.
此外，這座亞洲島嶼也以其自然美景與友善的人民而著稱，因此吸引了來自四面八方的遊客。

▶By contrast, Formosa is one of Argentina's least developed areas.

▶Its economy relies primarily on raising cattle and other agricultural activities such as growing cotton and fruit.

▶The number of cattle in the area exceeds 1.5 million, which is more than three times the number of people living there!

🤝 六｜結語：或許有天面對面
▶Whatever differences or similarities exist between the pair, each has a beauty that makes it special.
無論這兩地之間存在著什麼差異或相似之處，各自都有一種使它與眾不同的美。

▶Both Argentina's Formosa, with its gorgeous green plains and lush forests, and Taiwan, with its bustling cities and mighty, mountainous landscapes, are sure to impress visitors.
阿根廷的 Formosa 有著壯麗的綠色平原與蒼翠的森林，台灣則有熱鬧繁忙的城市與雄偉多山的地景，兩者都必定會讓遊客印象深刻。

▶Perhaps the people of these two lands on opposite sides of the world could even come face to face one day as well and get to know each other a little better!
或許有一天，這兩片位於地球兩端的土地上的人們，也能面對面相見，並更加彼此認識！
🔍 句構分析 ①the people of these two lands...S could... comeV face to face one day副詞 and get to knowV each otherO

常用俚語補充（提升口語語感）
👆 點擊每個俚語展開「意思 + 生活例句」。這些俚語都與課文主題（自由、正義、希望）相關，能讓口語更道地。
worlds apart
天差地遠；南轅北轍
▶
兩者差異極大、截然不同（呼應課文 be worlds apart）。
The two islands are worlds apart in landscape and economy.這兩座島在地景與經濟上天差地遠。
a stone's throw away
近在咫尺
▶
距離很近，一步之遙。
The night market is just a stone's throw away from my home.夜市離我家近在咫尺。
off the beaten track
人跡罕至的；偏僻的
▶
遠離熱門景點、鮮少人去的地方。
Argentina's Formosa is a place off the beaten track.阿根廷的 Formosa 是個人跡罕至之地。
the other side of the coin
事情的另一面
▶
同一件事的相反或不同面向。
The two Formosas show the other side of the coin of one name.兩個 Formosa 呈現同一名字的另一面。
from far and wide
從四面八方（呼應課文）
▶
來自各個遠近不同的地方。
Tourists come from far and wide to see Taiwan's mountains.遊客從四面八方前來欣賞台灣的山。
poles apart
兩極對立；截然相反
▶
意見或性質完全相反，如同南北兩極（雙關地理主題）。
Their economies are poles apart.兩地的經濟截然相反。

核心句型文法解析
👆 點擊每個句型標題，展開「公式 + 例句 + 常見錯誤 + 學測重點」。
句型一｜被動語態與 be + p.p. 片語（was reached / is covered with）▼
S + be + p.p. (+ by/with/in ...)　→ 主詞是動作的承受者

課文大量使用被動語態描述「被抵達、被命名、被覆蓋」等；注意介系詞搭配。

Both Formosas were unintentionally reached by European explorers.
兩個 Formosa 都被歐洲探險家無意間抵達。（課文）
The mountains are covered with forests; the globe is covered in ocean.
山被森林覆蓋；地球被海洋覆蓋。（課文）
✗ were reached by（漏 by）／混用介系詞；covered with/in 皆可，avoid「covered by ocean」語感較弱。
學測常考「主動 vs 被動」與 be known as / for、be made up of 等固定被動片語。
句型二｜關係子句與「介系詞＋關代」（that / which / some of which）▼
先行詞 + (介系詞 +) which / that + ... ；some of which + V

關代引導子句修飾先行詞；「數量詞 + of which」可接續補充說明。

an interesting geographical fact that connects these two locations
一個把兩地連結起來的地理事實（課文，限定用法）
majestic mountains, some of which soar thousands of meters above sea level
雄偉的山脈，其中有些高聳於海平面數千公尺之上（課文）
✗ some of them soar（them 不能連接兩句）；補充說明須用 some of which。
非限定關係子句（前有逗號）補充說明，不可用 that；學測高頻。
句型三｜be known as / for、be famous for（以…著稱）▼
be known as + 身分／名稱；be known/famous for + 特點

as 接「身分、名稱」；for 接「原因、特點」，兩者意義不同。

"Formosa" is known as an unofficial name for Taiwan.
Formosa 被稱為台灣的非官方名稱。（as＋名稱，課文）
Taiwan is known for its lively economy and is famous for its natural beauty.
台灣以活絡經濟聞名，也以自然美景著稱。（for＋特點，課文）
✗ be known for a name（名稱應用 as）；✗ be famous as its beauty（特點應用 for）。
學測常考 as/for 的區別；亦見 be known to（為…所知）。
句型四｜對比與比較（by contrast / while / 倍數表達）▼
By contrast, S + V ...；A is two/three times the + N + of B

本課以「對比」貫穿全文；倍數常用「倍數 + the size/number of」。

By contrast, Formosa is one of Argentina's least developed areas.
相較之下，Formosa 是阿根廷最不發達的地區之一。（課文）
Argentina's Formosa is two times the size of Taiwan.
阿根廷的 Formosa 面積是台灣的兩倍。（課文）
✗ two times bigger than the size（size 與 times 只擇一）；倍數用 times the size of。
對比連接詞：by contrast / in contrast / whereas / while；學測寫作對比常用。
句型五｜名詞子句與複合關係詞（what / that / whatever）▼
what / that + 子句 作主詞或補語；whatever + N + V（無論…）

what＝the thing that，本身含先行詞；whatever 引導讓步子句表「無論什麼」。

What many people don't know is that there is another Formosa.
許多人不知道的是，還有另一個 Formosa。（what 作主詞、that 作補語，課文）
Whatever differences or similarities exist between the pair, each has a beauty.
無論兩地有何異同，各自都有其美。（課文）
✗ What many people don't know is there is...（省略 that 於正式補語子句較不佳）；what 前不再加先行詞。
what vs that：what 含先行詞、that 不含；學測常考名詞子句連接詞。
