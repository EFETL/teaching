🟦 Dialogue｜媽媽迷上年輕演員 Ryan Bank（In the living room）

Zac 看到媽媽在哭——原來是她最愛的演員（actor）Ryan Bank 在電影裡哭，他的演技觸動她的心（touches my heart）。媽媽說：I’m old enough to be his mom，還說永遠不會看膩（be bored with）他的電影；Ryan 有才華又勤奮（talented and hard-working），八歲開始演戲至今已經得過好幾個獎（has won several awards）。Zac：I’m surprised to hear that.

🟩 Dialogue（一小時後）｜盜版鬼片？媽媽做錯了

Ryan Bank 的新片看起來很刺激（exciting），講古城堡（castle）裡的鬼（ghosts）。爸爸討厭恐怖片，Zac 說 I’ll pass——恐怖片對他來說很無聊（boring），而且電影限 18 歲以上，他年紀太小不能看（too young to watch it）。Zac 發現電影還在上映，媽媽卻從網路（Internet）下載（download）——是盜版（pirated）！媽媽：Sorry, I set a bad example. I was so excited that I didn’t think clearly. 她決定上網訂票（book a ticket online），Zac：I’m proud of you, Mom.

🟨 Reading｜Are You Sharing Lies?（你在散播謊言嗎？）

讀到很有趣的貼文（post）就馬上（right away）分享到 Line 群組，可能就在散播假（fake）訊息。電視、報社會先查證事實，網路上卻沒有人（nobody）幫忙查證，任何人（anybody）都能張貼，有人還故意（by design）寫假新聞衝點閱（hits）。三個方法分辨差別（tell the difference）：查作者和來源（source）、看句子有沒有拼字錯誤（spelling mistakes）、上事實查核網頁（fact-checking web page）。分享前要 think twice，Don’t let people trick you into spreading lies.

🟧 Grammar｜-ed / -ing ＋ so…that ＋ enough to ＋ too…to

課本 Grammar Focus 1：interesting / interested、exciting / excited、boring / bored、surprising / surprised（22 校全考）；Grammar Focus 2：so + adj. + that + 子句（21 校）；Usage A：adj. + enough to V（12 校）；Usage B：too + adj. + (for sb.) + to V（18 校），三句互換。

核心句型文法解析
👆 點擊每個句型標題，展開「公式 + 例句 + 常見錯誤 + 段考重點」。
句型一｜情緒形容詞 -ed（人的感受）vs -ing（事物性質）（Grammar Focus 1・22 校全考）▼
人 + be / feel + V-ed（bored, excited, surprised, interested, scared, touched） 事物 + be + V-ing（boring, exciting, surprising, interesting, touching）；scare → scary 常見介系詞：bored with、excited about、surprised at、interested in、scared of、tired of
動詞-ed（人）-ing（事物）
borebored withboring
exciteexcited aboutexciting
surprisesurprised at / thatsurprising
interestinterested ininteresting
scarescared ofscary（✗ scaring 當形容詞）
The book is interesting to the children. ＝ The children are interested in the book.
這本書對孩子們來說很有趣。＝孩子們對這本書有興趣。（課本例句）
Scary movies are boring to me. ／ I’ll never be bored with his movies.
恐怖片對我來說很無聊。／我永遠不會看膩他的電影。（課本對話）
Everyone was surprised that the dog could open the door.
大家都很驚訝那隻狗會開門。（that + 子句，✗ surprised at + 子句）
✗ I was scaring.　✗ The movie made me boring.（me 是人 → bored）　✗ Playing games is excited.（動名詞主詞是「事」→ exciting）
22 校、58 題，翰林 Unit 2 最大考點。判斷法：問自己「主詞是感受者，還是讓人有感受的東西？」
句型二｜so + adj. + that + 子句：如此…以致於（Grammar Focus 2・21 校）＋三句互換▼
S + be + so + adj. + that + S + can / could (not) + V so…that（否定）→ too…to　　so…that（肯定）→ enough to ※ 主詞一致才能改；that 子句時態要和主要子句一致
so…that改寫
The soup was so hot that I couldn’t drink it.The soup was too hot for me to drink.
Amy is so tall that she can reach it.Amy is tall enough to reach it.
I was so excited that I didn’t think clearly.（課本 Mom 的話）（意思是結果，不適合改寫）
The movie was very exciting, so the students kept talking about it. → The movie was so exciting that the students kept talking about it.
電影太刺激了，學生們一直在討論。（課本 Read 例句）
The water was so cold that we couldn’t swim in it. ＝ The water was too cold for us to swim in. ＝ The water was not warm enough for us to swim in.
水太冷了，我們不能下去游泳。（課本三句互換）
✗ so…that 用 very：very hot that…　✗ such hot that…（such 後接名詞：such a hot day）　✗ Mom was so excited that she can’t think clearly.（時態不一致）
so…that 21 校考；新北三和考 so（非 such）、右昌考 couldn’t 時態一致。
句型三｜adj. + enough + to V：夠…可以（Usage A・12 校）▼
S + be + adj. + enough + (for + 人) + to V原形 not + adj. + enough to ＝ too + 反義 adj. + to 例：not old enough to = too young to；not cheap enough = too expensive
Amy is tall enough to reach the top shelf.
Amy 夠高，搆得到最上層。
Mrs. Hugo is old enough to be the actor’s mother.
Hugo 太太的年紀大到可以當那個演員的媽媽。（課本例句）
Zac is not old enough to watch the movie. ＝ Zac is too young to watch the movie.
Zac 年紀還不夠大，不能看那部電影。（課本例句）
The bike is not cheap enough for Dad to buy. ＝ It’s too expensive for Dad to buy.
這台腳踏車不夠便宜，爸爸買不下手。
✗ enough old（enough 修飾形容詞時要放後面）　✗ not old enough = too old（要換反義字）
enough 放形容詞後（丹鳳、嘉義玉山、新北三多考）；改寫題一定要換「反義形容詞」（崇林、文府考）。
句型四｜too + adj. + (for sb.) + to V：太…而不能（Usage B・18 校）▼
S + be + too + adj. + (for + 人) + to V原形 = S + be + so + adj. + that + S + can’t / couldn’t + V ※ too…to 本身就是否定，不可再加 not；主詞若就是 to V 的受詞，受詞要省略
Kim is too young to understand the movie.
Kim 年紀太小，看不懂這部電影。（課本例句）
The movie is too scary for Kim to watch.
這部電影太可怕，Kim 不能看。（課本例句；✗ to watch it）
The box is too heavy for me to carry.
箱子太重，我搬不動。（✗ to carry it：主詞 the box 就是 carry 的受詞）
It’s never too late to start over.
重新開始永遠不嫌晚。
✗ I was too tired not to study.（雙重否定）　✗ The tea is too hot for me to drink it.　✗ He is too young to can drive.（to 後接原形）
右昌、嘉義玉山、大社都考「too…to 後面不能再接受詞 it」；文府考「too…to 已含否定」。
