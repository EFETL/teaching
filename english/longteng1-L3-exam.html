閱讀測驗

📄 新北高中 · 第一次期中考(113) · 閱讀測驗I
In spring, bears, frogs, and many other animals wake up from hibernation—a state where they sleep through the winter. Hibernation is a survival strategy because, without it, many animals would freeze or starve. It is more than just sleeping; it involves important changes in the body. When bears hibernate in winter, their heart rates slow down from about 50 beats per minute to around 10. Their body temperature also drops by 7 to 8 degrees Celsius. Bears can lose up to 40 percent of their body weight during hibernation, which is why they eat a lot to store fat before their long sleep. Interestingly, bears do not go to the bathroom during this time; their bodies can use waste as protein. Wood frogs are the most extreme hibernators. Their hearts actually stop beating, and 65 percent of the water in their bodies freezes during winter. They can stay frozen for up to seven months. When the weather warms up, their frozen blood melts, their hearts start beating again, and they are ready to hop around like before. The weather can be harsh sometimes, but nature has many ways to protect animals. Hibernation is one of these ways.

答案：hold / on / to / in / case　hold on to「緊抓住」（L3 片語）；in case「以防、以免」（L2 片語）。主考 L3 hold on to。
明倫高中 · 第一次期中考#翻譯18-19
有很多保持健康的方法，例如，你可以多運動和均衡飲食。There are many ways to stay healthy; ___(18)___ ___(19)___, you can exercise more and have a balanced diet.
答案：for example　例如（L3 片語 for example）。
明倫高中 · 第一次期中考#翻譯24-25
根據警方說法，搶銀行而被通緝的男子正躲藏在市中心某處。A___(24)___ ___(25)___ the police, the man wanted for bank robbery was hiding somewhere in the downtown area.
答案：According to　根據警方說法：A__ → According to the police（L3 片語）。
明倫高中 · 第一次期中考#翻譯26-27
普遍相信有高達 7 千萬人在第二次世界大戰中被殺害。___(26)___ ___(27)___ 70 million people are believed to have been killed in World War II.
答案：Up to　高達 7 千萬人：Up to 70 million people（L3 片語 up to）。
金門高中 · 第一次段考#5
當你站在行進中的公車上，抓緊車上其中一個扶桿是個好主意。 It is a good idea to h___9___ ___10___ ___11___ one of the poles while you are standing on a moving bus.

金門高中 · 第一次段考#6
我一天平均睡 7 小時，讀 3 小時的書。 I sleep 7 hours and study 3 hours a day ___12___ a ___13___.
答案：on / average　on average「平均而言」，L3 課文搭配。

台南二中 · 第一次期中考#翻譯1
當你出國旅行時，當心竊賊是很重要的。(It is...)
答案：When you travel abroad, it is important to watch out for thieves.　劃底線「當心竊賊」用 L3 片語 watch out for；句型 It is important to + V。
台南二中 · 第一次期中考#翻譯2
此外，你應該避免將個人資訊給陌生人。
答案：Besides / In addition, you should avoid giving your personal information to strangers.　avoid + Ving（L3 文法句型 avoid/keep + V-ing）；此外用 In addition（L2 片語）亦可。

小港高中 · 第一次月考#58
It's so im______e that Brian can say "hello" in more than ten languages.
答案：impressive　「Brian 能用十多種語言說 hello，真『令人佩服』」。
小港高中 · 第一次月考#59
The d______e between the eastern end of Russia and the western one in about ten thousand kilometers.
答案：distance　「俄羅斯東西兩端的『距離』約一萬公里」。
小港高中 · 第一次月考#60
After high school, the two best friends plan to go on a j______y around the world.
答案：journey　「兩位好友計畫高中畢業後環遊世界『旅行』」。
小港高中 · 第一次月考#61
Some animals can su______e from season to season by changing the color of their fur.
答案：survive　「有些動物靠改變毛色而『存活』過季節」，survive 生存。
