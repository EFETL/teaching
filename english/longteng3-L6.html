L6: The Country of the Blind ── 盲人國

故事速覽（三幕劇情）

⛰️ 第一幕｜墜谷入盲村

經驗豐富的登山家 Nuñez 攀爬厄瓜多高山時失足，滑下雪坡、跌落斷崖。渾身瘀傷、暈眩的他，醒來時發現自己身處一個與世隔絕的山谷小村。

👁️ 第二幕｜自以為優越

走進村莊，他很快察覺全村人都是盲人。他心生優越感，暗想「在盲人的國度裡，獨眼者稱王」，深信有視力的自己必能主宰這些看不見的村民。

🌾 第三幕｜被在地智慧折服

相處後他卻大為驚嘆：村民用敏銳的聽、觸、嗅覺務農、辨路，對山中家園瞭若指掌；沿路低牆是為安全行走而特意築的。眼見的優越感就此被在地智慧折服。

本課改編自 H.G. Wells 的短篇故事，敘述登山家 Nuñez 墜入一個與世隔絕的盲人村落。以下摘要以老師自撰的英文重述劇情（非課文原句），依情節分為三幕，供逐句精讀。

⛰️ 第一幕｜墜谷入盲村（A Fall into a Hidden Valley）
▶A skilled climber named Nuñez was making his way up one of the tallest peaks in Ecuador when he suddenly slipped.
一位名叫 Nuñez 的熟練登山者正在攀登厄瓜多最高峰之一時，突然失足滑倒。
🔍 句構分析 ①A skilled climber named NuñezS（含過去分詞修飾） was makingV（過去進行） his wayO up one of the tallest peaks in Ecuador副詞
②when he suddenly slipped時間副詞子句（過去式，突發事件）
was V-ing when + 過去式＝正在…時（突然）…（見句型一）；named Nuñez 過去分詞片語修飾 climber；make one's way = 前進。
▶He slid down a snow-covered slope, went over the edge of a cliff, and dropped a long way before hitting the ground.
他滑下覆雪的斜坡，翻過崖邊，墜落了好一段距離才落地。
🔍 句構分析 ①HeS slid downV a snow-covered slopeO , went overV the edge of a cliffO , and droppedV a long way before hitting the ground副詞
slope（斜坡）、slide down（滑下）為核心字詞；三個動詞（slid／went／dropped）並列共用主詞 He；before + V-ing = 在…之前。
▶Aching all over and still in a daze, he woke up in a valley that had been shut away from the outside world.
全身痠痛、依然頭暈目眩，他在一個與外界隔絕的山谷中醒來。
🔍 句構分析 ①Aching all over and still in a daze分詞／形容詞片語（表狀態，修飾 he） heS woke upV in a valley [that had been shut away from the outside world]副詞（含關係子句）
bruised and dazed 的同義改寫；in a daze = 恍惚（dazed 的名詞用法）；cut off from 的同義 shut away from = 與…隔絕。
▶Even though he was hurt, he forced himself to his feet and set off to explore the strange place.
儘管受了傷，他仍撐起身子，動身去探索這個陌生的地方。
🔍 句構分析 ①Even though he was hurt讓步副詞子句 heS forcedV himselfO to his feetOC and set offV to explore the strange place不定詞（表目的）
struggle 的近義 force oneself to one's feet = 掙扎起身；set off = 動身、出發（片語）；even though = 儘管。
👁️ 第二幕｜自以為優越（The One-Eyed King）
▶As he wandered through the unfamiliar land, he came upon a village unlike any he had ever seen.
當他在這片陌生的土地上漫遊時，他偶然發現了一座與他以往所見都不同的村莊。
🔍 句構分析 ①As he wandered through the unfamiliar land時間副詞子句 heS came uponV（片語動詞） a villageO unlike any he had ever seen形容詞片語修飾
unfamiliar（陌生的）為核心字；come upon = 偶然遇見（片語）；as 引導「當…時」。
▶The walls of the houses were painted unevenly, and low stone walls ran along every road.
屋牆被漆得參差不齊，而每條道路兩旁都築有低矮的石牆。
🔍 句構分析 ①The walls of the housesS were paintedV（被動） unevenly副詞
②low stone wallsS ranV along every road副詞
unevenly（不均勻地）為核心字（uneven 的副詞）；be painted 被動；此細節暗示居民看不見。
▶It did not take long for Nuñez to realize that every single villager was blind.
Nuñez 沒過多久就意識到，村裡每一個人都是盲人。
🔍 句構分析 ①It虛主詞 did not takeV longO for Nuñez意義上主詞 to realize [that every single villager was blind]真主詞（不定詞＋名詞子句）
It doesn't take long for sb. to V＝某人很快就…（見句型三）；that 子句作 realize 的受詞。
▶Certain that he was superior to these sightless people, he told himself that a man with eyes would surely rule over them.
深信自己比這些看不見的人優越，他心想：一個有眼睛的人必定能統治他們。
🔍 句構分析 ①Certain that he was superior to these sightless people形容詞片語（表原因，修飾 he） heS toldV himselfO [that a man with eyes would surely rule over them]名詞子句作受詞
superior（優越的）、sightless（失明的）為核心字；be superior to = 比…優越（用 to，不用 than）；句首形容詞片語見句型四。
🌾 第三幕｜被在地智慧折服（Humbled by Their Wisdom）
▶After spending some time with the locals, however, he was amazed at how much they truly knew.
然而，與當地人相處一段時間後，他對他們所知之多感到驚訝。
🔍 句構分析 ①After spending some time with the locals, however時間副詞（含轉折） heS was amazedV at how much they truly knew介系詞＋名詞子句
amazed（驚訝的）為核心字；be amazed at = 對…感到驚訝；after + V-ing = 在…之後。
▶Though blind, the villagers knew their mountain home so intimately that they never lost their way.
儘管眼盲，村民對他們的山中家園熟悉到從不迷路。
🔍 句構分析 ①Though blind讓步（Though (they were) blind 省略） the villagersS knewV their mountain homeO so intimately that they never lost their wayso...that 結果
intimately（熟稔地）為核心字；Though + adj. 讓步省略（見句型四）；so...that = 如此…以致於。
▶By relying on their keen senses of hearing, touch, and smell, they farmed the land and moved about with ease.
憑藉敏銳的聽覺、觸覺與嗅覺，他們耕種土地，行動自如。
🔍 句構分析 ①By relying on their keen senses of hearing, touch, and smell副詞（表方法） theyS farmedV the landO and moved aboutV with ease副詞
keen（敏銳的）為核心字；by + V-ing = 藉由…（表方法，見句型五）；rely on = 依靠。
▶The low walls beside every path had been built on purpose to guide the blind safely from place to place.
每條小路旁的低牆是特意築起的，用來引導盲人安全地往來各處。
🔍 句構分析 ①The low walls beside every pathS had been builtV（過去完成被動） on purpose to guide the blind safely from place to place副詞（表目的）
had been built 過去完成被動（牆早已被建好）；on purpose = 故意、特意；the blind（the + adj.）＝盲人（複數）。眼見的優越感就此被在地智慧折服。

核心句型文法解析
👆 點擊每個句型標題，展開「公式 + 例句 + 常見錯誤 + 學測重點」。
句型一｜S + was/were + V-ing + when + 過去式（正在…時，突然…）▼
S + was/were + V-ing ... when + S + 過去式.

過去進行式表「正在進行的背景動作」，when 引導突然插入的短暫動作（用過去式）。

Nuñez was climbing the mountain when he lost his footing.
Nuñez 正在爬山時失足了。（摘要）
I was cooking dinner when the lights suddenly went out.
我正在煮晚餐時，燈突然熄了。
✗ 把背景動作也用過去式（Nuñez climbed... when he lost...）會使「正在進行」語意消失。
對比：when + 過去進行、while + 過去進行；學測綜合測驗常考時態搭配。
句型二｜分詞／形容詞片語置句首（Bruised and dazed, he...）▼
p.p. / V-ing / Adj. + ..., S + V ...（片語邏輯主詞＝主句主詞）

句首的分詞或形容詞片語修飾其後的主詞，表狀態或原因；被動用 p.p.、主動用 V-ing。

Bruised and dazed, he found himself in a hidden valley.
渾身瘀傷、頭暈目眩，他發現自己身處一個隱蔽的山谷。（摘要）
Feeling superior to the villagers, he underestimated them.
自覺比村民優越，他低估了他們。
✗ 懸垂修飾：片語的邏輯主詞若與主句主詞不一致即錯（Bruised and dazed, the valley...）。
形容詞片語＝Being 省略（(Being) bruised and dazed）；學測改錯高頻考點。
句型三｜It doesn't take long for sb. to V（某人很快就…）▼
It + take(s)/took + (time) + for sb. + to V.

It 為虛主詞，真主詞是 to V；for sb. 表示動作者。「不花多久」用 not take long。

It didn't take long for Nuñez to realize that everyone was blind.
Nuñez 沒過多久就意識到每個人都是盲人。（摘要）
It takes about ten minutes to walk to the village.
走到村莊大約要十分鐘。
✗ It didn't take long for him realizing...（真主詞須用 to V，不用 V-ing）。
近義：It won't be long before + S + V（不久後就…）；學測常考轉換。
句型四｜Though + adj.（讓步子句的省略）▼
Though/Although (+ S + be) + adj., S + V ...

當讓步子句主詞與主句相同時，可省略「主詞＋be」，只留 Though + 形容詞。

Though blind, the villagers knew their home intimately.
儘管眼盲，村民對家園瞭若指掌。（摘要）
Although tired, he kept exploring the strange land.
雖然疲累，他仍持續探索這片陌生土地。
✗ 讓步連接詞與 but 不可並用（Though blind, but they knew...）。
同義倒裝：Blind though they were, ...（形容詞提前＋though）；學測進階句型。
句型五｜by + V-ing（表方法／手段）▼
S + V ... + by + V-ing ...（藉由做…）

介系詞 by 後接動名詞，表達「藉由某種方法」完成某事。

They moved around by using their keen senses of hearing and touch.
他們藉由敏銳的聽覺與觸覺四處移動。（摘要）
She improved her English by reading a story every night.
她藉由每晚讀一則故事來提升英文。
✗ by 後接原形（by use their senses）；介系詞後動詞須用 V-ing。
