神話故事速覽（三幕劇情）

🔥 第一幕｜造人與賜禮

泰坦 Prometheus 用黏土造人；弟弟 Epimetheus 把速度、力量、毛皮、翅膀都分給動物，輪到人類時禮物已發完。Prometheus 讓人類能直立，還慷慨賜火，使他們能取暖、煮食、造工具。

🐂 第二幕｜騙宙斯的獻祭

在 Mecone 分配獻祭時，Prometheus 把好肉藏進牛胃、把骨頭裹上油脂，誘宙斯選了骨頭。從此人類留肉、燒骨獻神——這份「愛人類」讓他與宙斯結下衝突。

⛓️ 第三幕｜盜火與永恆懲罰

宙斯震怒收回火，人類挨餓受凍。Prometheus 用茴香莖藏日火再次盜火給人，卻被宙斯放逐、鎖於巨岩，任鷹每日啄食其肝、日夜受苦。他因犧牲被後世尊為「人類的守護者」。

本課取材自希臘神話，敘述泰坦神 Prometheus（普羅米修斯）造人、盜火給人類，最終甘願受罰的故事。全文以敘事體呈現，依情節分為三幕。

🔥 第一幕｜造人與賜禮（The Gifts to Humankind）
▶At a time when there were all-powerful gods in the universe, a Titan known as Prometheus created human beings out of clay.
在宇宙間眾神全能的時代，一位名叫普羅米修斯的泰坦用黏土創造了人類。
🔍 句構分析 ①At a time when there were all-powerful gods in the universe時間副詞（含 when 關係子句）
②a Titan known as PrometheusS createdV human beingsO out of clay副詞（材料）
known as = 被稱為（過去分詞片語修飾 Titan）；out of clay = 用黏土（材料）；universe 為核心字。
▶His brother Epimetheus then gave all living creatures on earth different qualities.
他的弟弟埃庇米修斯接著賦予地球上所有生物不同的特質。
🔍 句構分析 ①His brother EpimetheusS gaveV（授與動詞） all living creatures on earthIO 間接受詞 different qualitiesDO 直接受詞
give + 人 + 物（授與動詞雙受詞）；creature（生物）、quality（特質）為補充字。
▶Epimetheus gave animals speed, strength, fur, and wings.
埃庇米修斯給了動物速度、力量、毛皮和翅膀。
🔍 句構分析 ①EpimetheusS gaveV animalsIO speed, strength, fur, and wingsDO（四名詞並列）
fur（毛皮）為核心字；四個名詞以逗號並列、最後用 and 連接。
▶By the time he got to human beings, however, he had run out of gifts.
然而，等他輪到人類時，他已經把禮物發完了。
🔍 句構分析 ①By the time he got to human beings時間副詞子句（過去式）
②heS had run out ofV（過去完成） giftsO— By the time + 過去式，主句用過去完成，見句型三
run out of = 用完（片語）；had run out 過去完成，強調更早已完成。
▶To make up for their weaker state, Prometheus enabled them to stand upright like the gods.
為了彌補他們較弱的處境，普羅米修斯讓他們能像神一樣直立行走。
🔍 句構分析 ①To make up for their weaker state不定詞（表目的） PrometheusS enabledV themO to stand uprightOC 受詞補語 like the gods副詞
make up for = 彌補；enable sb. to V = 使某人能夠…（見句型五對照）；upright（直立的）為核心字。
▶Not only kind but also generous, he gave them fire too so that they could keep warm, cook food, and make tools.
他不僅仁慈而且慷慨，還給了他們火，好讓他們能取暖、煮食和製作工具。
🔍 句構分析 ①Not only kind but also generous形容詞片語（修飾 he） heS gaveV themIO fireDO
②so that they could keep warm, cook food, and make tools目的副詞子句
not only A but also B（不僅…而且…，句型一）；so that + 子句 = 以便（表目的，句型二）；generous 為核心字。
🐂 第二幕｜騙宙斯的獻祭（The Trick at Mecone）
▶However, Prometheus's love for humankind brought him into conflict with Zeus, the king of the Olympian gods.
然而，普羅米修斯對人類的愛使他與奧林帕斯眾神之王宙斯起了衝突。
🔍 句構分析 ①Prometheus's love for humankindS broughtV himO into conflict with Zeus副詞 , the king of the Olympian gods同位語
bring sb. into conflict with = 使某人與…衝突；conflict、humankind 為核心/補充字；the king... 為 Zeus 的同位語。
▶When the gods and humans met at Mecone to decide on the distribution of sacrifices that humans should make to the gods, Prometheus tricked Zeus for the benefit of humans.
當眾神與人類在梅科涅相聚、要決定人類該向神獻上哪些祭品時，普羅米修斯為了人類的利益而欺騙了宙斯。
🔍 句構分析 ①When the gods and humans met at Mecone to decide on the distribution of sacrifices [that humans should make to the gods]時間副詞子句（含限定關係子句）
②PrometheusS trickedV ZeusO for the benefit of humans副詞
decide on = 決定；distribution（分配）、benefit（利益）為核心字；for the benefit of = 為了…的利益；that 子句見句型五（限定）。
▶He slaughtered a bull and created two offerings.
他宰殺了一頭公牛，做出兩份祭品。
🔍 句構分析 ①HeS slaughteredV a bullO and連接詞 createdV two offeringsO
slaughter（屠宰）為核心字；offering = 祭品；兩個動詞共用主詞 He。
▶One was a pile of beef hidden inside a bull's stomach, and the other was a pile of bones covered in shiny fat.
一份是藏在牛胃裡的一堆牛肉，另一份是裹著發亮油脂的一堆骨頭。
🔍 句構分析 ①OneS wasV a pile of beefC hidden inside a bull's stomach過去分詞片語修飾
②the otherS wasV a pile of bonesC covered in shiny fat過去分詞片語修飾
One... the other... = （兩者）一個…另一個…；hidden / covered 為過去分詞片語（被動）修飾名詞。
▶He then asked Zeus to choose one.
接著他請宙斯選一份。
🔍 句構分析 ①HeS then askedV ZeusO to choose oneOC（不定詞）
ask sb. to V = 要求某人做…（受詞補語用不定詞）；one 代替前面的 offering。
▶The king of the gods chose the second one, believing there must be juicy meat underneath the fat, but instead he ended up with nothing but bones.
眾神之王選了第二份，以為油脂底下一定有多汁的肉，結果卻只落得一堆骨頭。
🔍 句構分析 ①The king of the godsS choseV the second oneO , believing there must be juicy meat underneath the fat分詞構句（附帶狀況）
②but instead轉折 heS ended up withV nothing but bonesO
believing... 為現在分詞構句；underneath（在…下方）為補充字；end up with = 最後落得；nothing but = 只不過。
▶As a result, from that day on, humans would keep the meat for themselves and burn animal bones as an offering to the gods.
結果，從那天起，人類會把肉留給自己，而燒動物的骨頭作為獻給神的祭品。
🔍 句構分析 ①As a result副詞 from that day on時間 humansS would keepV the meatO and burnV animal bonesO as an offering副詞
as a result = 結果（片語）；from that day on = 從那天起；would 表過去習慣。
⛓️ 第三幕｜盜火、懲罰與封號（Theft, Punishment & Glory）
▶Angry about being tricked, Zeus took fire away from humankind.
因為被騙而生氣，宙斯把火從人類手中奪走。
🔍 句構分析 ①Angry about being tricked形容詞片語（分詞構句，表原因） ZeusS tookV fireO away from humankind副詞
Angry about... = 因…而生氣（形容詞引導分詞構句，見句型四）；being tricked = 被騙（動名詞被動）；take...away from = 從…奪走。
▶Without it, humans began to starve and freeze.
沒有了火，人類開始挨餓受凍。
🔍 句構分析 ①Without it條件副詞 humansS beganV to starve and freezeO（不定詞）
without it = 沒有它（＝火）；begin to V；freeze（受凍、結凍）為核心字。
▶Unable to make tools or weapons, they had to live in caves and often fell victim to wild beasts.
由於無法製作工具或武器，他們只能住在洞穴裡，還常成為野獸的犧牲品。
🔍 句構分析 ①Unable to make tools or weapons形容詞片語（表原因） theyS had to liveV in caves副詞 and often fell victim toV wild beastsO
Unable to... = 由於無法…（形容詞引導分詞構句，句型四）；fall victim to = 成為…的受害者（片語）；victim 為核心字；weapon 為補充字。
▶Prometheus saw how miserable they were and decided to help.
普羅米修斯看見他們多麼悲慘，便決定出手相助。
🔍 句構分析 ①PrometheusS sawV how miserable they wereO（名詞子句） and decidedV to helpO（不定詞）
how + adj. + S + V（名詞子句作受詞）；miserable（悲慘的）為核心字；decide to V。
▶He took a stalk of fennel and lit its hollow center with fire that he had stolen from the sun.
他取來一根茴香莖，用他從太陽偷來的火點燃了它中空的莖心。
🔍 句構分析 ①HeS tookV a stalk of fennelO and litV its hollow centerO with fire [that he had stolen from the sun]副詞（含限定關係子句）
hollow（中空的）為補充字；that he had stolen... 關係子句修飾 fire，用過去完成（偷在點燃之前）。
▶Then, he gave humans this stalk with the fire inside it.
然後，他把這根裡面藏著火的莖交給人類。
🔍 句構分析 ①heS gaveV humansIO this stalkDO with the fire inside it介系詞片語修飾 stalk
give + 人 + 物；with the fire inside it = 裡面有火的（附帶說明）。
▶Furious about Prometheus's theft, Zeus punished the Titan by banishing him to a place far to the east.
宙斯對普羅米修斯的偷竊勃然大怒，以將他放逐到遙遠東方來懲罰這位泰坦。
🔍 句構分析 ①Furious about Prometheus's theft形容詞片語（分詞構句） ZeusS punishedV the TitanO by banishing him to a place far to the east副詞（表方法）
furious（狂怒的）、theft（偷竊）、punish（懲罰）、banish（放逐）皆為核心字；by + V-ing = 藉由…。
▶There, Prometheus was chained to a rock, and an eagle sent by Zeus fed on Prometheus's liver.
在那裡，普羅米修斯被鎖在一塊岩石上，而宙斯派來的一隻鷹啄食他的肝臟。
🔍 句構分析 ①PrometheusS was chainedV（被動） to a rock副詞
②an eagle sent by ZeusS（含過去分詞修飾） fed onV（片語動詞） Prometheus's liverO
be chained to = 被鎖在…；sent by Zeus 過去分詞片語（被動）修飾 eagle；feed on = 以…為食（片語）。
▶Though his liver kept growing back, the eagle would return every day to gnaw at it again, torturing Prometheus until the end of time.
儘管他的肝不斷長回來，那隻鷹每天都會回來再次啃咬它，折磨普羅米修斯直到永遠。
🔍 句構分析 ①Though his liver kept growing back讓步副詞子句
②the eagleS would returnV every day to gnaw at it again副詞 , torturing Prometheus until the end of time分詞構句（表結果）
though = 儘管（讓步）；keep + V-ing = 持續…；torture（折磨）為補充字；torturing... 分詞構句表接續結果。
▶Through his sacrifice, Prometheus, who gave humans not only the ability to stand upright but also the gift of fire, which set the stage for human civilization, came to be known in later centuries as the "Champion of Humankind."
透過他的犧牲，普羅米修斯——這位不僅賜給人類直立能力、更給了人類火（一份為人類文明揭開序幕之禮）的神——在後世被稱為「人類的守護者」。
🔍 句構分析 ①Through his sacrifice副詞 PrometheusS , who gave humans not only the ability... but also the gift of fire, which set the stage for human civilization,非限定關係子句（插入） came to be knownV as the "Champion of Humankind"補語
who... / which... 皆為非限定關係子句（見句型五）；not only A but also B；set the stage for = 為…鋪路；civilization（文明）為補充字；come to be known as = 後來被稱為。

She stole my thunder by announcing the news first.她搶先宣布消息，搶走了我的風頭。
add fuel to the fire
火上加油
▶
讓原本已經很糟的情況變得更嚴重。
Shouting back just added fuel to the fire.回嗆只是火上加油。
an Achilles' heel
致命弱點（源自希臘神話）
▶
看似強大者身上唯一的罩門、致命的弱點。
Careless spelling is his Achilles' heel in writing.粗心拼字是他寫作的致命弱點。
a slap on the wrist
輕微的懲罰（對比宙斯的重罰）
▶
罰得太輕、不痛不癢，起不了嚇阻作用。
For such a big mistake, a small fine was just a slap on the wrist.犯這麼大的錯，小額罰款只是不痛不癢。
bite the bullet
咬牙硬撐、勇敢面對
▶
忍痛去做不得不做的難事或苦差事。
Prometheus bit the bullet and accepted his punishment.普羅米修斯咬牙承受了他的懲罰。

核心句型文法解析
👆 點擊每個句型標題，展開「公式 + 例句 + 常見錯誤 + 學測重點」。
句型一｜not only A but also B（不僅…而且…）▼
not only + A but (also) + B（A、B 詞性/結構須對等）

連接兩個對等成分；also 可省略，but 不可省略。

Not only kind but also generous, Prometheus gave humans fire.
普羅米修斯不僅仁慈而且慷慨，給了人類火。（課文）
He gave humans not only the ability to stand upright but also the gift of fire.
他不僅給人類直立的能力，也給了火。（課文）
✗ not only...and also...（連接詞用錯，須用 but also）。
句首倒裝：Not only did he give fire, but he also taught skills. 學測常考。
句型二｜so that（目的）vs so...that（結果）▼
S + V + so that + S + can/could + V（表目的） S + V + so + adj./adv. + that + S + V（表結果）

so that 引導「目的」；so...that 中間夾形容詞/副詞、引導「結果」。

He gave them fire so that they could keep warm.
他給他們火，好讓他們取暖。（課文，目的）
Zeus was so angry that he took the fire away.
宙斯如此生氣，以至於收回了火。（結果）
✗ 混用：so that 後不夾形容詞；so...that 不表目的（後不接 can 表意圖）。
目的亦可用 in order that / in order to V；學測常考 so that vs so...that 辨析。
句型三｜By the time + 過去式 → 主句用過去完成▼
By the time + S + 過去式, S + had + 過去分詞.

「等到…的時候，(更早)已經…」；先發生的動作用過去完成式。

By the time he got to humans, he had run out of gifts.
等他輪到人類時，禮物早已發完。（課文）
By the time we arrived, the show had already started.
我們到時，表演已經開始了。
✗ By the time he got there, he ran out...（漏用過去完成，時序不清）。
對照未來：By the time（未來）主句用未來完成 will have + p.p.。
句型四｜分詞構句／形容詞片語當狀語（句首修飾主詞）▼
V-ing / p.p. / Adj. + ..., S + V ...（主動用 V-ing，被動用 p.p.）

句首片語修飾緊接其後的主詞；邏輯主詞須與主句主詞一致。

Angry about being tricked, Zeus took fire away.
因被騙而生氣，宙斯奪走了火。（形容詞片語，課文）
Unable to make tools, they lived in caves.
由於無法製作工具，他們住在洞穴。（課文）
✗ 懸垂分詞：分詞的邏輯主詞若與主句主詞不一致即錯。
主動 V-ing、被動 p.p.；學測綜合測驗常考句首分詞構句。
句型五｜關係子句：限定 vs 非限定（有無逗號）▼
先行詞 + who/which/that + ...（限定，無逗號） 先行詞, who/which, ...（非限定，有逗號、補充說明）

限定子句界定範圍；非限定子句補充說明，且 that 不可引導非限定子句。

the sacrifices that humans should make to the gods
人類該獻給神的祭品（限定，課文）
Prometheus, who gave humans fire, which set the stage for civilization...
普羅米修斯——他給了人類火，而火為文明鋪路…（非限定，課文）
✗ 非限定子句用 that（Prometheus, that gave...）為錯。
which 可代替整個前句（...fire, which set the stage...）；學測常考關代選填與逗號判斷。
