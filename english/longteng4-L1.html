故事速覽（三幕）

🎲 第一幕｜打賭的起點

秋夜裡，老銀行家想起十五年前的一場宴會。賓客爭論死刑與終身監禁哪個較殘酷：銀行家認為痛快一死較仁慈，年輕律師卻認為活著總比死好。爭執升溫，銀行家拿出兩百萬盧布打賭律師撐不過五年，律師反而堅持加碼到十五年。

📚 第二幕｜漫長的監禁

律師被關進銀行家莊園裡的小屋，只能透過小窗傳紙條索取酒、菸、樂器等物品。物質享受填不滿孤寂，他轉而埋首讀書；從第六年起日夜苦讀歷史、哲學，四年讀了數百冊，精通多種語言、學識深厚。

💌 第三幕｜出人意料的結局

銀行家卻瀕臨破產，無法忍受把剩下的財產輸給律師，竟起了殺意。期滿前一晚他潛入小屋，發現律師留下一封親筆信：他看透財富的虛幻，決定放棄賭金。銀行家羞愧離去；律師在期滿前幾小時從窗戶離開、從此音訊全無，銀行家則把信鎖進保險箱以免流言。

🔍 句構顏色對照： 主詞 S　動詞 V　受詞 O　補語 C　修飾語

本課改寫自契訶夫（Anton Chekhov）的短篇小說，探討自由、金錢與人生價值。以下摘要以教師自行撰寫的英文呈現（非課文原句），依「打賭的起點／漫長的監禁／出人意料的結局」三幕分段。

🎲 第一幕｜打賭的起點（How the Bet Began）
▶The story opens with an aging banker who cannot sleep one autumn night because a memory from fifteen years ago keeps troubling him.
故事從一位年邁的銀行家開始：某個秋夜他輾轉難眠，因為一段十五年前的回憶不斷困擾著他。
🔍 句構分析 ①The storyS opensV with an aging banker [who cannot sleep one autumn night]副詞（含關係子句）
②because連接詞 a memory from fifteen years agoS keeps troublingV himO
who 引導形容詞子句修飾 banker；keep + V-ing = 持續…；aging = 年老的。課文用 back and forth（來回地）描寫他焦躁踱步（見片語）。
▶At a dinner party back then, the guests had argued about which punishment was crueler: putting a criminal to death or locking him up for the rest of his life.
當年在一場晚宴上，賓客們曾爭論哪一種刑罰比較殘酷：把罪犯處死，還是把他關到終老。
🔍 句構分析 ①At a dinner party back then副詞 the guestsS had arguedV（過去完成式） about which punishment was crueler介系詞＋名詞子句
②: putting a criminal to death or locking him up ...同位語（兩個動名詞片語）
had argued 過去完成式：回憶「更早」發生的事（見句型六）；put sb. to death = 處死；主題字 debate（辯論）、the death penalty（死刑）、life imprisonment（終身監禁）。
▶The banker felt that a quick death was more merciful than endless years behind bars, but a young lawyer argued that even a life spent in a cell was better than no life at all.
銀行家認為痛快一死比無止盡的牢獄歲月更仁慈，但一位年輕律師主張，即使在牢房裡度過一生，也比完全沒有生命好。
🔍 句構分析 ①The bankerS feltV that a quick death was more merciful than endless years behind barsO（名詞子句）
②a young lawyerS arguedV that even a life [spent in a cell] was better than no life at allO（名詞子句，含過去分詞片語）
behind bars = 坐牢；more merciful than 比較級；spent in a cell 過去分詞片語修飾 life；律師的立場可用 would rather + V1 + than + V2（寧願…也不…）表達（見句型二）。
▶Tempers rose so high that the banker put two million rubles on the table, betting that the lawyer could not endure five years of solitary confinement.
雙方火氣越來越大，銀行家甚至拿出兩百萬盧布，賭律師撐不過五年的單獨監禁。
🔍 句構分析 ①TempersS roseV so high副詞 that ...結果子句
②the bankerS putV two million rublesO on the table副詞 , betting that the lawyer could not endure ...分詞構句（主動）
so + adj./adv. + that = 如此…以致於；bet (that) S + V = 打賭…；solitary confinement = 單獨監禁（補充字 solitary）；endure = 忍受、撐過。
▶Instead of backing down, the lawyer boldly demanded fifteen years, and soon afterward he was shut inside a cabin that stood on the banker's estate to start his long confinement.
律師非但沒有退縮，反而大膽要求把期限拉長到十五年；不久後，他便被關進銀行家莊園裡的一間小屋，展開漫長的監禁。
🔍 句構分析 ①Instead of backing down副詞（介系詞＋動名詞） the lawyerS boldly副詞 demandedV fifteen yearsO
②heS was shutV（被動） inside a cabin [that stood on the banker's estate]地方副詞（含關係子句） to start his long confinement副詞（不定詞表目的）
back down = 讓步、退縮；instead of + V-ing；estate 莊園 ≈ property（地產，核心字）；律師「堅持」加碼可用 insist（核心字）。
📚 第二幕｜漫長的監禁（Fifteen Years Alone）
▶The lawyer could see no one and speak to no one; notes slipped out through a tiny window were the only way he could order whatever he wanted.
律師見不到任何人，也不能和任何人說話；從小窗遞出的紙條，是他索取任何想要之物的唯一方法。
🔍 句構分析 ①The lawyerS could seeV no oneO and speak toV no oneO
②notes [slipped out through a tiny window]S（含過去分詞片語） wereV the only way [he could order whatever he wanted]C
no one 表全部否定；whatever = anything that（任何…的東西）；分號（;）連接兩個語意緊密的獨立子句。
▶Even with every comfort supplied, he suffered from deep loneliness and slowly discovered that wine and music only left him feeling empty.
即使各種舒適享受一應俱全，他仍深受孤寂之苦，並漸漸發現美酒和音樂只讓他感到空虛。
🔍 句構分析 ①Even with every comfort supplied副詞（with + O + p.p.） heS suffered fromV deep lonelinessO
②slowly副詞 discoveredV that wine and music only left him feeling emptyO（名詞子句）
with + N + p.p. 表附帶狀況；leave + O + V-ing / adj. = 使…處於某狀態；suffer from = 受…之苦；時間一天天慢慢過去可用片語 wear on 表達（見片語）。
▶Tired of these empty pleasures, he set them aside and turned to reading for comfort.
厭倦了這些空洞的享樂，他把它們擱在一旁，轉向閱讀尋求慰藉。
🔍 句構分析 ①Tired of these empty pleasures分詞構句（省略 Being，表原因） heS setV themO aside副詞
②and turned toV（片語） readingO（動名詞） for comfort副詞
(Being) tired of ... 省略 being 的分詞構句（見句型五）；set aside = 擱置；turn to = 轉向、求助於；書本成了他的 companion（同伴，核心字）。
▶Starting in his sixth year, he studied with amazing discipline, and within four years he had read hundreds of books on history, philosophy, and many other fields.
從第六年起，他以驚人的自律埋首研讀；四年之內，他已讀完數百本涵蓋歷史、哲學等眾多領域的書。
🔍 句構分析 ①Starting in his sixth year分詞構句 heS studiedV with amazing discipline副詞
②within four years副詞 heS had readV（過去完成式） hundreds of books [on history, philosophy, and many other fields]O
within + 時間 = 在…之內；had read 表「到過去某時點為止已完成」；如飢似渴地讀書可用 devour（核心字）；日夜不停 = around the clock（片語）；一冊書 = a volume。
▶Thanks to this effort, he became fluent in a number of languages and grew truly wise, and this inner wealth came to mean more to him than any amount of money.
多虧這份努力，他能流利使用多種語言、變得真正睿智，而這份內在的財富對他而言，逐漸比任何金錢都更有意義。
🔍 句構分析 ①heS becameV（連綴） fluent in a number of languagesC and grewV（連綴） truly wiseC
②this inner wealthS came to meanV moreO to him than any amount of money副詞（比較）
become / grow + adj. 連綴動詞接補語；come to + V = 逐漸…；a number of + 複數名詞；他 acquire（習得）了 profound（深厚的）知識，兩者皆為核心字。
💌 第三幕｜出人意料的結局（An Unexpected Ending）
▶Meanwhile, time had not been kind to the banker: his once great fortune had nearly disappeared, and paying the bet would ruin him completely.
與此同時，歲月並未善待銀行家：他曾經龐大的財富幾乎消失殆盡，若付出賭金，他將徹底破產。
🔍 句構分析 ①timeS had not beenV（過去完成式） kindC to the banker副詞
②his once great fortuneS had nearly disappearedV
③paying the betS（動名詞） would ruinV himO completely副詞
once = 曾經（副詞，修飾 great）；go bankrupt = 破產（補充字 bankrupt）；wealth / wealthy（財富／富有的）為補充字；冒號（:）後接說明。
▶Unable to accept that his remaining money would go to the lawyer, the banker decided to kill the man before the deadline arrived.
無法接受自己剩下的錢將歸律師所有，銀行家決定在期限到來之前殺了他。
🔍 句構分析 ①Unable to accept that his remaining money would go to the lawyer分詞構句（省略 Being，表原因）
②the bankerS decidedV to kill the manO（不定詞） before the deadline arrived時間副詞子句
(Being) unable to V 表原因；decide to V；殺人的念頭「閃過腦海」= cross one's mind；他「絕對無法」忍受 = by no means（皆見片語）；murder = 謀殺。
▶On the last night of the bet, the banker slipped into the cabin, only to discover that the lawyer had fallen asleep next to a note intended for him.
打賭的最後一晚，銀行家悄悄溜進小屋，卻發現律師已在一張留給他的紙條旁睡著了。
🔍 句構分析 ①On the last night of the bet副詞 the bankerS slipped intoV the cabinO
②, only to discover that the lawyer had fallen asleep next to a note [intended for him]不定詞表意外結果（含名詞子句）
only to V = 結果卻…（出乎意料，見句型八）；had fallen asleep 過去完成式；intended for = 給…的；課文以倒裝描寫桌上的信（見句型七）。
▶In the note, the lawyer explained that years of reading had shown him how empty money and fame were, so he would give up the prize of his own free will.
律師在紙條中說明，多年的閱讀讓他看清金錢與名聲有多空虛，因此他將出於自願放棄這筆獎金。
🔍 句構分析 ①In the note副詞 the lawyerS explainedV that years of reading had shown him how empty money and fame wereO（名詞子句：show + IO + DO）
②heS would give upV（片語） the prizeO of his own free will副詞
how + adj. + S + V 間接問句（語序不倒裝）；give up ≈ abandon / surrender（核心字）；of one's own free will = 出於自願；他對世俗財富的 disgust（厭惡）與看透一切 vain（虛幻）也是核心字。
▶Filled with shame, the banker crept away; hours later the lawyer climbed out of the window and vanished, so the banker hid the note in his safe to avoid gossip.
滿懷羞愧，銀行家悄悄離開；幾小時後律師從窗戶爬出去、從此消失無蹤，於是銀行家把紙條藏進保險箱，以免引來閒話。
🔍 句構分析 ①Filled with shame分詞構句（被動） the bankerS crept awayV
②hours later副詞 the lawyerS climbed out ofV the windowO and vanishedV
③the bankerS hidV the noteO in his safe副詞 to avoid gossip副詞（不定詞表目的）
be filled with = 充滿；羞愧內疚 = guilt（核心字）；gossip ≈ rumor（謠言，補充字）；safe 作名詞為「保險箱」，一字多義。

核心句型文法解析
👆 點擊每個句型標題，展開「公式 + 例句 + 常見錯誤 + 學測重點」。
句型一｜(As) + adj. + as + S + V（讓步：雖然…）▼
(As) + adj./adv. + as + S + V, S + V.（= Although + S + V, ...）

把形容詞或副詞移到句首，表示「雖然…，但…」；美式常在句首多加一個 as，英式多省略。

As tired as she was, she kept practicing the piano.
雖然很累，她仍持續練琴。
Rich as he is, he never feels satisfied.
他雖然富有，卻從不感到滿足。
✗ As tired as she was, but she kept practicing.（句型本身已表讓步，不可再加 but）。
可與 Although / Though 子句互換；名詞置首時要去冠詞：Child as he was, ...。學測句子改寫常考。
句型二｜would rather + V1 + than + V2（寧願…也不…）▼
S + would rather + 原形V1 + than + 原形V2

would rather 表偏好，前後兩個動詞都用原形；縮寫為 I'd rather。

I would rather walk home than wait for the late bus.
我寧願走路回家，也不要等那班誤點的公車。
She would rather stay poor than tell a lie.
她寧可窮困，也不願說謊。
✗ would rather to walk than waiting（would rather 與 than 後都須接原形動詞）。
= prefer V-ing to V-ing = prefer to V rather than V；would rather + S + 過去式 表「寧願別人…」。
句型三｜too + adj. + (for sb.) + to V（太…而不能…）▼
S + be + (far/much) too + adj. + (for sb.) + to V

too ... to 本身帶有否定意味；要加強語氣時在 too 前加 far 或 much。

This box is too heavy for me to lift.
這個箱子太重，我搬不動。
Some childhood memories are far too sweet to forget.
有些童年回憶甜美得讓人忘不了。
✗ This box is too heavy for me to lift it.（不定詞的受詞就是句子主詞，句尾不可再加 it）。
可改寫為 so + adj. + that + S + can't + V；學測綜合測驗與改寫常考。
句型四｜propose / suggest / insist that + S + (should) + 原形V▼
S + propose / suggest / insist / demand + that + S + (should) + 原形V

表「建議、要求、堅持」的動詞，that 子句用 (should) + 原形；should 常省略，但動詞仍維持原形。

The teacher proposed that each student write a short story.
老師提議每位學生寫一篇短篇故事。
My father insisted that I (should) apologize to my sister.
爸爸堅持要我向妹妹道歉。
✗ The teacher proposed that each student writes a story.（省略 should 後仍須用原形 write）。
若 insist / suggest 表「堅稱、暗示某事實」，that 子句用一般時態：He insisted that he was innocent.
句型五｜分詞構句（V-ing 主動／p.p. 被動／Having p.p. 先發生）▼
V-ing ..., S + V（主動） p.p. ..., S + V（被動） Having + p.p. ..., S + V（動作先發生）

分詞構句的意義上主詞必須與主要子句的主詞相同；Unable to ...、Tired of ... 則是省略 Being 的形容詞片語。

Locked in her room, the girl wrote long letters to her friends.
被鎖在房間裡，女孩寫了好幾封長信給朋友。
Having finished one novel, he started another right away.
讀完一本小說後，他立刻開始讀下一本。
✗ Having finished the novel, a new one was started.（主詞不一致，形成懸垂分詞）。
先判斷主詞與分詞是主動或被動，再看動作先後；學測改寫與綜合測驗高頻。
句型六｜過去完成式 had + p.p.（過去的過去）▼
By the time / When / Before + S + 過去式, S + had + p.p.

兩件過去的事，較早發生的用 had + p.p.；故事倒敘、回憶往事時特別常見。

By the time we arrived, the movie had already started.
我們到的時候，電影已經開演了。
She suddenly realized that she had left her keys at school.
她突然發現自己把鑰匙忘在學校了。
✗ By the time we arrived, the movie has started.（主要子句應用過去完成式 had started）。
by the time + 過去式 → 主要子句 had + p.p.；by the time + 現在式 → 主要子句 will have + p.p.。
句型七｜分詞置首倒裝（Sitting in the corner was ...）▼
V-ing / p.p. + 地方副詞 + be + S

為了強調畫面，把分詞片語放句首，be 動詞接著出現，真正的主詞放最後；be 動詞須與後方主詞一致。

Sitting in the corner was an old man with a gray beard.
角落裡坐著一位留著灰鬍子的老人。
Hidden under the bed were two boxes of old photos.
床底下藏著兩箱舊照片。
✗ Hidden under the bed was two boxes of old photos.（主詞 two boxes 為複數，應用 were）。
還原語序：An old man was sitting in the corner.；故事描寫與學測閱讀常見。
句型八｜..., only to + V（結果卻…，出乎意料）▼
S + V ..., only to + 原形V

only to V 表示「令人失望或意外的結果」，與表目的的 to V 不同。

I hurried to the station, only to find that the train had left.
我匆忙趕到車站，結果火車已經開走了。
He studied all night, only to fall asleep during the test.
他熬夜讀書，結果考試時睡著了。
✗ ..., only finding / only to found（only to 後須接原形動詞）。
only to find / discover / realize 最常見；前面通常有逗號。
