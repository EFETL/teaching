課文速覽（三大重點）

🖋️ 詩人與緣起

美國詩人 Robert Frost 以樸素語言寫深刻哲思。名作〈The Road Not Taken〉1915 年發表，靈感來自好友 Edward Thomas 在林間岔路總猶豫不決；此詩是對其猶豫的溫柔評論。

🌲 詩作與意象

黃樹林中兩條岔路成為人生重大抉擇的隱喻。前兩節寫選擇的侷限（只能走一條），第三節寫成熟接受抉擇的不可逆，末節那聲「嘆息」留下多重解讀空間。

🧭 主題與啟示

詩的雋永在於直探抉擇的本質：意義不在選到「完美」的路，而在我們日後如何述說自己的決定。它與每個面對難題的人心有戚戚，成為不朽經典。

🖋️ 導論｜關於 Robert Frost
▶As one of America's most beloved poets, Robert Frost is known for using simple language to express deep ideas.
身為美國最受喜愛的詩人之一，羅伯特·佛洛斯特以用簡單語言表達深刻思想而聞名。
🔍 句構分析 ①As one of America's most beloved poets介系詞片語（身分） Robert FrostS is known forV（被動片語） using simple language to express deep ideasO（動名詞）
be known for + N/V-ing = 以…聞名；beloved = 深受喜愛的。
▶Unlike many of his contemporaries, who favored complicated language, Frost wrote in a style that could be understood by readers from all walks of life.
不同於許多偏好複雜語言的同時代詩人，佛洛斯特以一種各行各業讀者都能理解的風格寫作。
🔍 句構分析 ①Unlike many of his contemporaries [, who favored complicated language,]介系詞片語（含非限定關係子句） FrostS wroteV in a style [that could be understood by readers from all walks of life]副詞（含關係子句）
contemporary（同時代的人）、complicated（複雜的）為核心字；all walks of life = 各行各業；who / that 關係子句見句型三。
▶His poetry appears as plain as everyday conversation.
他的詩讀來就像日常對話一樣樸實。
🔍 句構分析 ①His poetryS appearsV（連綴動詞） as plain as everyday conversationC（as...as 同級比較）
appear + adj. = 顯得…；as plain as = 和…一樣樸素；plain（樸素的、清楚的）為核心字。
▶However, beneath this simplicity lie penetrating insights into life.
然而，在這份樸實之下，蘊藏著對人生深刻的洞見。
🔍 句構分析 ①beneath this simplicity地方副詞（置句首） lieV penetrating insights into lifeS（真主詞）— 地方副詞置首的倒裝，動詞 lie 與複數主詞一致，見句型五
倒裝：地方副詞置句首 → 動詞在主詞前（句型五）；penetrating（深刻的）為核心字。
▶One of his most renowned works, "The Road Not Taken," exemplifies his outstanding ability to draw profound meaning from ordinary experiences.
他最著名的作品之一〈未行之路〉，正體現了他從平凡經驗中汲取深刻意義的傑出能力。
🔍 句構分析 ①One of his most renowned works, "The Road Not Taken,"S（含同位語） exemplifiesV his outstanding ability to draw profound meaning from ordinary experiencesO
renowned（著名的）、outstanding（傑出的）為核心字；exemplify（例證）、profound（深刻的）為補充字；draw...from = 從…汲取。
▶The poem, first published in 1915, originated from Frost's friendship with poet Edward Thomas.
這首詩最早發表於 1915 年，源自佛洛斯特與詩人愛德華·湯瑪斯的友誼。
🔍 句構分析 ①The poemS , first published in 1915,過去分詞片語（插入） originated fromV（片語） Frost's friendship with poet Edward ThomasO
first published in 1915 過去分詞片語作補充；originate from = 源自（片語）；publish / publication 為補充字。
▶During their woodland walks, Thomas would hesitate at every fork, wondering about unexplored routes.
在林間散步時，湯瑪斯總會在每個岔路口猶豫，想著那些沒走過的路。
🔍 句構分析 ①During their woodland walks時間副詞 ThomasS would hesitateV at every fork副詞 , wondering about unexplored routes分詞構句（表附帶）
would 表過去反覆的習慣；wondering... 現在分詞構句（見句型四）；fork = 岔路。
▶Frost composed the poem partly as a gentle comment on his friend's indecision.
佛洛斯特寫這首詩，部分是對好友猶豫不決的一種溫柔評論。

compose（創作）、comment（評論）、indecision（猶豫不決）皆為核心字；comment on = 對…評論。
🌲 詩作本文｜The Road Not Taken（四節）
▶Two roads diverged in a yellow wood, / And sorry I could not travel both / And be one traveler, long I stood / And looked down one as far as I could / To where it bent in the undergrowth;
第一節：金黃樹林裡兩條路分岔，/ 可惜我一人無法同時走兩條，/ 身為孤獨的旅人，我佇立良久，/ 極目遠望其中一條，/ 直到它彎入樹叢深處消失。
🔍 賞析重點 ①Two roadsS divergedV in a yellow wood地方副詞— diverge（分岔）點題
②sorry (that)...情緒形容詞引導：形容詞 sorry 後省略 that，帶出「無法兩條都走」的遺憾。
diverge = 分岔；yellow wood 點出秋天意象；「兩條路」即人生抉擇的核心隱喻。
▶Then took the other, as just as fair, / And having perhaps the better claim, / Because it was grassy and wanted wear; / Though as for that the passing there / Had worn them really about the same,
第二節：於是我選了另一條，一樣美好，/ 也許還更值得一走，/ 因為它綠草如茵、少有人跡；/ 不過就經過的情況而言，/ 兩條路其實被踏得差不多。
🔍 賞析重點 ①tookV（主詞 I 省略） the otherO as just as fairC（as...as）
②wanted wear：want 此處為古義「缺少」，wanted wear＝少有人走。
as just as fair = 一樣地美好；wanted wear（少人踏過）與末節「less traveled」呼應，卻又說兩路「about the same」，埋下反諷。
▶And both that morning equally lay / In leaves no step had trodden black. / Oh, I kept the first for another day! / Yet knowing how way leads on to way, / I doubted if I should ever come back.
第三節：那天早晨兩條路一樣，/ 鋪滿無人踏黑的落葉。/ 啊，我把第一條留待他日！/ 但深知路總是接著路，/ 我懷疑自己是否還能回來。
🔍 賞析重點 ①no step had trodden black關係子句（省略 that）：leaves (that) no step had trodden black，過去完成表更早。
②knowing how way leads on to way分詞構句：明知「路接著路」，回不去了。
tread-trod-trodden（踩踏）；way leads on to way = 一條路通往另一條路（抉擇的不可逆）；此節展現成熟的接受而非單純懊悔。
▶I shall be telling this with a sigh / Somewhere ages and ages hence: / Two roads diverged in a wood, and I— / I took the one less traveled by, / And that has made all the difference.
第四節：許久許久以後的某處，/ 我將會嘆著氣訴說這件事：/ 樹林裡兩條路分岔，而我——/ 我選了人跡較少的那一條，/ 而這造就了一切的不同。
🔍 賞析重點 ①shall be telling未來進行式：想像未來訴說此事。
②and I—破折號：停頓與「嘆息」，暗示欲言又止的情感複雜度。
with a sigh = 帶著一聲嘆息（全詩情感核心）；the one less traveled by = 人跡較少的那條；末句 made all the difference 留下多重解讀。
🧭 賞析一｜主題（Theme）
▶"The Road Not Taken," which on the surface depicts a solitary traveler's journey in an autumn forest, presents a philosophical reflection on life.
〈未行之路〉表面上描寫一位孤獨旅人在秋林中的旅程，實則呈現對人生的哲學省思。
🔍 句構分析 ①"The Road Not Taken"S , which on the surface depicts...,非限定關係子句（插入） presentsV a philosophical reflection on lifeO
depict（描繪）、solitary（孤獨的）為核心字；reflection（省思）為補充字；on the surface = 表面上。
▶The image of two paths diverging in a yellow wood functions as a compelling metaphor for the crucial decisions we face.
黃樹林中兩條岔路的意象，成為我們所面對的重大抉擇的絕佳隱喻。
🔍 句構分析 ①The image of two paths diverging in a yellow woodS functions asV a compelling metaphor for the crucial decisions we faceC
compelling（引人入勝的）為核心字；function as = 起…作用、充當；metaphor for = …的隱喻。
▶The first two stanzas lay out the limits of choice—we can walk only one path and choose only one option, which reflects a universal dilemma at life's crossroads.
前兩節鋪陳出抉擇的侷限——我們只能走一條路、只能選一個選項，這反映了人生十字路口普世的兩難。
🔍 句構分析 ①The first two stanzasS lay outV（片語） the limits of choiceO — ..., which reflects a universal dilemma...破折號補述＋which 非限定子句
lay out = 鋪陳、清楚陳述（片語）；which 代整個前句（見句型三）；crossroads = 十字路口。
▶Like the speaker, we hesitate because our predictions about the future are clouded by uncertainty.
如同詩中說話者，我們之所以猶豫，是因為對未來的預測被不確定性所蒙蔽。
🔍 句構分析 ①Like the speaker介系詞片語 weS hesitateV because our predictions... are clouded by uncertainty原因副詞子句
prediction（預測）為核心字；be clouded by = 被…蒙蔽；like（如同）為介系詞，後接名詞。
▶The third stanza demonstrates mature acceptance of choice's irreversible nature rather than simple regret: "Yet knowing how way leads on to way, / I doubted if I should ever come back."
第三節展現的是對抉擇不可逆本質的成熟接受，而非單純的懊悔：「但深知路總是接著路，/ 我懷疑自己是否還能回來。」
🔍 句構分析 ①The third stanzaS demonstratesV mature acceptance of choice's irreversible natureO rather than simple regret對比
acceptance（接受）為補充字；rather than = 而非；irreversible = 不可逆的。
▶This awareness adds weight to our choices, since we may never learn what the alternative paths might have offered.
這份體認為我們的抉擇增添了份量，因為我們或許永遠無從得知另一條路會帶來什麼。
🔍 句構分析 ①This awarenessS addsV weightO to our choices副詞 since... might have offered原因子句
add weight to = 使…更有份量；might have offered = 過去可能提供（對過去的推測）。
✨ 賞析二｜雋永的魅力（The Poem's Lasting Appeal）
▶The poem's lasting fascination stems from its final stanza, which invites different interpretations.
這首詩歷久不衰的魅力源自它的最後一節，那一節容許了不同的詮釋。
🔍 句構分析 ①The poem's lasting fascinationS stems fromV（片語） its final stanzaO , which invites different interpretations非限定關係子句
stem from = 源自（片語）；invite different interpretations = 引發不同解讀。
▶Some readers celebrate the speaker's declaration, "I took the one less traveled by, / And that has made all the difference," seeing it as encouragement to choose an unconventional path.
有些讀者讚頌說話者的宣告：「我選了人跡較少的那一條，/ 而這造就了一切的不同」，把它視為鼓勵人們選擇不落俗套之路。
🔍 句構分析 ①Some readersS celebrateV the speaker's declaration...O , seeing it as encouragement...分詞構句
declaration（宣告）為補充字；unconventional（不落俗套的）為補充字；see A as B = 把 A 視為 B；seeing... 分詞構句（見句型四）。
▶However, other readers point to earlier lines—"Though as for that the passing there / Had worn them really about the same"—to argue that neither path was genuinely "less traveled."
然而，另一些讀者指出前面的詩句——「不過就經過的情況而言，/ 兩條路其實被踏得差不多」——主張其實沒有哪條路真的「較少人走」。
🔍 句構分析 ①other readersS point toV earlier linesO to argue that neither path was genuinely "less traveled"不定詞（表目的）
point to = 指出；neither = 兩者皆非（接單數）；引號內詩句作為佐證。
▶They suggest that the speaker is simply justifying his decision.
他們認為說話者只是在為自己的決定辯解罷了。
🔍 句構分析 ①TheyS suggestV that the speaker is simply justifying his decisionO（名詞子句）
justify（為…辯解）為核心字；suggest that = 認為、暗示。
▶More importantly, the power of the final stanza lies not only in what the speaker claims but also in how he tells it.
更重要的是，末節的力量不僅在於說話者「說了什麼」，也在於他「怎麼說」。
🔍 句構分析 ①the power of the final stanzaS liesV not only in what the speaker claims but also in how he tells itnot only...but also 並列副詞
not only A but also B（句型一）；lie in = 在於；what / how 引導名詞子句。
▶In the final stanza, the speaker imagines telling the story in the future, and the "sigh" he anticipates displays the poem's emotional complexity.
在最後一節，說話者想像自己未來訴說這個故事，而他所預期的那聲「嘆息」，展現了全詩情感的複雜。
🔍 句構分析 ①the speakerS imaginesV telling the story in the futureO（動名詞）
②the "sigh" [he anticipates]S（含關係子句） displaysV the poem's emotional complexityO
imagine + V-ing（想像做…，接動名詞）；anticipate = 預期；(that) he anticipates 為關係子句修飾 sigh。
▶The emotional weight of this sigh is already present in the pause after "and I—" in the third-to-last line.
這聲嘆息的情感份量，早已藏在倒數第三行「and I—」之後的那個停頓裡。
🔍 句構分析 ①The emotional weight of this sighS isV already presentC in the pause after "and I—"副詞
third-to-last = 倒數第三；present（存在的）此處為形容詞。
▶The dash suggests that the speaker is struggling to find words, making the sigh (echoing the sound of "I") that follows feel natural.
那個破折號暗示說話者正努力尋找措辭，讓緊接而來的嘆息（呼應「I」的發音）顯得十分自然。

struggle to V = 費力做…；make + O + 原形（使役，making the sigh feel natural）；echoing... 插入補述。
▶This sigh could express different things, such as regret, gentle self-criticism, or acceptance of life's uncertainty.
這聲嘆息可能表達不同的情緒，例如懊悔、溫和的自省，或是對人生無常的接受。
🔍 句構分析 ①This sighS could expressV different thingsO , such as regret, ... or acceptance...舉例並列
such as = 例如；三個並列名詞（regret / self-criticism / acceptance）作舉例。
▶Throughout the poem, Frost mixes emotions.
通篇全詩，佛洛斯特將各種情緒交織在一起。
🔍 句構分析 ①Throughout the poem副詞 FrostS mixesV emotionsO

▶The opening conveys loss ("sorry I could not travel both"), while the speaker also weighs each path with anxious care.
開頭傳達出失落（「可惜我無法兩條都走」），同時說話者又以焦慮而審慎的心情衡量每一條路。
🔍 句構分析 ①The openingS conveysV lossO while the speaker also weighs each path with anxious care對比副詞子句

▶This emotional complexity reflects how we really feel when making choices.
這種情感的複雜，正映照出我們做選擇時真實的感受。

🌱 賞析三｜人生啟示（Life Lessons）
▶This poem offers insights for whoever is facing important decisions.
這首詩為任何正面臨重大抉擇的人提供了洞見。
🔍 句構分析 ①This poemS offersV insightsO for whoever is facing important decisions介系詞 + 名詞子句

▶Throughout our lives, we encounter numerous crossroads, such as selecting a university major or deciding on a career path, where we must choose without knowing the final outcome.
終其一生，我們會遇到無數的十字路口，例如選填大學科系或決定職涯方向，在那些時刻我們必須在不知最終結果的情況下做出選擇。

▶Whatever we choose will become part of our personal story.
無論我們選擇什麼，都將成為我們個人故事的一部分。
🔍 句構分析 ①Whatever we chooseS（名詞子句） will becomeV part of our personal storyC

▶The poem suggests that significance lies not in selecting the "perfect" choice but in how we explain our decision later.
這首詩暗示：意義不在於選出「完美」的選項，而在於我們日後如何解釋自己的決定。

▶Rather than emphasizing the importance of not having regrets, the poem prompts us to consider how meaning in life comes from the choices we make.
這首詩並非強調「不留遺憾」的重要，而是促使我們思考：人生的意義如何來自我們所做的抉擇。

▶"The Road Not Taken" has an enduring appeal because it gets at the essence of decision-making.
〈未行之路〉之所以歷久彌新，是因為它直探抉擇的本質。

▶Instead of offering simple answers, the poem invites readers to reflect on the complexity of choice.
這首詩並不提供簡單的答案，而是邀請讀者去省思抉擇的複雜。

核心句型文法解析
👆 點擊每個句型標題，展開「公式 + 例句 + 常見錯誤 + 學測重點」。
句型一｜not only A but also B（不僅…而且…）▼
not only + A but (also) + B（A、B 詞性/結構須對等）

連接對等成分；also 可省，but 不可省。

The power lies not only in what the speaker claims but also in how he tells it.
力量不僅在於他說什麼，也在於他怎麼說。（課文）
✗ not only...and also...（連接詞用錯）。
句首倒裝：Not only did he choose, but he also reflected. 學測常考。
句型二｜地方副詞置句首的倒裝▼
地方副詞(片語) + 動詞 + 主詞（完全倒裝）

地方/方位副詞置句首以強調時，主詞與動詞倒裝；動詞與「真主詞」一致。

Beneath this simplicity lie penetrating insights into life.
在這份樸實之下，蘊藏著深刻的洞見。（課文；主詞 insights 為複數，故用 lie）
Here comes the bus. / On the hill stands a temple.
公車來了。／山上矗立著一座廟。
✗ Beneath this simplicity lies penetrating insights.（主詞複數，動詞不可用 lies）。
主詞為代名詞時不倒裝：Here it comes.（非 Here comes it）。
句型三｜關係子句：限定 vs 非限定▼
先行詞 + who/which/that + ...（限定，無逗號） 先行詞, who/which, ...（非限定，有逗號、補充說明）

限定子句界定範圍；非限定子句補充說明，且 that 不可引導非限定；which 可代整個前句。

a style that could be understood by readers（限定，課文）
一種讀者能理解的風格
his contemporaries, who favored complicated language（非限定，課文）
他的同時代人，他們偏好複雜的語言
✗ 非限定用 that（his contemporaries, that favored...）為錯。
..., which reflects a universal dilemma —which 代前面整句；學測常考。
句型四｜現在分詞片語／分詞構句▼
主句, V-ing ...（表附帶狀況或結果，邏輯主詞＝主句主詞）

用 V-ing 接在主句後表「同時／附帶／結果」，邏輯主詞須與主句主詞一致。

Thomas would hesitate at every fork, wondering about unexplored routes.
湯瑪斯總在每個岔路猶豫，想著沒走過的路。（課文）
It strikes a chord with readers, making it a timeless classic.
它引起共鳴，因而成為不朽經典。（課文，表結果）
✗ 懸垂分詞：分詞邏輯主詞與主句主詞不一致即錯。
主動用 V-ing、被動用 p.p.；學測綜合測驗常考。
句型五｜複合關係代名詞 whoever / whatever▼
whoever / whatever + (S) + V ...（＝anyone who / anything that，引導名詞子句）

複合關係代名詞本身含先行詞，引導名詞子句，可當主詞或受詞。

The poem offers insights for whoever is facing important decisions.
這首詩為任何面臨重大抉擇的人提供洞見。（課文，＝anyone who）
Whatever we choose will become part of our story.
無論我們選什麼都會成為故事的一部分。（課文，作主詞）
✗ 讓步用 no matter what/who 引導副詞子句，勿與名詞子句的 whatever/whoever 混淆位置。
whoever（主格）vs whomever（受格）；學測偶考格位判斷。
