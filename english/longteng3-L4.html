主題速覽（三面向）

🎉 面向一｜節日精神

墨西哥的 亡靈節（Day of the Dead） 以歡樂而非悲傷面對死亡。人們不用哀悼（mourn）去送別逝去的親人，而是熱情地歡迎他們「回家」團聚，把死亡看成生命循環中自然而不可避免的一部分。

🕰️ 面向二｜起源與時間

這項傳統據信數百年前源自阿茲特克人（the Aztecs）。如今全墨西哥於11 月 1 日與 2 日盛大慶祝：1 日紀念逝去的孩童，2 日則以已故的成人為主，相信亡者的靈魂會在這兩天返回人間探望家人。

🌼 面向三｜習俗與供品

家家戶戶事先（in advance）在市集採買萬壽菊、蠟燭、糖骷髏與「亡者麵包」，在家中布置彩色祭壇，擺上照片與亡者生前愛吃的食物；再到墓園整理墓地、點燭共餐，把墓園化為歡慶的場所。

🎉 面向一｜節日精神（A Joyful Spirit）
▶The Day of the Dead is a Mexican holiday on which people face the loss of loved ones with laughter instead of tears.
亡靈節是墨西哥的一個節日，在這一天，人們以歡笑而非淚水面對失去至親。
🔍 句構分析 ①The Day of the DeadS isV a Mexican holidayC on which people face the loss of loved ones with laughter instead of tears關係子句修飾 holiday
on which 引導關係子句修飾 holiday；instead of + N = 而不是…（見句型五）；face = 面對。
▶Rather than mourning quietly in black, families cheerfully invite their departed relatives home for a warm reunion.
家人並不默默地穿著黑衣哀悼，而是開心地邀請已故的親人回家團聚。
🔍 句構分析 ①Rather than mourning quietly in black介副片語（rather than + Ving） familiesS inviteV their departed relativesO home for a warm reunion副詞
rather than + Ving = 而非…（見句型三）；mourn（哀悼）、depart（過世）、reunion（團聚）為核心/補充字。
▶Throughout this joyful occasion, people pay tribute to relatives who have already crossed into the afterlife.
在這個歡樂的日子裡，人們向已跨入來世的親人致敬。
🔍 句構分析 ①Throughout this joyful occasion副詞 peopleS pay tribute toV（片語動詞） relativesO who have already crossed into the afterlife關係子句
occasion（場合）為核心字；pay tribute to = 向…致敬；who have crossed 現在完成式關係子句。
▶Have you ever pictured a beloved relative who has died coming back to join your holiday festivities?

▶To the people who keep this custom, death is not something horrible but a natural stage that awaits everyone.
對延續這項習俗的人而言，死亡並非可怕之物，而是人人終將面對的自然階段。
🔍 句構分析 ①To the people who keep this custom副詞（含關係子句） deathS isV not something horrible but a natural stageC（not A but B） that awaits everyone關係子句

▶It is the scent of the marigolds and the glow of the candles that are believed to guide the wandering souls back home.

It is ... that ... 為分裂句（強調句），強調「香氣與燭光」；scent（香氣）、glow（光芒）；guide sb. back = 引導某人回來。
▶Far from being afraid of the returning souls, families clean up the graves, set candles aglow, and share a feast beside the tombs until sunrise.
家人非但不怕返回的靈魂，反而清理墓地、點亮蠟燭，並在墓旁共享盛宴直到日出。
🔍 句構分析 ①Far from being afraid of the returning souls介副片語（Far from + Ving） familiesS clean upV the gravesO , setV candlesO aglowOC , and shareV a feastO
Far from + Ving = 一點也不…（與句型五 instead of + Ving 呼應）；tidy / restore 同義 clean up；feast（盛宴）為核心字。
▶In the end, they treat death not as something horrible but as a natural stage everyone must reach, choosing to welcome it rather than turn away.
最終，他們把死亡看成不是可怕的事，而是人人終將走到的自然階段，選擇歡迎它而非逃避。
🔍 句構分析 ①In the end副詞 theyS treatV deathO not as something horrible but as a natural stage [everyone must reach]補語（not...but...） , choosing to welcome it rather than turn away分詞構句
treat A as B = 把 A 視為 B；not as A but as B 對比；rather than + 原形（見句型三）；呼應 inevitable（不可避免）的概念。

句型一｜Have you ever + p.p. …?（你曾經…嗎？）▼
Have/Has + S + (ever) + 過去分詞 …?（現在完成式問「經驗」）

用現在完成式詢問「到目前為止是否有過某種經驗」，常與 ever 連用。

Have you ever imagined sharing a meal with a departed relative?
你曾經想像過和已故親人共餐嗎？（改寫自課文開頭）
Have you ever seen a colorful sugar skull?
你曾看過色彩繽紛的糖骷髏嗎？
✗ Did you ever imagined...（動詞時態錯；現在完成須用 have + p.p.，且 imagined 不接在 Did 後）。
回答常用 Yes, I have. / No, never.；學測克漏字常考現在完成表經驗。
句型二｜be thought/believed to (have) + V（被認為…）▼
S + is/are thought/believed + to V（同時） S + is/are thought/believed + to have + p.p.（更早發生）

表「一般認為…」；若動作比主句更早，to 後用完成式 have + p.p.。

The tradition is believed to have originated with the Aztecs.
這項傳統據信源自阿茲特克人。（改寫自課文）
The souls are thought to return to earth on these days.
據信靈魂會在這幾天回到人間。（改寫自課文）
✗ is believed originated...（漏掉 to have，時態不清）。
= It is believed that the tradition originated...；學測常考兩種句式互換。
句型三｜A rather than B（是 A 而非 B）▼
... A + rather than + B（A、B 詞性／結構須對等）

rather than 連接兩個對等成分，表「寧可是 A 而不是 B」。

Mexicans celebrate death with joy rather than mourn it with sorrow.
墨西哥人以歡樂慶祝死亡，而非以悲傷哀悼。（改寫自課文）
They see death as a beginning rather than an end.
他們把死亡看成開始，而非結束。
✗ celebrate death rather than mourning it（前為原形 celebrate，後宜對等用 mourn）。
rather than 置句首時，其後常接 Ving：Rather than mourning..., they celebrate...。
句型四｜would rather + V (than V)（寧願…也不…）▼
S + would rather + 原形V (+ than + 原形V)

表達偏好；would rather 後接原形動詞，than 後亦接原形。

Mexicans would rather embrace death than ignore it.
墨西哥人寧願擁抱死亡，也不願忽視它。（改寫自課文）
I would rather celebrate a life than mourn a loss.
我寧願慶祝一段人生，也不願哀悼一場失去。
✗ would rather to embrace / would rather embracing（than 後與 rather 後皆用原形）。
would rather + S + 過去式 表「希望對方…」（假設）；學測易與 prefer to V 混考。
句型五｜Instead of + N/Ving（而不是…）▼
Instead of + N / Ving, S + V …（介系詞 of 後接名詞或動名詞）

instead of 為介系詞片語，後面須接名詞或動名詞（V-ing），不接原形。

Instead of dreading the ghosts, Mexicans look forward to their visit.
墨西哥人非但不害怕鬼魂，還期待它們來訪。
They light candles instead of tears.
他們點起蠟燭，而不是流下眼淚。
✗ Instead of fear the spirits（of 後應接動名詞 fearing，不可用原形）。
instead（副詞，單獨用）≠ instead of（介系詞片語，後接受詞）；學測常考兩者辨析。
句型六｜keep + O + 補語（使…保持某狀態）▼
S + keep + O + adj. / Ving / p.p.（受詞補語）

keep 為不完全及物動詞，後接受詞與受詞補語，表「使受詞維持某狀態」。

Mexicans keep the old custom alive with great enthusiasm.
墨西哥人以極大的熱情讓古老的習俗延續不墜。（改寫自課文）
Bright candles keep the graveyard glowing all night long.
明亮的蠟燭讓墓園整夜閃著光。
✗ keep the custom alively（補語用形容詞 alive，非副詞）。
同類動詞 find / leave / make + O + OC；學測常考補語詞性（adj. vs adv.）。
句型七｜It is said/believed that …（據說；一般認為…）▼
It + is said / believed / thought + that + 子句 （＝ S + is said / believed / thought + to V）

用虛主詞 It 引導，that 子句為真主詞；亦可改寫成「主詞 + be said to V」。

It is said that the spirits return to their old homes on these two days.
據說在這兩天裡，靈魂會回到舊家。（改寫自課文）
The spirits are said to return to their old homes on these two days.
據說靈魂會回到舊家。（同義改寫）
✗ It is said the spirits return...（省略 that 雖可，但正式寫作宜保留；勿寫成 It says that...）。
兩種句式互換是學測改寫熱點：It is said that S+V ↔ S is said to V。
句型八｜分詞構句（Ving / p.p. 開頭，表附帶或原因）▼
Ving / (Being) p.p. …, S + V …（分詞與主句共用主詞）

將副詞子句省略連接詞與主詞、動詞改為分詞；主動用 Ving，被動用 p.p.。

Getting ready for the visit, families head to the markets beforehand.
為了迎接到訪，家人會提前前往市集。（改寫自課文）
Handed down for centuries, the custom is still practiced today.
這項習俗流傳數百年，至今仍被實行。
✗ Get ready for the visit, families head...（句首須用分詞 Getting，非原形）。
分詞的邏輯主詞須與主句主詞一致，否則成「垂懸分詞」，學測常設此陷阱。
