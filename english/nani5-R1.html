💬 Read and Fill In｜Tim 與 Mr. Cooper（the PE teacher）
▶Tim wants to join his school’s athletics team. He is talking with Mr. Cooper, the PE teacher.
Tim 想加入學校的田徑隊。他正在和體育老師 Mr. Cooper 談話。
🔍 句構分析 ①TimS wantsV to join his school’s athletics teamO
②HeS is talkingV with Mr. Cooper對象 the PE teacher同位語
athletics team 田徑隊。Mr. Cooper, the PE teacher：逗號後是同位語，說明他的身分。
▶Mr. CooperWhy do you want to join the team?
你為什麼想加入田徑隊？
🔍 句構分析 ①Why疑問詞 do助動詞 youS wantV to join the teamO
want to + V。
▶TimI love running. Besides, the team has finished first in different races many times.
我熱愛跑步。而且，這支隊伍已經在不同比賽中拿過很多次第一名。
🔍 句構分析 ①IS loveV runningO（動名詞）
②Besides此外 the teamS has finishedV（現在完成式） firstC in different races地點 many times次數
填空：has finished——many times（次數）＝經驗 → 現在完成式；the team 單數用 has。besides 此外。finish first 得第一。
▶Mr. CooperRunning is fun, but being a runner on this school’s athletics team is different.
跑步很好玩，但當這所學校田徑隊的跑者可就不一樣了。
🔍 句構分析 ①RunningS（動名詞） isV funC
②but連接詞 being a runner on this school’s athletics teamS（動名詞片語） isV differentC
動名詞當主詞，不管多長都用單數動詞 is。
▶Mr. CooperHave you ever trained before?
你以前受過訓練嗎？
🔍 句構分析 ①Have助動詞 youS ever曾經 trainedV（p.p.） before以前
Have you ever + p.p. … before? 問經驗。📊 段考就考這句。
▶TimYes, I have. I was on the athletics team when I was in the fifth grade.
有，我受過。我五年級的時候是田徑隊的一員。
🔍 句構分析 ①Yes, I have.簡答
②IS wasV（過去式） on the athletics team在隊上 when I was in the fifth grade過去時間子句
填空：have——Have you…? → Yes, I have.（✗ Yes, I did.）第二句有 when I was in the fifth grade（明確過去）→ 過去式 was。grade 年級。
▶Mr. CooperWhy only for one year? What happened?
為什麼只參加一年？發生了什麼事？
🔍 句構分析 ①Why only for one year?省略句
②WhatS happenedV（不及物）
happen 不及物，不能被動（✗ What was happened?）。
▶TimI fell down during the City Athletics Games, and my left leg was broken.
我在市運會的時候跌倒了，我的左腿骨折了。
🔍 句構分析 ①IS fell downV（過去式） during the City Athletics Games期間
②and連接詞 my left legS was brokenV（過去式被動）
填空：was broken——腿是「被弄斷」→ 被動；事情發生在過去 → was + p.p.（break–broke–broken）。fall–fell–fallen。
▶TimI was told to give up running.
我被告知要放棄跑步。
🔍 句構分析 ①IS was toldV（過去式被動） to give up runningO（不定詞）
填空：was told——「被告知」→ be told to V（tell–told–told）。give up + V-ing 放棄做……。
▶Mr. CooperI’m sorry to hear that.
聽到這件事我很遺憾。
🔍 句構分析 ①IS ’mV sorryC to hear that原因（不定詞）
I’m sorry to hear that. 表達同情的常用句。
▶TimLuckily, my leg got better last month, so I am able to run again.
幸運的是，我的腿上個月好了，所以我又能跑步了。
🔍 句構分析 ①Luckily幸運地 my legS gotV（連綴） betterC last month過去時間
②so連接詞 IS amV able to run againC
填空：am（課本提示 am / was / have been）——腿上個月好了，現在能跑 → am able to。last month → 過去式 got。be able to＝can。
▶Mr. CooperSounds great. You are in.
聽起來很棒。你入隊了。
🔍 句構分析 ①Sounds great.＝That sounds great.
②YouS areV inC（入選、加入）
You are in.＝你被錄取了／你加入了。
▶TimThank you very much. I won’t let you down.
非常謝謝您。我不會讓您失望的。
🔍 句構分析 ①ThankV youO very much非常
②IS won’t let downV youO（夾在中間）
let sb down 讓某人失望；代名詞要夾在 let 和 down 中間。

▶BrianWhat? A bird’s beak mask? Why? What’s it for?

What’s it for? 它是做什麼用的？
▶SylviaThey put flowers in their masks to hide the smell of the dead bodies around them to keep them from getting sick.
他們在面具裡放花，遮住周圍屍體的臭味，好讓自己不會生病。

②to hide the smell of the dead bodies around them目的
③to keep them from getting sick目的

▶BrianSounds cool. Can you show me a picture of a plague doctor?
聽起來很酷。你能給我看一張瘟疫醫生的照片嗎？
🔍 句構分析 ①Sounds cool.聽起來很酷
②Can助動詞 youS showV me間接受詞 a picture of a plague doctor直接受詞
show + 人 + 物。
▶BrianSo ... did the masks work?
所以……那些面具有用嗎？

work 這裡是「有效」。

②Even though the masks didn’t work讓步子句 the scary and strange image of the plague doctorsS can still be seenV（助動詞被動） in some movies, video games and even at Halloween parties地點

核心句型文法解析
👆 點擊每個句型標題，展開「公式 + 例句 + 常見錯誤 + 會考／段考重點」。
句型一｜R1 時態判斷：過去式 vs. 現在完成式（段考必考）▼
過去式：明確過去時間（yesterday, last…, …ago, in 2018, just now, when I was…）／When…? 現在完成式：到現在為止（since, for, ever, never, already, yet, so far, before, 次數）／How long…?

有明確的過去時間就用過去式；強調「從過去到現在」的經驗、持續或結果才用現在完成式。課文 Tim 的對話就是最好的對照。

過去式（課文）現在完成式（課文）
I was on the team when I was in the fifth grade.The team has finished first … many times.
My leg got better last month.Have you ever trained before?
Plague doctors treated sick people during the Black Death.Henry has played tennis since 2:00 p.m.
Jack grew vegetables a few years ago.
Jack 幾年前種過菜。（a few years ago → 過去式）
I went to a camp for five days last week.
我上週去參加了五天的營隊。（有 for 也有 last week → 過去式）
✗ I have gone to a camp last week. ✗ When have you trained?（When 問句用過去式）✗ The team finished first many times since 2020.（since → 完成式）
6 校 8 題。口訣：有過去時間點 → 過去式；沒有、但連到現在 → 現在完成式。看到 for 先別急，確認動作是否延續到現在。
句型二｜Grow Your Grammar：現在完成式（持續＋經驗）▼
S + have/has + p.p. + since + 時間點 S + have/has + p.p. + for + 一段時間 S + have/has + p.p. + (once / twice / three times)

同一件事可用 since 或 for 兩種說法；次數表經驗。

Henry has played tennis since 2:00 p.m.
Henry 從下午兩點就開始打網球。（課本）
Henry has played tennis for 2 hours.
Henry 已經打網球兩個小時了。（課本）
Henry has read this story (twice).
Henry 讀過這個故事（兩次）。（課本）
✗ since 2 hours ✗ for 2:00 p.m. ✗ Henry has readed（read 三態同形）
since 接時間點、for 接時間長度——6 校考 15 題，是第一次段考最大宗。
句型三｜Grow Your Grammar：被動語態（主動 → 被動）▼
主動：S + V + O 被動：O + be + p.p. (+ by S) 被動時態：am/is/are p.p.｜was/were p.p.｜will/can be p.p.｜have/has been p.p.

把主動的受詞移到句首當主詞，動詞改成 be + p.p.，原主詞變成 by + 受格。be 動詞要跟著原句時態與新主詞變化。

主動被動
Lucy led the blind man across the road.The blind man was led across the road by Lucy.（課本）
Someone broke my left leg.（概念）My left leg was broken.（課文）
People can still see the image.The image can still be seen.（課文）
I was told to give up running.
我被告知要放棄跑步。（課文）
The image of the plague doctors can still be seen in some movies.
瘟疫醫生的形象至今仍可在電影中看到。（課文）
✗ My leg broke by the car.（被動要用 be + p.p.）✗ can still seen ✗ was lead（lead 的 p.p. 是 led）
lead–led–led、tell–told–told、break–broke–broken 是 R1 填空必背三態。
句型四｜簡答與不能被動的動詞▼
Have/Has…? → Yes, S have/has. / No, S haven’t/hasn’t. Did…? → Yes, S did. / No, S didn’t. 不及物動詞 happen / appear / fall / die → 不能被動

看問句的助動詞決定簡答；happen、fall down 這類沒有受詞的動詞不能改被動。

Have you ever trained before? — Yes, I have.
你以前受過訓練嗎？——有。（課文）
What happened?
發生了什麼事？（課文；✗ What was happened?）
I fell down during the City Athletics Games.
我在市運會時跌倒了。（課文；✗ was fallen down）
✗ Have you trained? — Yes, I did. ✗ What was happened? ✗ I was fallen down.
段考：Peter’s been to the USA, but his sisters haven’t.（省略句也跟著完成式用 haven’t）
